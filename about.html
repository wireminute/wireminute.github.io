About WireMinute

WireMinute is an independent digital newsroom. Our promise is in our tagline: News, updated every minute.

We publish original reporting and live updates around the clock — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — for readers who want the news as it happens: clear, factual and in our own words, without noise or opinion dressed up as fact.

What we cover

Our coverage spans the stories shaping the day, organised into clear sections:

  • Latest — the newest developments as they break
  • World — major international events
  • Politics — governments, elections and policy
  • Business — markets, companies and the economy
  • Technology — the technology changing everyday life
  • Sports — results, stories and sporting drama
  • Health — medicine, wellbeing and public health

How our stories are produced

Every WireMinute story is written by our own newsroom, in our own words. We follow events as they develop, check the facts against multiple independent sources before we publish, and update a story as new confirmed details arrive — a developing story on WireMinute grows with the facts instead of waiting for tomorrow. The same events are covered by many news outlets around the world; what makes each report its own is the reporting and the writing, and ours is entirely our own.

Photographs on WireMinute are real photographs of the people and places in the story. We license them properly — public-domain and Creative Commons sources — and we credit the photographer wherever a licence asks us to.

Our editorial standards

  • Verified before published. Major claims are checked against at least two independent sources before a story goes live. If a fact is still unconfirmed, the story says so plainly.
  • Developing stories are labelled. When news is breaking, we publish what is confirmed, mark the story as developing, and update it in place as new facts are confirmed, so a story always reflects the latest verified picture.
  • Our own words. Every headline and article on WireMinute is written by WireMinute. We never copy another outlet's text or reporting; the same event may be covered around the world, but our account of it is our own work.
  • News, not opinion. We report what happened. We do not dress opinion up as fact, and where a claim comes from an interested party, we attribute it clearly to that party.
  • Independent. WireMinute is independently owned, and our coverage is not influenced by advertisers, sponsors or any outside interest.

Corrections

Accuracy matters to us as much as speed. If we get something wrong, we correct it as soon as we spot it or it is pointed out to us. If you see an error, please tell us through our Contact page — give us the headline and roughly when you saw it, and we will check it straight away.

Talk to us

News tips, corrections and feedback are always welcome. You can reach the newsroom any time through our Contact page.
