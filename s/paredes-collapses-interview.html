Paredes Collapses During Live TV Interview After Boca's Copa Sudamericana Win

Argentina midfielder Leandro Paredes collapsed during a live television interview on Saturday, minutes after captaining Boca Juniors to victory in the Copa Sudamericana final, and later told fans he was fine.

The 32-year-old went down mid-sentence while speaking to broadcaster DSports after Boca's 2-0 win over Atletico Mineiro in Barranquilla, Colombia. The interviewer tried to break his fall as medical staff rushed to help him, and Paredes was treated on the pitch before getting back to his feet, waving to the crowd and jogging to the dressing room. He had lifted the trophy only minutes earlier, after Boca ended an 18-year wait for an international title.

Paredes had already struggled during the match, which was played in sweltering heat, with temperatures around 32 degrees and humidity near 80 per cent. He felt dizzy late in the first half, recovered a little during the break, and then struggled again early in the second half before asking to be taken off just before the hour mark. His manager, Rodolfo Arruabarrena, said the midfielder had been honest about feeling unwell and had asked for the change himself.

Boca's goals came from Santiago Ascacibar in the first half and Miguel Merentiel shortly after the restart, sealing a 2-0 win in the final at the Estadio Metropolitano. Paredes, a World Cup winner with Argentina in 2022 who has also played for Paris Saint-Germain, Juventus and Roma, later reassured supporters on social media, saying the episode was just a dizzy spell and thanking everyone for their messages. He also shared a smiling photograph of himself in bed and was later filmed greeting his team-mates at a restaurant, giving a thumbs up as they cheered.

The club's medical staff put the episode down to a drop in blood pressure, according to ESPN, and Paredes had a precautionary check in hospital before being allowed home. Boca did not issue a formal medical update after the match. The incident drew a wide reaction online, with footage of the interviewer catching the falling midfielder shared across social media.
