DR Congo Rebels Call Off Advance on Bunia and Pull Back After Days of Fighting

A rebel group in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo has halted its advance on the city of Bunia and begun pulling back from territory it seized in recent days, after heavy fighting with government forces displaced thousands of civilians.

The Convention for the Popular Revolution, known as the CRP and led by Thomas Lubanga, said on Tuesday it had ordered its fighters to stop moving toward Bunia, the capital of Ituri province, and to withdraw from areas taken in the last few days. The group said it was acting in line with commitments made during talks with the Congolese government mediated by Uganda.

The announcement came a day after the rebels seized Iga-Barriere, a strategic locality about 25 kilometres north of Bunia, forcing government troops to fall back toward the provincial capital. Local media reported the group had also taken the localities of Lopa and Linji on Sunday after fierce fighting. Thousands of people have fled their homes toward Bunia since the weekend.

The fighting has disrupted the response to the Ebola outbreak in the region. The World Health Organization said on Sunday that clashes near a hospital north of Bunia forced health workers and aid partners to evacuate, interrupting work against what is the country's largest and deadliest Ebola outbreak.

Lubanga, a former militia leader convicted by the International Criminal Court in 2012 over the recruitment of child soldiers, announced the formation of the CRP in 2025 and opened a new front against President Felix Tshisekedi. He remains under United Nations sanctions. The group said its decision followed an October 9 meeting in Uganda between Lubanga and a delegation of senior Congolese government and military officials, hosted by President Yoweri Museveni. The Congolese military has not yet issued a detailed statement on the latest fighting.

Key points

  • The Convention for the Popular Revolution, led by Thomas Lubanga, says it has halted its advance on Bunia and will withdraw from areas seized in recent days
  • The rebels seized Iga-Barriere, about 25km north of Bunia, on Monday, forcing government troops to fall back toward the provincial capital
  • Thousands of civilians have fled toward Bunia since the weekend, and fighting near a hospital disrupted the Ebola response, the World Health Organization said
  • The group cited commitments from Uganda-mediated talks with the government; Lubanga was convicted by the International Criminal Court in 2012 over child soldiers
