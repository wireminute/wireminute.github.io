Blast and Fire at Bangkok Oil Refinery as Nearby Residents Told to Leave

A large explosion and fire at an oil refinery in eastern Bangkok sent thick black smoke across the city on Thursday afternoon, as officials urged people living nearby to leave the area at once.

The blast struck the Bangchak refinery on Sukhumvit Soi 62 in the Phra Khanong district. The district office said an explosion had occurred at the site and told residents in the surrounding area to evacuate immediately for their own safety. Flames rose high above the plant, and reporters some distance away felt the blast shake their doors. Students were moved quickly out of nearby schools.

The company said the fire was brought under control within the afternoon and later put out, and that production at the site had been halted. At least three people were taken to hospital with injuries, and no deaths were reported. The prime minister said the fire was in a smaller unit rather than the main production area, and the cause is under investigation.

Key points

  • An explosion and fire hit the Bangchak refinery in eastern Bangkok on Thursday afternoon, with thick smoke seen across the city.
  • The district office urged nearby residents to evacuate at once; at least three people were taken to hospital and no deaths were reported.
  • The fire was brought under control and later extinguished; production at the site was halted and the cause is under investigation.
