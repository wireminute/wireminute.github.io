Explosion Rocks Sanaa as Coalition Intercepts Houthi Missiles Aimed at Riyadh

An explosion rocked Yemen's capital, Sanaa, after air strikes hit areas around its international airport, as the Saudi-led coalition said its air defences had intercepted two ballistic missiles fired by the Houthis towards Riyadh.

An explosion rocked Yemen's capital, Sanaa, after air strikes targeted areas around Sanaa International Airport over the past 24 hours. The cause of the blast and any casualties were not yet known. The Houthis said the Saudi-led coalition had carried out 105 air strikes and missile attacks on civilian sites in the capital and 10 governorates in the same period.

The Saudi-led coalition said its air defences had intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched by the Houthis towards Riyadh, as well as a cruise missile in the western part of the country and a drone near Jeddah's port. Explosions were heard over Riyadh around midnight, and Saudi civil defence later said the danger had passed in Riyadh Province. The coalition said no civilian injuries resulted from debris of the intercepted drone.

Key points

  • An explosion rocked Sanaa after air strikes targeted areas around its international airport; the cause and any casualties are not yet known.
  • The Saudi-led coalition said its air defences intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched by the Houthis towards Riyadh.
  • The coalition also reported intercepting a cruise missile and a drone near Jeddah's port, and said no civilian injuries resulted from the intercepted drone's debris.
