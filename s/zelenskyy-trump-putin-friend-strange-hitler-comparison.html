Zelenskyy Says Calling Putin a Friend Is Like Calling Hitler a Friend

Volodymyr Zelenskyy has said it is strange for Donald Trump to call Vladimir Putin his friend, likening it to calling Adolf Hitler a friend during the Second World War.

Volodymyr Zelenskyy has said it is strange for Donald Trump to call Vladimir Putin his friend, comparing it to calling Adolf Hitler a friend during the Second World War. Speaking in English in a BBC interview at his office in Kyiv, the Ukrainian president said Putin was an aggressor and a killer who could not be a friend.

"Of course it's up to the President of the United States, but... it's strange," Zelenskyy said when asked about the friendship between the two leaders. "He is an aggressor, and a killer. He can't be a friend. Just cannot be." He added: "It is the same that somebody said about Hitler during the Second World War that he is a friend. No, he is not a good friend."

His remarks came about a week after Trump and Putin met at a summit in China, where the US president embraced the Russian leader as a friend. Days later, Trump posted a photograph on Truth Social showing the two men shaking hands, with the caption "My Friend". Trump later defended the description at a White House news conference, saying he had a very good relationship with the Russian leader.

Zelenskyy also used the interview to question Washington's support for Kyiv, saying delays in delivering Patriot missile systems were costing Ukrainian lives and that the United States was not pressing Moscow hard enough to end the war. He said American attention was drifting towards the Middle East.

He said peace talks were expected to continue in December, but it was not clear whether European negotiators would have a seat at the table, with the Kremlin appearing opposed to their presence. He also said Ukraine could now shoot down about 70 per cent of Russia's fastest drones, and that the share was rising. Asked about restrictions affecting interceptor drones, he described the situation as a game of ping-pong between SpaceX and political decision-makers.

Key points

  • Zelenskyy calls Trump's description of Putin as "my friend" strange, saying an aggressor and a killer cannot be a friend.
  • He likens calling Putin a friend to calling Hitler a friend during the Second World War.
  • His remarks follow Trump's embrace of Putin at a summit in China and a Truth Social photograph captioned "My Friend".
  • Zelenskyy also says Patriot delivery delays are costing Ukrainian lives and that US attention is drifting to the Middle East.
