Thomas Markle Indicted on Two Child Abuse Image Counts, Faces Up to 40 Years

A federal grand jury in Los Angeles has indicted Thomas Markle on two counts of possessing child sexual abuse material, doubling the charges against the 82-year-old father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, and exposing him to as much as 40 years in prison if convicted.

The indictment, returned on Tuesday, says Markle knowingly kept 35mm film negatives showing sexually explicit material involving children, including at least one image of a prepubescent child younger than 12. Each count carries a maximum of 20 years in federal prison. Prosecutors say the material was found in two Los Angeles storage units Markle has rented since 2008, where federal agents seized about 78 items, including slides, film negatives and books. Court papers say two of the images show a man a witness identified as Markle with a nude prepubescent girl, and a third shows a nude prepubescent girl in a room that appears to be part of a motel chain in the Philippines.

Markle was first charged in a criminal complaint on November 5 with a single count, and federal agents arrested him the next day at a senior living facility in Florida, where he remains in custody. He is expected to be moved to Los Angeles in the coming weeks to be formally charged on the new indictment in a federal court there. A judge in Florida ruled last week that he is mentally fit to stand trial, after a psychiatrist said he understood the charges against him, despite a dementia diagnosis earlier this year.

The case also reaches the Philippines, where Markle lived for months in Cebu before returning to the United States this year. Police there have lodged three complaints with prosecutors, two covering the alleged distribution of such material and one covering possession, and prosecutors are deciding whether to take the case to court. Investigators say the inquiry began after Markle's son reported finding suspected illegal images among belongings left at his father's home in Cebu and handed over electronic devices. They say about 7,500 photographs and one video have been recovered from devices linked to Markle, with forensic checks on the remaining devices still under way.

Markle has denied the allegations, calling them untrue. Prosecutors stress that he is presumed innocent until proven guilty and that the investigation is continuing.

Key points

  • A Los Angeles grand jury indicted Thomas Markle, 82, on two counts of possessing child sexual abuse material; each count carries up to 20 years.
  • Prosecutors say the material includes 35mm negatives of children, one under 12, found in two Los Angeles storage units he has rented since 2008.
  • Markle was arrested on November 6 in Florida and remains in custody; he will be moved to Los Angeles to face the new charges in the coming weeks.
  • Philippine police have lodged three related complaints in Cebu; Markle denies the allegations and is presumed innocent until proven guilty.
