Anthropic prices shares at $83 in record $81.3bn stock market debut

Anthropic has priced its shares at $83 each in a stock market debut that raised $81.3 billion, the biggest initial public offering ever recorded, with trading due to begin on Thursday.

The company behind the Claude chatbot said late on Wednesday that it and its seven co-founders had sold 980 million shares at $83 each, above the $70 to $78 range it had set out in its filings last week. The offering was also enlarged from the 825 million shares first marketed, after demand from investors far exceeded the stock on offer.

At the sale price, Anthropic is valued at roughly $1.5 trillion, which would place it among the twelve largest listed companies in the world, just behind the electric car maker Tesla. The previous record for a stock market debut belonged to SpaceX, whose June listing raised $75 billion.

Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley led the sale, working with more than twenty other banks. The shares are scheduled to start trading on Thursday on the Nasdaq exchange in New York under the ticker ANT, and the offering is expected to close on Friday.

Alongside the public sale, large technology investors including Google and Nvidia agreed to buy $26 billion of non-voting stock, taking the total amount raised to $107.3 billion. Google already owns a large stake in Anthropic, and Amazon's holding of about 1.92 billion shares would be worth roughly $159 billion at the offer price.

The listing makes Anthropic the first leading developer of frontier artificial intelligence models to sell shares to the public, ahead of its main rival OpenAI, which is not expected to seek a listing before 2027. The seven founders, who left OpenAI in 2021 to build Anthropic around Claude, each hold roughly 240 million to 244 million shares, worth about $20 billion apiece at the sale price. Between them they sold 23.6 million shares in the offering, while keeping nearly all of their holdings.

Key points

  • Anthropic priced its shares at $83 each, above the $70 to $78 range, selling 980 million shares and raising $81.3 billion.
  • The sale is the biggest stock market debut on record, passing the $75 billion raised by SpaceX in its June listing.
  • At roughly $1.5 trillion, the valuation would make Anthropic one of the twelve largest listed companies in the world.
  • Trading begins on Thursday on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, alongside a separate $26 billion private placement from large technology investors.
