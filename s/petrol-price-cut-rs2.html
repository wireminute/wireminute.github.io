Petrol Cut by Rs2 to Rs330 a Litre in Ninth Straight Reduction; Diesel Unchanged at Rs360

Petrol will sell at Rs330 a litre from Saturday after the government cut its price by Rs2 for the ninth time in a row, while high-speed diesel stays unchanged at Rs360.

The new rates were announced on Friday and will run from Saturday, November 14, to Monday, November 16. Petrol had been selling at Rs332 a litre before the cut. The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority issued the notification, and the regulator said the rates were set under the government's approved pricing formula. It is the ninth reduction in a row.

The latest cut follows a reduction of Rs3 a litre in petrol and Rs4 in diesel only a day earlier, on Thursday. The quick succession of changes reflects a new system of daily price reviews, under which the regulator now publishes fuel prices every day so that movements in international oil prices reach motorists faster.

Even after the cut, the tax burden stays heavy: Rs114 of every litre of petrol sold goes to the state in taxes and duties, and Rs100 of every litre of diesel. Under an arrangement announced on November 2, oil refineries will absorb a cumulative Rs15 billion of the petroleum levy over 30 days.

The government also launched a targeted fuel relief scheme in September, aimed at cushioning households from fuel costs pushed up by conflict in the Middle East. With prices now reviewed daily, the rates could move again as soon as the current window ends on Monday — and further small moves in either direction are possible as global oil prices shift.

The regulator has also begun publishing the daily prices on its website, saying the aim is to pass changes in world oil prices through to consumers more quickly. For motorists, the practical effect is a shorter wait between a fall in global prices and relief at the pump — and a faster pass-through when prices rise.

Key points

  • Petrol falls by Rs2 to Rs330 a litre from Saturday, November 14; high-speed diesel stays at Rs360.
  • The new rates run for three days, until Monday, November 16 — the ninth consecutive cut in petrol prices.
  • A Rs3 cut in petrol and a Rs4 cut in diesel on Thursday came just a day earlier, under a new daily price review system.
  • Taxes and duties stay heavy — Rs114 a litre on petrol and Rs100 on diesel — while refineries absorb Rs15 billion of the levy over 30 days.
