FIFA Confirms US-Led Bid for 2031 Women's World Cup and UK for 2035

The United States, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica will host the 2031 Women's World Cup, and England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland will stage the 2035 edition, after world football's governing body confirmed both hosts on Monday.

The decision was taken at an online meeting of FIFA's Congress in Zurich, where the 211 member federations ratified the two bids. Neither faced a rival, so the vote was a formality, but it settles the hosting picture for the next decade and gives both tournaments to the sport's biggest markets. The two editions will be the first Women's World Cups with 48 teams and 104 matches, bringing the women's tournament into line with the men's.

The 2031 tournament will be played across 20 cities, with 14 in the United States, four in Mexico, plus San Jose in Costa Rica and Kingston in Jamaica. The United States has hosted the Women's World Cup before, on its own in 1999 and 2003, and will now lead a four-nation project across the CONCACAF region.

The 2035 tournament will be the first World Cup of any kind staged in the United Kingdom since England hosted and won the men's tournament in 1966. Wembley Stadium in London is among the venues, and the four home nations will run the event together under their 'All Together' bid, which the four associations describe as the biggest single-sport occasion ever held in Britain. The tournament will run for 39 days in the summer of 2035, with the final list of host cities and stadiums to be confirmed later.

The double award gives both sets of hosts years to prepare, and it hands the women's game its biggest stage yet: a 48-team tournament on the same scale as the men's World Cup for the first time. The focus now turns to the host cities, with the full list of stadiums to be settled in the months ahead.

Key points

  • FIFA's Congress confirmed the United States, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica as hosts of the 2031 Women's World Cup.
  • England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland will jointly host the 2035 edition, the first World Cup in the UK since 1966.
  • Both tournaments will feature 48 teams and 104 matches, matching the men's World Cup.
  • The 2031 tournament will use 20 cities across the four host nations, and the 2035 final list of stadiums is still to be confirmed.
