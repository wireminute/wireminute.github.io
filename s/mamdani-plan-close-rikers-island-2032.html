Mamdani Unveils Plan to Close Rikers Island by 2032, Five Years Past Legal Deadline

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has unveiled his administration's first comprehensive plan to close the Rikers Island jail complex by 2032, five years after the legal deadline, replacing it with four smaller borough-based jails.

Mamdani released the plan, called Close Rikers: Strategic Plan, at a news conference at City Hall on Friday. It sets out the steps the city will take to shut the jail complex, which has long been criticised for violence, overcrowding and poor sanitation, and to move detainees into smaller jails closer to courthouses and their communities.

Under the plan, the first replacement jail will open in Brooklyn in 2029, followed by facilities in Queens and the Bronx in 2031 and in Manhattan in 2032, each with 1,040 beds. The city will also create 236 additional therapeutic housing units for people with serious mental health conditions and complex medical needs, bringing the total to 340 beds across the city's public hospitals.

The plan depends on cutting the jail population. Rikers currently holds about 6,600 people, and the administration aims to reduce that to 5,500 by the time the Brooklyn jail opens and to 4,100 by 2032, by expanding alternatives to detention, restoring online bail payments, cutting court delays and investing in housing and social services. It also commits $146 million next fiscal year to mental health care, substance use treatment, education, job training and services for victims of crime.

The timeline is the plan's most contentious part. The City Council ordered Rikers to close by August 2027, and the new 2032 date will need councillors to pass legislation extending the deadline. Mamdani blamed delays under his predecessor's administration and the pandemic for missing the original date, saying: "It is time to finally close Rikers Island." The corrections officers' union, which has long pushed to keep the jail open, and residents near the planned borough jails are expected to oppose the plan.

Key points

  • New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has unveiled a plan to close the Rikers Island jail complex by 2032, five years after the legal deadline of August 2027.
  • Four borough-based jails will replace Rikers, opening in Brooklyn in 2029, Queens and the Bronx in 2031, and Manhattan in 2032.
  • The jail population is to fall from about 6,600 to 4,100 by 2032, with $146 million invested in mental health care, treatment, education and victim services.
  • The City Council must pass legislation to extend the deadline, and the plan is expected to face opposition from the corrections officers' union and local residents.
