Crimean Bridge Attacked for Third Time in a Week as Rail Traffic Halts

The bridge linking Russia with Crimea has been attacked for a third time in a week, Russian officials said on Thursday, halting train services across the crossing on the day President Vladimir Putin was scheduled to sit down with Donald Trump and Xi Jinping in Shenzhen.

Sergei Aksyonov, the Russian-installed head of Crimea, confirmed the latest attack in a post on social media, saying traffic across the bridge had been suspended while the damage was assessed. Russia's transport ministry said train services had been halted after an overnight drone attack, adding that nobody had been hurt and specialists were examining the bridge.

Ukraine's General Staff hinted in the morning that the bridge had been struck, without giving details. Kyiv has not formally claimed the overnight attack. The crossing, a 19-kilometre road and rail link opened in 2018, is one of President Putin's flagship projects and a vital logistics link for Russian troops operating in southern Ukraine.

The latest strike follows two far heavier attacks earlier in the week. On Monday, drones hit the bridge in what Moscow described as the largest drone barrage of the war, with its defence ministry claiming 1,336 Ukrainian drones were shot down. The Kremlin said that attack caused only minor damage.

A day later, a far heavier strike knocked out the middle of the bridge's roadway, sending its main arch into the sea, according to verified images and video of the damage. Ukraine's security service said one of the bridge's metal arches and the road span beneath it had been completely destroyed. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed the attack, describing it as the final stage of an operation planned over several weeks, and said it was a response to Russian attacks on Ukraine's infrastructure and bridges.

Aksyonov said two people had been killed in the attack on Crimea on Tuesday. Russia's defence ministry claimed 1,441 drones were shot down that night, a second record in two days, though neither figure has been independently verified. The bridge attacks come as Putin prepares to meet Trump and Xi in Shenzhen on Thursday for the first three-way summit of the leaders.

  • Tuesday's strike knocked out the middle of the bridge's roadway and sent its main arch into the sea; Ukraine's security service said the arch was completely destroyed.
