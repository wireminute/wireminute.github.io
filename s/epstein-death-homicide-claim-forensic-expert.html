Forensic Expert Says Epstein Was Murdered, Not a Suicide in 2019

A forensic expert who spent years studying Jeffrey Epstein's autopsy records says the financier was strangled in his New York jail cell in 2019, a finding that challenges the official ruling that he took his own life.

Michael Freeman, who edits the Journal of Forensic and Legal Medicine, said in a CBS News interview that the injuries in the autopsy record did not fit a death by hanging. In his view they pointed to strangulation from behind with a narrow knotted fabric ligature, later used to hang the body from a bunk bed. Epstein was discovered dead in a Manhattan jail cell on 10 August 2019, and the autopsy ruled the death a suicide.

The claim revives long-running questions about the death. The cameras watching the corridor outside his cell had failed that day, and a 2023 Justice Department report described longstanding faults in the jail's camera system. His brother Mark wants the case reopened, while the city's medical examiner's office says it stands by the suicide finding.

  • The city's medical examiner stands by its finding, while Epstein's brother wants the case reopened.
