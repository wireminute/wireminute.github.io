Trump Warns of Fresh Iran Strikes After New Year if Talks Fail

President Donald Trump has said he is inclined to order major new strikes against Iran after the new year if negotiations fail to produce an agreement, accusing Iranian negotiators of stalling while stepping up attacks since the United States midterm elections.

In an interview with Fox News on Sunday, Trump said he was inclined to resume major military action after January 1 if no deal is reached. He described Iranian negotiators as professional tap dancers and said they talk constantly without taking concrete steps towards an agreement.

Trump said Iran had grown more aggressive since the midterm elections held on November 3, and predicted that tit-for-tat strikes between American and Iranian forces would continue. He said the United States was in no hurry to decide while talks continued through mediators.

Trump also dared Tehran to test the American naval blockade of Iranian ports, saying any Iranian vessels that challenged it would end up at the bottom of the sea. Maritime security data showed 31 attacks in and around the Strait of Hormuz in October and about 19 more through November 18, the highest monthly levels since the war with Iran began in February.

The president's remarks came a day after he told Fox News he intends to secure a permanent agreement with Iran that would stop it ever obtaining a nuclear weapon and bind future presidents. Iran's foreign minister said Tehran wants a dignified agreement that ensures no further war, but will keep its enriched uranium inside the country.

Israel's prime minister has told security officials a new operation against Iran is unavoidable, according to Israeli broadcaster Channel 13, with joint plans said to be approved by the Israeli military and United States Central Command while Trump weighs whether to give the go-ahead. The head of Central Command, Admiral Brad Cooper, concluded a week-long visit to the Middle East on Sunday after meeting senior officials in Israel, Qatar and Saudi Arabia.

Key points

  • Trump told Fox News he is inclined to order major strikes against Iran after the new year if negotiations fail, calling Iranian negotiators professional tap dancers.
  • He said Iran has grown more aggressive since the November 3 midterm elections, predicted tit-for-tat strikes would continue, and dared Tehran to test the US naval blockade.
  • Trump also said on Saturday he intends to secure a permanent Iran deal that no future president can undo; Tehran says it wants a dignified deal but will keep its enriched uranium.
  • Netanyahu has told security officials a new operation is unavoidable, with joint plans said to be approved by the Israeli military and US Central Command as Trump weighs the decision.
