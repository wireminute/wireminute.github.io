Saudi Defences Down Two Missiles and Three Drones as Houthi Attacks Widen

Saudi air defences intercepted two ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh and shot down three drones over the kingdom's Eastern Province on Sunday morning, as Bahrain told its residents to move to the nearest safe place amid a widening threat across the Gulf.

The coalition backing Yemen's government said its forces destroyed the two missiles heading for the capital and three drones launched towards the east of the country within three hours. Saudi civil defence sounded an alert in Riyadh shortly after 4am warning of an incoming threat, then said the danger had passed. There was no immediate word of damage or casualties.

Bahrain's interior ministry issued an alert of its own the same morning, urging citizens and residents to stay calm and move to the nearest safe location, and warning sirens were reported across the kingdom. The ministry gave no details of a specific threat.

The strikes followed a day of warnings and interceptions on Saturday, when the coalition said it had shot down two drones launched towards Riyadh and the kingdom's southern regions. The United States mission in Saudi Arabia issued a nationwide security alert the same day, urging Americans across the Middle East to exercise heightened vigilance and warning of an increased threat of armed attacks by the Houthis and of Iranian efforts to target American officials in the region.

The State Department ordered non-emergency government employees and their families to leave Saudi Arabia on 23 October, and American officials now need special permission to use any airport in the country. The Houthis have launched repeated strikes on Saudi targets in recent weeks, including airports, energy infrastructure and Riyadh's financial district, as fighting between the United States, Israel and Iran grinds on. Iran's navy said two tankers hit naval mines and caught fire in the eastern Strait of Hormuz, the seventh such incident in a day.

Key points

  • Saudi defences destroyed two ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh and three drones over the Eastern Province within three hours on Sunday morning.
  • A civil defence alert sounded in Riyadh shortly after 4am; Bahrain issued its own alert and told residents to move to the nearest safe place.
  • The attacks followed Saturday's interception of two drones and a US security alert urging Americans across the Middle East to stay vigilant.
  • The Houthis have stepped up strikes on Saudi airports, energy sites and Riyadh's financial district as the wider war grinds on.
