Drones Strike Police Base in Colombian Border City of Cúcuta, Wounding Three Civilians

Explosive-laden drones struck a police base in the Colombian border city of Cúcuta early on Monday, wounding three civilians and damaging more than 60 homes, authorities said.

Explosive-laden drones struck a police base in the Colombian border city of Cúcuta early on Monday, wounding three civilians and damaging more than 60 homes, authorities said. The drones hit the Norte de Santander police command in the Tasajero neighbourhood at about 2.15am, and parts of the area lost electricity and gas. The injured were a couple and their 18-month-old baby, police said.

No group has claimed responsibility and no authority has named those responsible, although the ELN guerrilla group is active in the region and has repeatedly attacked with drones in nearby Catatumbo. A police station in Curumaní was also attacked with drones on Monday, and on Saturday an attack on a military base in La Jagua de Ibirico killed one soldier and wounded 12. It was the first time a major Colombian city has been hit from the air by guerrilla explosives. The story is still developing.

Key points

  • Explosive-laden drones struck the Norte de Santander police command in Cúcuta early on Monday, wounding three civilians, including an 18-month-old baby, and damaging more than 60 homes.
  • No group has claimed the attack and no authority has named those responsible; the ELN guerrilla group is active in the region and has carried out repeated drone attacks nearby.
  • A police station in Curumaní was also hit with drones on Monday, days after a base attack in La Jagua de Ibirico killed one soldier and wounded 12 others.
