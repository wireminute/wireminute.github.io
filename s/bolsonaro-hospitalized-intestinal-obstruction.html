Bolsonaro Hospitalised in Brasilia With Near-Complete Intestinal Obstruction

Former Brazilian president Jair Bolsonaro has been taken to hospital in Brasilia with a near-complete intestinal obstruction after suffering severe abdominal pain, his doctor and his son have said.

Bolsonaro, 71, was taken to hospital on Friday after severe abdominal pain and swelling, his son Carlos said. Tests showed a partial but near-complete blockage of the intestine, his doctor, Brasil Caiado, told reporters. He is receiving pain medication while doctors try to reverse the condition without an invasive procedure.

The former president, who governed Brazil from 2019 to 2022, has been treated repeatedly for abdominal problems since he was stabbed during a campaign event in 2018, undergoing more than nine operations since. He is serving a 27-year prison sentence for plotting a coup after losing the 2022 election and is under humanitarian house arrest on medical grounds. His doctor said he is clinically stable, conscious and calm, and will remain under observation for the next 24 hours.
