Zardari Calls on PM Shehbaz in Lahore as Leaders Review Politics, Economy and Security

President Asif Ali Zardari met Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif in Lahore on Saturday for detailed talks on the country's politics, economy and security, with the prime minister updating him on his recent trip to Turkiye for the UN climate conference.

President Asif Ali Zardari travelled to the prime minister's residence in Lahore's Model Town on Saturday, where he and Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif held wide-ranging talks on politics, the economy and national security, the Prime Minister's Office said.

The prime minister updated the president about his recent trip to Turkiye, where he attended the 31st Conference of the Parties and set out Pakistan's efforts on climate action. The two leaders also discussed cooperation among the allied parties in the ruling coalition, keeping consultations going, and progress on public welfare projects.

Both leaders restated their resolve to work together to speed up economic stability and development in the country, the Prime Minister's Office said. National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq and former prime minister Raja Pervez Ashraf were among those present, along with Senator Saleem Mandviwalla and Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar. The prime minister's adviser Rana Sanaullah and MNA Hamza Shehbaz Sharif also attended.

The meeting came two days after Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi called on the president in Nawabshah. After reports suggested the two had discussed creating new provinces, the President House said no such discussion had taken place and that the talks had covered national security and the regional situation. The latest meeting reflects the continuing coordination between the two parties that anchor the coalition government.

The prime minister warmly welcomed the president when he arrived at the Model Town residence, the Prime Minister's Office said. The two leaders have met regularly since the coalition government took office, with the presidency and the prime minister's office coordinating on legislation and major policy decisions.

Key points

  • President Zardari met Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif at his Model Town residence in Lahore on Saturday for detailed talks on politics, the economy and security.
  • Shehbaz updated the president on his recent trip to Turkiye, where he attended the 31st UN climate conference and set out Pakistan's climate action efforts.
  • The leaders discussed cooperation among allied parties, continued consultations and progress on public welfare projects, and restated their commitment to work together on economic stability and development.
  • The meeting followed Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's call on the president two days earlier; the President House has said no discussion of new provinces took place at that meeting.
