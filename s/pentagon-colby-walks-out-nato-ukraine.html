Pentagon Strategist Walked Out of NATO Meeting After Rebuking Ukraine Over Deep Strikes

The Pentagon's top policy official left a meeting with European allies in Brussels early after criticising Ukraine for its long-range strikes inside Russia, according to a report that has alarmed several allied governments.

Elbridge Colby, the under secretary of defence for policy, left Tuesday's gathering of the Ukraine Defence Contact Group at NATO headquarters before it ended, shortly after telling allies that Kyiv's strikes deep inside Russia served little military purpose, according to the report.

Elbridge Colby, the under secretary of defence for policy, told Tuesday's meeting of the Ukraine Defence Contact Group at NATO headquarters that Kyiv's long-range attacks had little military value. He said they had not helped bring the war closer to an end or slow Russia's advance on the front line, according to people present.

Colby then left the meeting early, before Ukraine's defence minister gave a classified briefing on the war. The United States was represented for the rest of the session by Matthew Whitaker, its ambassador to NATO, another person familiar with the meeting said. One account said Colby had to leave to catch a flight.

German officials who were in the room said they were troubled by the episode. German sources said they worried the Trump administration had in effect taken Russia's side against Ukraine and its European backers. Germany's defence minister, Boris Pistorius, pushed back against Colby's remarks during the meeting, according to reports of the exchange.

The rebuke is striking because Ukraine's deep strikes have been one of Kyiv's main ways of hitting Russian fuel, weapons and drone production far behind the front line, including the repeated attacks this week on the bridge linking Russia with Crimea. German officials also noted that the United States has itself carried out deep strikes, including in Iran. The Pentagon has not commented publicly on the report.

Key points

  • Elbridge Colby, the Pentagon's policy chief, rebuked Ukraine at Tuesday's defence contact group meeting in Brussels, saying its deep strikes inside Russia had little military value.
  • He left the meeting early, before a classified briefing from Ukraine's defence minister, and the US ambassador to NATO took his place.
  • German officials said they were concerned the Trump administration had in effect sided with Russia; Germany's defence minister pushed back at the meeting.
  • The Pentagon has not commented publicly on the report, and one account said Colby left to catch a flight.
