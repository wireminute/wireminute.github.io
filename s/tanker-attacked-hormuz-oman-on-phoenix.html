Tanker Hit Twice in Strait of Hormuz as One Seafarer Goes Missing

A tanker first struck in the Strait of Hormuz on Monday was hit again on Tuesday, forcing its crew to abandon ship, and one Indian seafarer is missing, maritime officials and India’s shipping minister said.

The vessel, the Panama-flagged liquefied petroleum gas carrier Tara Gas, was first struck on Monday evening about seven nautical miles north of Khasab, on Oman’s Musandam Peninsula. The impact disabled the ship and set off a fire in its engine room. Britain’s maritime trade office first reported the crew safe, then said in a Tuesday update that the tanker had been hit a second time, caught fire again, and that its crew were unaccounted for.

India’s shipping minister said the second attack forced the entire 25-member crew to abandon the vessel. Twenty-four crew members, including 24 Indian nationals, have been accounted for, while one Indian seafarer remains missing. He said officials were working with the ship’s management company and rescue teams in the region to find the missing seafarer and support the rescued crew. The maritime office did not name the vessel or say who was behind the attacks.

The Tara Gas was the second tanker reported hit near Oman on Monday. Hours earlier, a separate warning said another tanker had been struck by an unknown projectile about three nautical miles off the Omani coast while sailing out of the strait, also suffering an engine-room fire. Maritime monitors named that vessel as the Panama-flagged On Phoenix, and said all of its crew were accounted for with no casualties.

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said six tankers had now been hit in the strait since Saturday, including two they said struck naval mines, a count no outside source has independently verified. Iran’s navy said the Tara Gas had been moving through a route Tehran considers unauthorised. The strait is one of the world’s most important shipping lanes for oil and gas, and Britain’s maritime office has advised ships in the area to transit with caution and report anything suspicious.

Key points

  • The Panama-flagged LPG carrier Tara Gas was hit twice near Oman, first on Monday evening and again on Tuesday, catching fire both times.
  • The crew abandoned ship after the second strike; 24 of the 25 crew are accounted for and one Indian seafarer is missing.
  • A separate tanker, the On Phoenix, was struck hours earlier on Monday about three nautical miles off Oman; all of its crew are safe.
  • Iran says six tankers have now been hit in the strait since Saturday, a count no outside source has independently verified.
