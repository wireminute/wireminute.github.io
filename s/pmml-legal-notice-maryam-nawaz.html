PMML Serves Legal Notice on Maryam Nawaz Over 'Terrorist' Remarks

Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz has been served a legal notice by the Pakistan Markazi Muslim League, which demands she withdraw remarks describing the party as 'terrorist', 'illegal' and 'banned', and apologise within seven days.

The notice was sent on Friday on the instructions of the party's president, Khalid Masood Sandhu, through lawyers from a Lahore firm. It says the chief minister made the remarks in a post on X and during a public speech, and asks her to delete the posts and all related material from social media and other platforms under her control. If the demands are not met within seven days of receipt, the party says it may take the matter to court.

The party says it is registered with the Election Commission of Pakistan and has taken part in elections under the country's constitution and laws since 2022. It contested last year's general election with the chair as its electoral symbol, and says it finished as the second-largest party in the NA-77 Gujranwala by-election held on 8 November.

The row follows that by-election, in which the ruling PML-N retained the seat. Its candidate, Rashid Bashir Virk, won with about 43,000 votes, ahead of the PMML's Muhammad Ibrahim Gujjar on about 30,000. The PMML has rejected the result, alleging rigging, and has asked the election commission to verify the polling records and order a recount. The chief minister and her party have in turn questioned how a party they link to proscribed organisations can take part in elections.

The PMML is widely described as the political front of Jamaat-ud-Dawa, the organisation founded by Hafiz Saeed, who has been in jail in Lahore since 2019 after convictions in terror-financing cases. The party denies any links with Jamaat-ud-Dawa or with proscribed organisations.

Key points

  • The Pakistan Markazi Muslim League has served a legal notice on Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz over remarks calling the party 'terrorist', 'illegal' and 'banned'.
  • The notice demands an unconditional apology and the removal of the posts within seven days, warning of legal action if the demands are not met.
  • The party says it is registered with the Election Commission and has contested elections since 2022, including finishing second in the NA-77 Gujranwala by-election.
  • The PMML is widely described as the political front of Jamaat-ud-Dawa, founded by Hafiz Saeed; the party denies any links with it or with proscribed groups.
