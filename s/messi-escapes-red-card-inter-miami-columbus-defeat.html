Messi Escapes Red Card After Throwing Ball at Opponent as Inter Miami Crash 3-0 to Columbus

Lionel Messi escaped a red card after throwing the ball into an opponent's face as Inter Miami crashed to a 3-0 defeat against Columbus Crew in the opening match of their MLS Cup playoff series on Saturday.

The flashpoint came on the stroke of half-time at Nu Stadium. With Miami already trailing 1-0 and down to 10 men, Messi went to take a throw-in and found Columbus forward Gonzalo Tapia standing close to block a quick restart. Messi first pushed the ball into Tapia's face, then hurled the throw-in at his head from point-blank range, sending the Chilean striker to the ground. Referee Allen Chapman showed yellow cards to both players, a decision Columbus head coach Laurent Courtois called far too lenient, saying afterwards that everyone knew Messi deserved more than a yellow.

Chapman later defended the call, explaining that the force used was negligible and the incident did not reach the level of violent conduct. The ruling kept Messi on the pitch, but it did little for Miami. Tapia had already put Columbus ahead from the penalty spot in the ninth minute, and the hosts' night had worsened in the 36th minute when defender Gonzalo Lujan was sent off for a second yellow card after a reckless challenge on Stephen Moreira.

Columbus made their extra man count after the break. Taha Habroune doubled the lead in the 52nd minute, and Tapia completed his brace six minutes later to seal a 3-0 win in front of a shell-shocked home crowd. The defeat came shortly after Messi had collected the 2026 MLS Golden Boot, and it leaves the defending champions facing an early exit from the playoffs they won last season.

Miami coach Kily Gonzalez admitted nothing had gone right for his side, saying they had effectively created Columbus's chances themselves and would have to learn from the night. The series now moves to Ohio for game two on Friday, when seventh seeds Columbus, the 2023 champions, can eliminate Messi and Miami with another victory.

Key points

  • Messi was shown only a yellow card after throwing the ball into Gonzalo Tapia's face at a throw-in on the stroke of half-time.
  • Referee Allen Chapman defended the decision, saying the force was negligible and the incident did not amount to violent conduct.
  • Columbus won 3-0 at Nu Stadium, with Tapia scoring twice and Taha Habroune adding the second goal.
  • Columbus can eliminate the defending MLS champions with a win in game two of the best-of-three series in Ohio on Friday.
