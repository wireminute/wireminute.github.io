Israel Ends One-Day Tank Raid Across Gaza Ceasefire Line as Main Road Reopens

Israel's military says it has finished a one-day tank operation in central Gaza, the most serious ground incursion across the ceasefire line since the truce that halted major fighting more than a year ago.

Israel's military said on Thursday it had completed a ground operation in central Gaza, a day after tanks crossed the ceasefire line in the most serious ground incursion since the truce that halted major fighting more than a year ago. "The operation is completed," the military said, after troops moved into Hamas-controlled territory west of the so-called Yellow Line near the Al-Bureij and Nuseirat refugee camps.

The operation began on Wednesday, when the military said troops and intelligence personnel had started a targeted activity to dismantle a tunnel it described as an immediate and direct threat to its soldiers. It was the first time since the October 2025 ceasefire that Israeli forces had publicly announced a raid deep beyond the boundary dividing areas under Israeli military control from those under Hamas control.

The defence ministry body that oversees civilian affairs in the Palestinian territories said residents could again move freely in the targeted areas, and that the Salah al-Din road, Gaza's main north-south highway, had reopened to traffic. Palestinian families had fled the area a day earlier as tanks rolled down the road.

The Yellow Line was drawn under the ceasefire agreed in October 2025, which halted more than two years of war and left Israeli forces in control of part of the territory while Hamas retained the rest. The raid tested that arrangement, and it drew attention precisely because it was the first publicly announced ground push across the line since the fighting stopped.

The military has not given a full account of the operation or said whether the tunnel was destroyed. It confirmed the raid had ended, but had not issued a formal statement on its conclusion by Thursday afternoon.

Key points

  • Israel's military says a one-day tank operation in central Gaza is complete, a day after it began.
  • The raid targeted a tunnel west of the Yellow Line, the boundary between Israeli- and Hamas-controlled areas under the October 2025 ceasefire.
  • It was the highest-profile ground incursion across the line since the truce halted major fighting more than a year ago.
  • The Salah al-Din road has reopened and residents can move freely again in the areas near Al-Bureij and Nuseirat, the military's civilian affairs body said.
