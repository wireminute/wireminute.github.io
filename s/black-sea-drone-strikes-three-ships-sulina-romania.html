Drones Strike Three Ships Off Romania's Black Sea Coast, Jets Scrambled Twice

Three commercial vessels have been hit by drones off Romania's Black Sea coast near the Sulina canal, and Romania scrambled fighter jets twice in response. No one was hurt, and the crews put out the fires on board.

The strikes came late on Saturday, the Romanian authorities said on Sunday. Prime Minister Luca Niculescu said drones had hit three commercial vessels near the Sulina canal, the waterway joining the Danube to the Black Sea, and that the armed forces had scrambled fighter jets twice in response.

Defence Minister Stefan Radu Oprea said there were no casualties and that the crews had managed to control the fires that broke out on board. The ships were the Greek-flagged oil and chemical tanker Evia Petrol V, the bulk carrier Marsol and the oil products tanker Ali Najafov, the last two flagged in Saint Kitts and Nevis. The defence ministry said Evia Petrol V was struck four nautical miles southeast of the canal and Marsol five nautical miles southeast, both inside Romanian territorial waters.

A fourth vessel had already been hit late on Friday. The Palau-flagged general cargo ship Larissa 1 was struck at about 9.40pm local time, around 12 kilometres from the coast near Sfantu Gheorghe, while heading for the port of Sulina and onward to Ukraine. The strike damaged a cargo crane but caused no fire, and no crew members were hurt.

Romanian officials did not identify who launched the drones, but said the attacks were a direct result of Russia's war in Ukraine, and the prime minister described them as violations of international law. Romania has asked NATO for help with surveillance aircraft and drone defence systems.

The Sulina canal is a main export route for Ukraine, and the strikes are the latest in a series of attacks on merchant shipping off Romania and Bulgaria since early October, in which ships have been sunk and sailors killed or left missing.

Key points

  • Three commercial ships were struck by drones late on Saturday near Romania's Sulina canal; no casualties were reported and crews controlled the fires on board.
  • The ships were the Greek-flagged tanker Evia Petrol V and the bulk carrier Marsol and tanker Ali Najafov, flagged in Saint Kitts and Nevis; two were hit inside Romanian territorial waters.
  • A fourth ship, the Palau-flagged cargo vessel Larissa 1, was hit late on Friday near Sfantu Gheorghe; a crane was damaged but there was no fire and no injuries.
  • Romania scrambled fighter jets twice and has asked NATO for surveillance and drone-defence support; who launched the drones is still unknown.
