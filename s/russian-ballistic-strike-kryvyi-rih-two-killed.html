Russian Strike on Zelenskyy's Hometown Kills Two, Wounds 60

Two Russian ballistic missiles hit the centre of Kryvyi Rih on Friday evening, killing a seven-year-old boy and a 20-year-old paramedic, and the wounded toll has now risen to 60, including six children, local officials said.

The missiles struck the car park of a popular restaurant in the city centre, in a densely populated area, on World Children's Day. It was the second attack on the city in a day, after a strike on an infrastructure facility earlier on Friday, and it followed a large Russian aerial assault that killed seven people in Kyiv the same morning. Kryvyi Rih is the home town of President Volodymyr Zelensky.

The boy died in hospital after being brought in with serious head injuries. The paramedic had rushed to the scene to help the wounded and also died in hospital. Of the 60 injured, 28 were still in hospital on Saturday morning, including children, and five people were in intensive care, the regional military administration said. A three-year-old girl and a four-year-old boy were among the children hurt.

The attack followed a pattern officials described as a deliberate strike on rescuers. After the two ballistic missiles hit, further rockets and drones struck the same spot while emergency workers were treating the wounded, the head of the city's Defence Council said. A further drone hit an infrastructure facility in the city overnight, and rescue and repair work was hampered by continuing air raid alerts. The regional prosecutor's office said the missiles were preliminarily identified as Iskander-M ballistic missiles.

The strikes set off four fires and damaged about 25 apartment buildings, a hospital, schools, businesses, garages, ambulances and other vehicles, according to preliminary information. Ukraine's president said Ukrainian forces had hit three Russian ballistic missile launchers overnight, destroying one of them. Elsewhere, 11 people, including two children, were wounded when a Russian drone hit a five-storey apartment building in the Kharkiv region, the State Emergency Service said. The Kryvyi Rih attack came hours after two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland. The story is still developing.

Key points

  • Two Russian ballistic missiles hit a restaurant car park in central Kryvyi Rih on Friday evening, killing a seven-year-old boy and a 20-year-old paramedic
  • 60 people were wounded, including six children; 28 remain in hospital and five are in intensive care, regional officials said on Saturday
  • Further strikes hit the same site during the rescue operation, in what officials called a deliberate attack on emergency workers
  • Four fires broke out and about 25 apartment buildings, a hospital, schools and businesses were damaged; Ukraine said its forces hit three Russian missile launchers overnight
