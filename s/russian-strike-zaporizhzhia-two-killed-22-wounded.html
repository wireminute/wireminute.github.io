Russian Strike on Zaporizhzhia Kills Two and Wounds 32, Including Four Children

A Russian strike on a residential area of Zaporizhzhia overnight killed two people and wounded 32, including four children, Ukrainian regional officials said on Tuesday, as the casualty toll rose steadily through the morning.

A Russian strike on a residential area of Zaporizhzhia overnight killed two people and wounded 32, including four children, Ukrainian regional officials said on Tuesday, as the casualty toll rose steadily through the morning.

The attack came after 4 a.m. and destroyed a private house and partly demolished a five-storey apartment building in the city's Kosmichnyi district, the acting head of the Zaporizhzhia regional military administration, Mykhailo Semikin, said. Other apartment blocks, houses, cars and a transport facility were also damaged. Semikin first reported 11 people hurt, then 22, before raising the figure to 32 as rescue teams counted more casualties through the morning.

Among the injured are four children, the youngest not yet a year old and the others aged four, six and 16. One woman is in a serious condition with blast and head injuries. Earlier reports spoke of people possibly trapped under the rubble, and search and rescue work was under way at the damaged buildings.

Ivan Fedorov, who jointly leads the Zaporizhzhia Regional Defence Council, also reported two dead and confirmed the rising toll. Semikin gave the updated figures in messages on Telegram, and one local report put the injured figure as high as 40 earlier in the morning before officials settled on 32. The casualty figures could not be independently confirmed, and Russia and Ukraine both deny deliberately targeting civilians.

It was the second deadly strike on the city in two days. A Russian attack on a private clinic in Zaporizhzhia on Monday killed three people, according to local reports. The story is still developing.

Key points

  • Two people were killed and 32 wounded, including four children, in an overnight Russian strike on homes in Zaporizhzhia.
  • A private house was destroyed and a five-storey apartment building partly demolished in the Kosmichnyi district; other buildings, cars and a transport facility were damaged.
  • The injured toll rose through the morning from 11 to 22 and then to 32, regional officials said; one woman is in a serious condition with blast and head injuries.
  • The figures could not be independently confirmed, and the story is still developing.
