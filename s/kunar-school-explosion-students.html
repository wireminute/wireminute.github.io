At Least Six Students Killed and 97 Injured in Blast Beside School in Afghanistan's Kunar

At least six students have been killed and 97 others injured in an explosion beside a middle school in Afghanistan's eastern Kunar province, a hospital official said on Saturday, after explosives stored in a nearby house went off.

The blast struck at about 10 a.m. local time in the Andar Ser area, in a house beside the middle school, according to provincial officials. Mawla Dad, the head of Kunar Provincial Hospital, said the dead and injured were students and warned that the figures were preliminary and could change as more information came in.

Mawlawi Sami-ul-Haq, the province's director of information and culture, said the house belonged to people involved in mining operations who had stored dynamite there. Investigators were examining the scene, and officials have not yet said whether anyone will face action over the stored explosives.

Earlier in the day, police spokesperson Farid Dehqan had said only that several students were killed or wounded, giving no exact casualty figure. The injured were taken to hospitals in the province, where medical staff were treating dozens of wounded students, and the hospital head said staff were still counting the injured as more arrived through the morning.

No group has claimed responsibility, and officials have not described the blast as an attack. Kunar, which shares a border with Pakistan, has lived with insecurity and the dangers of explosives for years, and incidents involving stored or leftover explosives near schools and places where children gather have happened before. The story is still developing, and further details are expected as officials complete their checks at the scene.

Key points
