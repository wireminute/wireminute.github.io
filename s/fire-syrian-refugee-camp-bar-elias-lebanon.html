Fire Sweeps Through Syrian Refugee Camp in Lebanon's Bekaa Valley, Leaving Hundreds Homeless

A fire swept through a Syrian refugee camp in the town of Bar Elias in eastern Lebanon on Saturday, destroying dozens of tents and leaving hundreds of people without shelter, as firefighters brought the blaze under control.

The blaze broke out at the camp in Bar Elias, in the Zahle district of the Bekaa Valley, and spread quickly through the tightly packed tents. Civil defence teams from several centres were called out and extinguished the fire, but the scale of the damage is still being counted: witnesses put the toll at all 59 tents housing 75 families, while the civil defence's initial assessment counted 30 tents destroyed.

One person was taken to hospital after breathing in smoke, according to the civil defence, and no deaths have been reported. The cause of the fire has not been established, and officials have not yet commented on it. Lebanon has hosted large numbers of Syrian refugees since the conflict in Syria began in 2011, many of them living in informal tented settlements across the Bekaa Valley.
