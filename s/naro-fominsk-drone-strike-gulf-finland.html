Estonia Says Ukrainian Drones Disabled Russian Missile Ship in Gulf of Finland

Estonia's military says Ukrainian drones struck a Russian missile corvette in the Gulf of Finland this week, leaving the warship unable to sail under its own power and killing and wounding members of its crew.

The ship, the Naro-Fominsk, a Buyan-M-class corvette armed with cruise missiles, was hit on Wednesday morning during a wider Ukrainian drone operation against the Ust-Luga oil export terminal on Russia's Baltic coast. Ukraine's security service said it carried out the strike. Finland's armed forces confirmed that a warship had been hit in the gulf, without saying where or how.

The fullest account so far came from Estonia on Friday. Colonel Aron Kalmus, a deputy commander in the Estonian Defence Forces, told a briefing that more than three drones struck the ship near Gogland Island, in the gulf. The damage was extensive, he said, and members of the crew were killed and wounded. Estonian military intelligence had established how many drones hit the vessel, he added, but he would not give the number.

After the attack, the corvette first tried to make for St Petersburg under its own power, then faltered and had to be helped by another vessel, according to the Estonian account. Estonia's defence minister had said earlier that the ship was hit at about 05:30 local time on Wednesday and was seen heading back toward St Petersburg with smoke coming from it. Moscow has said nothing about the strike, and the full extent of the damage is unclear.

The strike is believed to be the first time Ukrainian drones have attacked a Russian ship in a part of the Baltic Sea used by civilian shipping, and it brings the war closer to the coastline of NATO members Finland and Estonia, which both border the gulf. Finland's foreign minister said the situation in the Baltic remained stable and safe, with no threat to marine traffic. The attack came as Ukraine widened its campaign against Russian energy and military targets, including the Ust-Luga terminal, which handles oil exports from the Baltic coast.

Key points

  • Estonia says Ukrainian drones hit the Russian corvette Naro-Fominsk in the Gulf of Finland on Wednesday, with more than three drones striking the ship.
  • The Buyan-M-class missile ship was left unable to sail on its own and had to be assisted toward St Petersburg; crew members were killed and wounded.
  • Ukraine's security service said it carried out the strike as part of a wider drone operation against the Ust-Luga oil terminal on Russia's Baltic coast.
  • The attack is believed to be the first on a Russian warship in a Baltic lane used by civilian shipping, close to the coasts of NATO members Finland and Estonia.
