Another Tanker Hit in Hormuz as Iran Fires on US Bases in Kuwait and Bahrain

A tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the Strait of Hormuz and Iran fired on American bases in Kuwait and Bahrain, as the Saudi-led coalition pressed its air campaign against the Houthis in Yemen.

A tanker sailing through the Strait of Hormuz was hit by a projectile of unknown origin on Tuesday evening, maritime monitors said, in the latest attack on shipping in the waterway. The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations centre said the vessel was struck in its engine room about six nautical miles north of Khasab, on Oman's Musandam coast. No one on board was hurt, the crew was reported safe, and the extent of the damage was not yet known. The agency did not name the vessel, say what fired the projectile, or link the incident to earlier attacks.

The strike came on a day of widening exchanges across the Gulf. Iran fired on an American airbase in Kuwait and launched attacks toward Bahrain, in what appeared to be retaliation for American strikes the day before. A US military official said American forces had carried out a defensive strike using precision weapons against a missile launch site on Qeshm Island, off Iran's southern coast. Bahrain said it had come under attack for a second day in a row, and Saudi Arabia condemned Iranian attacks on Kuwait, Bahrain and a tanker belonging to the United Arab Emirates.

In Yemen, the Saudi-led coalition said its warplanes destroyed a ballistic missile launch platform in Saada, in the north of the country, and intercepted a missile heading for southern Saudi Arabia along with two drones. The coalition said a wider air operation had hit 60 targets across territory held by the Houthis, including the capital Sanaa and the provinces of Amran, Saada and Hodeida. The Houthis said the strikes killed 17 people, including 15 at a sponge factory in Hodeida and two in the Kitaf district of Saada, and wounded others at a shoe factory in Sanaa.

At sea, Iran's army said three oil tankers caught fire east of the Strait of Hormuz after striking naval mines, and its Revolutionary Guard said the vessels had ignored warnings and entered restricted waters with their navigation systems switched off. Iranian state media later said a fourth tanker had also hit a mine. None of those claims could be independently verified, and the vessels were not identified. The fighting, now in its ninth month, continues to disrupt one of the world's most important oil shipping routes and to unsettle energy markets.

Key points

  • Maritime monitors say a tanker was hit by a projectile north of Khasab, Oman; its engine room was struck, no one was hurt and the crew was reported safe
  • Iran fired on a US airbase in Kuwait and toward Bahrain, while the US said it carried out a defensive strike on a missile site on Qeshm Island
  • The Saudi-led coalition says it destroyed a Houthi missile platform in Saada and hit 60 targets; the Houthis say 17 people were killed
  • Iran's army says three tankers caught fire after hitting mines east of Hormuz; the claims could not be independently verified
