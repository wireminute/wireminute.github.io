Russian Ballistic Missiles Hit Kyiv Shopping Centre and Two Buildings, Three Injured

Russian ballistic missiles struck Kyiv late on Wednesday, hitting a shopping centre and two other buildings in the city's Desnianskyi district and injuring three people, the city's mayor said.

Explosions were heard across the Ukrainian capital from about 10:45pm local time, after the air force warned of a ballistic missile threat and a high-speed target heading towards the city. Mayor Vitali Klitschko said a building in an industrial zone in the Desnianskyi district, on the eastern bank of the Dnipro river, was hit first, followed by a second non-residential building in the same district. A shopping centre in the district was then struck.

Three people were injured in the attacks, and two were taken to hospital, one of them in a serious condition, according to updated figures from city officials. Klitschko said the attack was continuing and urged residents to remain in shelters. A second wave of explosions was reported at about 11:36pm local time, and monitoring channels warned more missiles could follow. The extent of the damage was still being assessed.
