Andorra Goalkeeper Owono Banned for 12 Matches Over Fatal Collision with De Miguel

FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesús Owono has been suspended for 12 matches after the on-field collision that killed Tenerife forward Jesús de Miguel earlier this month, Spain's football federation has ruled.

The disciplinary committee of the Spanish Football Federation handed down the ban on Friday, calling it the maximum punishment available for violent conduct that causes serious injury. The ruling follows the second-division match between Andorra and Tenerife on 2 November, when Owono charged out towards midfield to meet a high ball and collided with De Miguel.

The referee dismissed Owono at once, recording that the goalkeeper had caught his opponent in the chest with his leg and used excessive force while the ball was still in play. Play was stopped, and De Miguel, 30, was taken to hospital, where he was placed in intensive care. He suffered a cardiac arrest and died the following day, 3 November.

In its written decision, the committee said it had to judge the incident on the facts set out in the referee's report, and that the fatal outcome was already a matter of public record. Weighing the force of the challenge and the circumstances around it, the panel concluded that the full 12-match suspension was justified. The decision also carries fines set out in the federation's disciplinary code, and it was made under Article 102, which covers violent challenges on an opponent in play that cause serious injury.

Police in Spain have opened an investigation into the death under judicial supervision. Owono, 25, has described the days since the match as the hardest of his life. He has pulled out of Equatorial Guinea's squad for the November Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers against Zimbabwe, and Andorra coach Carles Manso said on Thursday that the goalkeeper himself would decide if and when he returned to football, with the club giving him time and space.

De Miguel's funeral was held on Thursday 5 November, two days after his death. The ban rules Owono out of Andorra's next 12 league fixtures.

Key points

  • Jesús Owono has been suspended for 12 matches, the maximum sanction, after his challenge on Tenerife forward Jesús de Miguel.
  • The collision happened in the 2 November second-division match between FC Andorra and Tenerife; De Miguel, 30, died the next day after a cardiac arrest.
  • The federation's disciplinary committee ruled the challenge was violent conduct causing serious injury under Article 102 of its code, and the decision also carries fines.
  • Spanish police have opened an investigation under judicial supervision; Owono has withdrawn from Equatorial Guinea's November qualifiers.
