Two Soldiers Martyred as 16 Militants Killed in North Waziristan Operations

Security forces killed 16 militants in North Waziristan on Saturday, the military said, in operations that also claimed the lives of two soldiers.

The operations were based on intelligence and targeted militant hideouts in Mir Ali, Datta Khel, Ali Khel and nearby areas over the past 12 hours. In Ali Khel, 12 militants were killed, and two of the dead were Afghan nationals. Two more militants were killed in Mir Ali and two in Datta Khel. The military described the dead as members of a group it calls Fitna al-Khawarij, which it says is backed by India, and said the strikes dealt a heavy blow to the network in the district. The state uses the name Fitna al-Khawarij for fighters of the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan.

The two soldiers who died were named as Havildar Asmat Ali, 43, from Swabi, and Naik Aftab Ali, 32, from Mardan. The military said both fought bravely before dying. Weapons and ammunition were recovered from the dead militants, who the military said had taken part in numerous attacks, including the killing of civilians.

The military said clearance operations were still under way to track down any militants left in the area, and that the wider campaign would continue at full pace. President Asif Ali Zardari praised the security forces and offered condolences to the families of the two soldiers, saying the campaign would go on until the last militant was eliminated. The military said the campaign, known as Azm-e-Istehkam, would continue until the threat was removed.

The fighting came a day after the military said 74 militants had been killed in operations across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan, 43 in the northwest over two days, including 35 in Mir Ali, and 31 in Balochistan since an operation began on 3 November. Attacks have risen sharply in both provinces since the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan ended a ceasefire with the government in November 2022.

Key points

  • Sixteen militants were killed in intelligence-based operations in North Waziristan on Saturday, the military said.
  • Twelve were killed in Ali Khel, including two Afghan nationals, and two each in Mir Ali and Datta Khel.
  • Havildar Asmat Ali, 43, of Swabi and Naik Aftab Ali, 32, of Mardan were martyred in the fighting.
  • The operations came a day after the military said 74 militants had been killed across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan.
