Ukraine Says Five Russian Warplanes Hit in Strike on Savasleyka Airfield

Ukraine said on Wednesday it had damaged five Russian military aircraft in a strike on an airfield about 300 kilometres east of Moscow that houses jets capable of carrying hypersonic missiles.

Ukraine said on Wednesday it had damaged five Russian military aircraft in a strike on the Savasleyka airfield, about 300 kilometres east of Moscow, in one of its deepest attacks of the war on a base used by jets that carry hypersonic missiles.

The General Staff said the overnight strike hit three MiG-31 fighter jets and two Su-34 fighter-bombers at the airfield in the Nizhny Novgorod region. It said early checks showed the damaged aircraft would be unable to fly for some time, while the full extent of the damage was still being assessed. Savasleyka is home to MiG-31K jets adapted to carry Kinzhal aeroballistic missiles, which Russia has used against Ukrainian cities.

Kyiv said the same overnight operation also hit two oil refineries in the Samara region, around 800 kilometres from the front line: the Syzran and Kuibyshev plants, where processing units and storage tanks were damaged. The refineries have been struck repeatedly during the war, and the strikes are part of a long-running Ukrainian campaign against Russian fuel production. Ukrainian forces also reported hitting a long-range radar station in occupied Luhansk and fuel depots, supply warehouses and an ammunition store in occupied parts of Luhansk and Donetsk.

The strikes came as Russia launched fresh overnight attacks on Ukraine's energy infrastructure, cutting power in several regions. Ten people were killed across Ukraine between Monday and Tuesday, and thousands of homes were left without electricity, in the latest exchange of long-range strikes between the two sides. Russia did not immediately confirm the airfield strike.

Key points

  • Ukraine says overnight strikes on Savasleyka airfield hit three MiG-31 jets and two Su-34 bombers; early checks say the jets will be grounded for some time.
  • Savasleyka, about 300 km east of Moscow, hosts MiG-31K jets that carry Kinzhal hypersonic missiles.
  • Two refineries in the Samara region, Syzran and Kuibyshev, were also hit, with processing units and storage tanks damaged.
  • A radar station and fuel and supply sites in occupied eastern Ukraine were hit in the same operation, as Russian strikes on Ukrainian energy sites killed 10 people.
