Boxing Champion Jorge Linares Detained in Texas as US Immigration Opens Deportation Case

Former three-weight world boxing champion Jorge Linares is being held by US immigration authorities in Texas and faces deportation proceedings after officers detained him at El Paso International Airport on Sunday.

US immigration authorities confirmed on Tuesday that the 41-year-old Venezuelan remains in custody at the El Paso Processing Centre and will stay there while removal proceedings go ahead. He was detained on Sunday 15 November by Border Patrol agents and officers from Immigration and Customs Enforcement, known as ICE, at the airport in the Texas border city of El Paso.

Immigration officials say Linares entered the United States on 26 October 2023 with permission to stay for a temporary period and remained in the country after that permission ran out. A spokesperson said that having an application pending, or holding work authorisation, does not by itself give a person legal status in the United States.

Linares announced his own detention in a social media post on Sunday, writing that immigration officers were holding him in El Paso and calling the situation crazy. His lawyers, the firm Wilner and O'Reilly, said on Tuesday that his stay in the country is lawful, that he has cooperated fully with the authorities and that they are in contact with immigration officials to resolve the matter as quickly as possible. The two accounts leave his legal status unresolved for now.

Linares retired from boxing in 2023 after a professional career of more than two decades, winning 47 of his 56 bouts, 29 of them by knockout, and holding world titles at featherweight, super featherweight and lightweight. Gilberto Mendoza, president of the World Boxing Association, confirmed the detention and said immigration lawyers were handling the case.

Key points

  • Linares, 41, was detained on Sunday at El Paso International Airport and is being held at the El Paso Processing Centre in Texas.
  • US immigration authorities say he entered the country in October 2023 on a temporary permit and stayed beyond the period it allowed.
  • His lawyers insist his stay in the United States is lawful and say he has cooperated fully with the authorities.
  • The retired three-weight world champion won 47 of his 56 professional bouts before retiring in 2023.
