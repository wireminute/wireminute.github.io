Pakistan Reach First-Ever SAFF Championship Final After Late Win Over Bangladesh

Pakistan reached the final of the SAFF Championship for the first time on Saturday, beating hosts Bangladesh 1-0 in Dhaka to set up a title clash with arch-rivals India on Tuesday.

Abdul Samad struck the only goal five minutes from the end of normal time at the National Stadium in Dhaka, punishing a mistake by Bangladesh goalkeeper Mehedi Hasan Srabon to silence a packed home crowd. The win takes Pakistan into their first final since the regional championship began in 1993.

Bangladesh had pressed for long spells and chased a first final appearance since 2005, but could not find a way through a disciplined Pakistan defence, and the late goal ended their hopes in front of their own supporters. Pakistan, who had lost 3-2 to India in the group stage, held firm through the closing minutes to protect the lead.

The breakthrough came after a mistake by Srabon, whose throw from inside his own area went wrong, allowing Samad to finish with a left-footed strike. The semi-final had been goalless for most of a tense, physical contest, with both sides struggling to create clear chances before the late twist.

Pakistan will now face India in the final on Tuesday, 17 November, at the same ground in Dhaka. India reached the decider by beating Bhutan 2-1 in the day's first semi-final and will be chasing a record-extending 10th title, while Pakistan will be playing for their first.

The result caps a historic campaign for Pakistan, coached by Nolberto Solano, who had never before gone beyond the semi-finals of the competition. For Bangladesh, the defeat ended a bid for a second title after their triumph in 2003, and a first final in more than two decades.

Key points

  • Pakistan beat hosts Bangladesh 1-0 in Saturday's semi-final in Dhaka to reach their first SAFF Championship final.
  • Abdul Samad scored the only goal five minutes from the end of normal time after a mistake by Bangladesh goalkeeper Mehedi Hasan Srabon.
  • Pakistan will face India in the final on Tuesday, 17 November, at the same stadium in Dhaka.
  • India beat Bhutan 2-1 in the first semi-final and are chasing a record-extending 10th title; Pakistan lost 3-2 to India in the group stage.
