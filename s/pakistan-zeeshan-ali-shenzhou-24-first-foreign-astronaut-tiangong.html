Pakistan's Zeeshan Ali to Become First Foreign Astronaut Aboard China's Tiangong Station

Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali will fly to China's Tiangong space station aboard the Shenzhou-24 mission launching on Wednesday, becoming the first foreign astronaut to visit the station and the first Pakistani to travel to space, the China Manned Space Agency announced on Tuesday.

The agency named the three-person crew as commander Tang Hongbo, flight engineer Wu Yi and Zeeshan Ali, a payload specialist from Pakistan's space agency, the Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission. The Shenzhou-24 spacecraft is scheduled to lift off at 8:18 a.m. Beijing Time on Wednesday, 25 November, from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China aboard a Long March-2F rocket.

Zeeshan Ali is expected to spend about a week aboard Tiangong, carrying out scientific experiments alongside the Chinese crew, before returning to Earth aboard the Shenzhou-23 spacecraft, which has been docked at the station since May. Two members of the Shenzhou-23 crew, Zhang Zhiyuan and Lai Ka-ying, the first astronaut from Hong Kong, will return with him after their six-month rotation.

The third Shenzhou-23 crew member, Zhu Yangzhu, will remain aboard the station for another six months alongside the two Chinese Shenzhou-24 astronauts, putting him on course to complete China's first year-long stay in orbit.

Zeeshan Ali, a former pilot in Pakistan's armed forces, was chosen from two Pakistani candidates who trained for months at China's astronaut training centre in Beijing. The other candidate, Khurram Daud, will serve as backup. The two were picked through a three-stage assessment under a cooperation agreement China and Pakistan signed in Islamabad in February 2025, and the mission is the first joint Chinese-foreign crewed flight in the history of China's human spaceflight programme. The Shenzhou-24 mission logo features the jasmine, Pakistan's national flower.

Pakistan's planning minister, Ahsan Iqbal, travelled to Beijing to attend the departure ceremony, calling the flight a historic moment for the country. The mission is China's 18th crewed spaceflight, and the crew names were announced a day before launch, in line with the agency's usual practice.

Key points

  • Muhammad Zeeshan Ali of Pakistan's space agency SUPARCO was named to the Shenzhou-24 crew, launching at 8:18 a.m. Beijing Time on Wednesday, 25 November, from Jiuquan.
  • He will be the first foreign astronaut to visit China's Tiangong space station and the first Pakistani to travel to space.
  • He will spend about a week aboard the station as a payload specialist running scientific experiments, then return to Earth aboard the Shenzhou-23 spacecraft.
  • He was chosen from two Pakistani candidates under a February 2025 China-Pakistan agreement; Khurram Daud will serve as backup.
