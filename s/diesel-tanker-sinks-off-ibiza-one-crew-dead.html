Diesel Tanker Sinks Off Ibiza, Killing One Crew Member as Spain Activates Pollution Plan

A tanker carrying diesel has sunk off the Spanish island of Ibiza, killing one crew member, and Spain has raised its maritime pollution alert as rescue teams watch for fuel leaking from the wreck.

The Malta-flagged chemical tanker Med Emre went down off Ibiza in the early hours of Thursday while travelling from Algeciras to Barcelona, emergency services in the Balearic Islands said. The ship could have been carrying as much as 7,000 tonnes of diesel. Rescuers brought all 17 crew members to safety, but one later died in hospital on the island.

Spain's maritime authority raised its pollution emergency response to level two, its second-highest setting, and crews are monitoring the area for fuel in the water. Officials said no fuel had been spotted so far, but the wreck sits near protected waters and the environmental risk is still being assessed.
