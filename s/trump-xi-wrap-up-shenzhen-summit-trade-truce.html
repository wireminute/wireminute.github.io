Trump, Xi and Putin Conclude First-Ever Trilateral Talks After 90 Minutes With No Deal Announced

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin have concluded the first-ever trilateral meeting of the leaders of the United States, China and Russia, after about 90 minutes of talks in Shenzhen, with no joint statement or agreement announced.

The two leaders met at Qilin Villa, a lakeside state guesthouse in Shenzhen, in their third substantial meeting this year and their first in person in two months, following their meeting at the White House in September. They agreed to draw up an action plan for a constructive strategic stability relationship built on respect, fairness and reciprocity, though what the plan will contain was not spelled out. They also agreed to keep working together on artificial intelligence and narcotics enforcement.

On trade, the two sides agreed in principle to extend the truce that is due to expire in January, and they plan a further bilateral meeting at the Group of Twenty summit in Florida in December to settle outstanding issues. Trump said the trade committee set up at their May meeting in Beijing had helped restore balance and that the tariff burden was being shared fairly. Xi said ties were stable and improving and that he wanted to keep them on the right track. On Taiwan, Xi said the American side should do the right thing and avoid major disruptions to the relationship.

On security, both leaders said they shared the goal of keeping crude oil moving through the Strait of Hormuz and preventing Iran from obtaining nuclear weapons, against the backdrop of the conflict involving Iran. The meeting came as the three leaders gathered in Shenzhen for the Asia-Pacific summit, with the wars in Ukraine and Iran, nuclear arms and the military use of artificial intelligence all on the wider agenda.

After a late lunch, Trump and Xi moved into the three-way session with Putin, which began at about 4:16 p.m. local time and ran for roughly 90 minutes, state media reported. Trump told the other two leaders they were making history, Xi called the gathering of groundbreaking significance, and Putin said the three countries shared an interest in resolving Ukraine. Xi said every effort must be made to prevent nuclear war, and went further by calling for nuclear weapons to be banned outright and destroyed entirely so the world could be free of them. Trump said the three countries were uniquely positioned to discuss reducing the number of nuclear weapons. No joint statement was issued and no agreements from the three-way talks had been confirmed. Earlier in the day, Trump and Putin had held a roughly 40-minute bilateral meeting that the Kremlin described as a preparatory session for the trilateral talks. The last treaty limiting the nuclear arsenals of the United States and Russia expired in February, leaving no binding caps in place between the two largest nuclear powers. Putin also said the three countries held joint responsibility for maintaining international peace and security.

Key points

  • Trump and Xi wrapped up their Shenzhen bilateral, agreeing in principle to extend the trade truce due to expire in January.
  • The three-way meeting with Putin lasted about 90 minutes and ended with no joint statement or agreement announced; Xi said every effort must be made to prevent nuclear war and called for nuclear weapons to be banned and destroyed entirely.
  • They share the aim of keeping oil flowing through the Strait of Hormuz and preventing Iran from obtaining nuclear weapons.
  • The leaders agreed to draw up an action plan for a stable strategic relationship and to keep cooperating on artificial intelligence and narcotics enforcement.
