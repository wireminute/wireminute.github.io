Haiti Police Say 34 Suspected Gang Members Killed in Martissant Raid

Haiti's national police said at least 34 suspected gang members were killed in a major operation on Thursday in Martissant, a southern district of the capital that has been under gang control for more than five years, and reported no officers or civilians hurt.

The operation took place in Martissant, in the south of Port-au-Prince, a city of around 3 million people. Police released footage of armoured vehicles moving through the neighbourhood, and a separate clip showed a building collapsing under a large cloud of smoke. The force said the raid targeted armed groups that have held the district since 2021.

Police said the operation was carried out alongside the government's anti-gang Task Force, which deploys explosive drones with backing from Vectus Global, a private security company founded by Erik Prince, who also founded Blackwater. The force said no Salvadoran personnel took part in the raid, even though about 2,500 Salvadoran troops are now in Haiti.

Martissant sits on the main road out of Port-au-Prince towards Haiti's western and southern cities, a route used by thousands of people every day before rival gangs began fighting for control of the area in 2021. Security analysts say the neighbourhood is now dominated by the Gran Ravine, Ti Bwa and 5 Segond gangs. Armed groups have taken over much of the capital and the regions around it.

Security operations against the gangs have stepped up since the Salvadoran troops arrived last month. The United Nations estimates that more than 2,250 people have been killed in such operations during the first nine months of this year, including more than 300 civilians. Authorities have not published an overall casualty count for the campaign.

Police have not named any of those killed in Thursday's raid, and the dead have not been independently identified. The raid comes as the police and their partners press a campaign to push the gangs out of districts they have held for years.

Key points

  • Police say at least 34 suspected gang members were killed in Thursday's operation in Martissant, in southern Port-au-Prince, with no officers or civilians reported hurt.
  • The raid was run with the government's anti-gang Task Force, which uses explosive drones backed by the private firm Vectus Global; no Salvadoran troops took part.
  • Martissant has been under gang control since 2021 and sits on the main road linking the capital to Haiti's western and southern cities.
  • The UN estimates more than 2,250 people have been killed in security operations in the first nine months of this year, including over 300 civilians.
