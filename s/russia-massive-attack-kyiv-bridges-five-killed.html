Russia's Overnight Barrage Kills Eight in Ukraine as Kyiv's Bridges Are Hit

Russia fired more than 80 missiles and 400 drones at Ukraine overnight, killing eight people and damaging three bridges across the Dnipro River in Kyiv, in the biggest attack on the capital's crossings since the war began.

Ukraine's Air Force said Russia launched 84 cruise missiles, ballistic missiles and more than 400 drones overnight and into Friday morning, striking Kyiv and nine other regions. Almost all of the cruise missiles were destroyed, officials said. Six people were killed in Kyiv, including three believed to be employees of the city water utility, and two more were killed in Lviv, where a railway station was hit. Twenty-eight people were wounded, the prime minister said.

Ballistic missiles damaged the Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi bridges over the Dnipro. Traffic on the Podilskyi crossing was closed from the right bank to the left bank, the Darnytskyi bridge was shut for assessment, and the Southern Bridge stayed open in one direction only, with weight limits for vehicles. The mayor urged residents to avoid driving to work. Fires broke out in the Obolonskyi, Podilskyi and Holosiivskyi districts, a critical infrastructure facility was damaged, and several districts lost power, with the grid operator calling the situation extremely critical.

Russia's defence ministry said it had targeted road and rail bridges in Kyiv used to transport weapons and military equipment to the battlefield, and said it had also struck military, energy and transport targets, including port infrastructure near Odesa. The attack came a day after President Vladimir Putin promised a far more painful response to Ukraine's strikes on the bridge linking Russia and Crimea, which severed its highway earlier this week.

The overnight barrage followed a day-long wave of drone strikes on central Kyiv on Thursday, while Russia, China and the United States were holding a three-way summit in China. Drones repeatedly struck the central Podil district, killing one person and injuring at least four. The Austrian embassy was damaged when a missile fell in front of the building during opening hours, shattering windows and damaging vehicles, with about ten people inside at the time. The Dutch embassy suffered minor damage, debris fell near the Brazilian embassy and Ukraine's foreign ministry, and one of the large windows of St Volodymyr's Cathedral was shattered. The European Union's foreign policy chief said the bloc would call in Russia's envoy in Brussels, saying international law protects diplomatic premises, and the Netherlands summoned the Russian ambassador.

Key points

  • An overnight barrage of 84 cruise missiles and more than 400 drones killed eight people, six in Kyiv and two in Lviv, and wounded 28.
  • Three bridges over the Dnipro were damaged, the Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi, with traffic closed or restricted across the capital.
  • Russia said it targeted bridges used to move weapons and equipment, a day after Putin promised a harsher response to strikes on the Crimea Bridge.
  • A day earlier, drone strikes on central Kyiv damaged the Austrian and Dutch embassies and a cathedral, killing one person, and the EU said it would call in Russia's envoy.
