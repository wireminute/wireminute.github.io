Russia Strikes Kyiv Bridges in Massive Overnight Attack Hours After Summit With Trump and Xi

Russia launched a massive overnight missile and drone attack on Ukraine hours after President Vladimir Putin left Shenzhen, killing at least five people and damaging three bridges across the Dnipro River in Kyiv.

Russia launched a massive overnight missile and drone attack on Ukraine hours after President Vladimir Putin left Shenzhen, where he had met Donald Trump and Xi Jinping for three-way talks. The strikes, which lasted more than six hours, killed at least five people and damaged three bridges across the Dnipro River in Kyiv, the city's mayor said.

Three people were killed in Kyiv, believed to be employees of the city water utility, and about 15 were injured. Two more were killed in Lviv, where a strike hit a railway station. Several districts of Kyiv lost power, and the grid operator called the situation extremely critical, with emergency outages in western regions.

Russia's defence ministry said it struck military, energy and transport targets, including port infrastructure near Odesa. The attack came a day after Putin promised a far more painful response to Ukraine's strikes on the bridge linking Russia and Crimea.

Key points

  • At least five people were killed, three in Kyiv and two in Lviv, and about 15 were injured in the capital.
  • Three bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv were damaged, with traffic suspended or restricted, and power cut in several districts.
  • Russia said it struck military, energy and transport targets; the attack followed Putin's vow of retaliation for strikes on the Crimea Bridge.
