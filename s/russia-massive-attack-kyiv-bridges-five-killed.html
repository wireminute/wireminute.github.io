Russia Pounds Kyiv for Over a Day, Damaging Embassies, a Cathedral and Three Bridges

Russia pounded Kyiv with drones and missiles for more than a day, killing at least five people, damaging the embassies of Austria and the Netherlands and a historic cathedral, and knocking out power and three bridges across the Dnipro River.

The assault began with a day-long wave of drone strikes on central Kyiv on Thursday, while Russia, China and the United States were holding a three-way summit in China. Explosions echoed across the capital through the day, and drones repeatedly struck the central Podil district. One person was killed and at least four were injured, Ukraine's prime minister said.

The Austrian embassy was damaged when a missile fell in front of the building during opening hours, shattering windows and damaging vehicles, with about ten people inside at the time. No staff were hurt. The Dutch embassy suffered minor damage, and debris fell near the Brazilian embassy and Ukraine's foreign ministry. One of the large windows of St Volodymyr's Cathedral was shattered.

The attacks drew a swift diplomatic response. The European Union's foreign policy chief, Kaja Kallas, said the bloc would call in Russia's envoy in Brussels, saying international law protects diplomatic premises. The Netherlands summoned the Russian ambassador, and Austria's chancellor called the strikes completely unacceptable.

Hours later, Russia launched a massive overnight missile and drone attack, hours after President Vladimir Putin left the summit in Shenzhen. The strikes, which lasted more than six hours, killed three people in Kyiv, believed to be employees of the city water utility, and two more in Lviv, where a railway station was hit. Three bridges over the Dnipro were damaged, several districts lost power, and the grid operator called the situation extremely critical.

Russia's defence ministry said it struck military, energy and transport targets, including port infrastructure near Odesa. The attack came a day after Putin promised a far more painful response to Ukraine's strikes on the bridge linking Russia and Crimea.

Key points

  • Day-long drone strikes on central Kyiv on Thursday damaged the Austrian and Dutch embassies and St Volodymyr's Cathedral, killing one person and injuring at least four.
  • The EU said it would call in Russia's envoy in Brussels, and the Netherlands summoned the Russian ambassador, after the embassy damage.
  • An overnight missile and drone attack then killed five people in total, three in Kyiv and two in Lviv, and damaged three bridges over the Dnipro.
  • Russia said it hit military, energy and transport targets, a day after Putin vowed a harsher response to strikes on the Crimea Bridge.
