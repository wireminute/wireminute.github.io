Verstappen Takes Wild Las Vegas Win as Late Mercedes Drama Hands McLaren a Double Podium

Max Verstappen won a thrilling Las Vegas Grand Prix on Saturday, surviving a night-long battle with both Mercedes cars before late trouble for the Silver Arrows handed McLaren a double podium, for his third win on the streets of Nevada in four years.

Max Verstappen won a wild Las Vegas Grand Prix on Saturday night, beating Lando Norris by 0.639 seconds after a race in which the lead changed hands 35 times across 50 laps under the lights. Oscar Piastri completed the podium in third, 1.529 seconds behind the winner, after starting 17th on the grid.

Verstappen started from pole, inherited from Kimi Antonelli after the newly crowned world champion took a five-place grid penalty for an engine change, but he spent much of the night fending off the two Mercedes cars. George Russell led spells of the race and Antonelli moved into second on lap 39, yet neither Silver Arrow finished on the podium.

Russell's challenge ended three laps from the finish when he locked up and clipped a wall, dropping him to ninth. Antonelli then lost power in the closing laps, and the two McLarens swept past him. Antonelli held on for fourth, ahead of Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto, while Norris recovered from an early drop to 11th to finish second.

The two Ferraris collided with each other during the race, and Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc each collected a 10-second penalty for separate incidents, finishing seventh and tenth. Verstappen called the race surreal, saying he had never seen anything like it in Formula 1.

The victory was the 75th of Verstappen's career and his fourth in six races. It moved him 37 points clear of Hamilton in the fight for second place in the championship, on 304 points to Hamilton's 267, with Russell fourth on 264, level with Norris. Antonelli, 20, had already been crowned the youngest world champion in history earlier in the month, and Mercedes had already sealed both titles, so the Las Vegas fight was for pride and the runner-up spot.

Key points

  • Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas Grand Prix by 0.639 seconds from Lando Norris, with Oscar Piastri third, after 35 lead changes in 50 laps.
  • George Russell hit the wall three laps from the end and finished ninth; Kimi Antonelli lost power late on and faded to fourth.
  • The win was the 75th of Verstappen's career and his fourth in six races; he now leads Hamilton by 37 points in the fight for second in the championship.
  • Antonelli, already crowned the youngest world champion at 20, started sixth after a grid penalty, while both Ferraris collided and collected 10-second penalties.
