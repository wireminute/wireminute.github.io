Trump Says Victory Over Iran Is at Hand and Offers Tehran a Chance to Make a Deal

President Donald Trump told American troops on Thursday that the United States has "victory at hand" in its war on Iran, while offering Tehran another chance to reach a deal and avoid further bloodshed.

Speaking from his Mar-a-Lago home in Florida during a series of Thanksgiving calls to service members deployed overseas, Trump said the timing of victory was his to decide. "We have victory at hand," he said on the first call, to an Army unit involved in the campaign against Iran, known as Operation Epic Fury. He was joined by First Lady Melania Trump.

Trump said Washington was still giving Iran room to negotiate, offering Tehran "every chance to make a deal" while doubting its leaders would take it. He claimed Iran's air force, navy and air defences had been destroyed and that Tehran had been unable to resume work at the nuclear sites struck by the United States. He also said American forces had fired 150 missiles to stop attacks on commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz.

The president named a further target if the United States were to strike again, saying he would "absolutely hit Pickaxe Mountain", but that no real activity had been seen there. He said he had discussed Iran in recent talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping, and that both leaders wanted a deal and were helping him in that regard.

Iran's foreign minister, Abbas Araghchi, said on Thursday that diplomacy remained part of Tehran's approach, with mediators from Qatar and Pakistan still passing messages between the two sides, but that strengthening the country's resilience against outside pressure was the immediate priority. There has been no ceasefire, and fighting has continued across the Gulf this week, with two tankers catching fire after hitting mines near Oman's Khasab port on Thursday. Two days earlier, Trump said American military operations against Iran were funded through January, while acknowledging that the incoming Democratic-controlled Congress could try to limit further action.

Key points

  • Trump told US troops in Thanksgiving calls from Mar-a-Lago that the United States has "victory at hand" in its war on Iran, with the timing his to decide.
  • He said Washington is giving Tehran "every chance to make a deal" to spare further lives, but he doubts Iran's leaders will take it.
  • Trump claimed Iran's air force, navy and air defences had been destroyed, said 150 US missiles were fired to protect shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, and named Pickaxe Mountain as a target for any future strike.
  • Iran's foreign minister said diplomacy continues with Qatar and Pakistan mediating, but Tehran's priority is resilience; there is no ceasefire and fighting continued this week.
