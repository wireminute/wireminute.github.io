Saudi Air Defences Intercept Five Houthi Missiles Aimed at Riyadh and the South

Saudi air defences intercepted five Houthi missiles on Saturday, a cruise missile aimed at Riyadh, three ballistic missiles aimed at the southern region and one aimed at the Red Sea town of Al-Shuqaiq, the Saudi-led coalition said, as civil defence alerts sounded in five areas across the kingdom.

The coalition said in its latest statement on Saturday that its air defences had intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile fired towards Al-Shuqaiq, a town on the Red Sea coast in Jazan province. Debris from the interception came down in the area but caused no civilian casualties, the coalition said. Al-Shuqaiq is home to major desalination and power generation facilities that supply water and electricity to the kingdom’s southern regions.

The Al-Shuqaiq interception was the fifth of the day. Coalition spokesman Major General Turki al-Maliki said earlier on Saturday that Saudi defences had shot down a cruise missile aimed at the Riyadh region, and in separate statements that three ballistic missiles aimed at the southern region had also been destroyed before they could reach their targets. The coalition gave no further details on launch sites or timing.

Civil defence issued seven emergency alerts through the national early warning platform as the missiles came in, covering Khamis Mushait, where two warnings were issued, Abha, Jazan, Al-Kharj in the Riyadh region and the Eastern Province. Each alert was lifted shortly afterwards, the Jazan warning within eight minutes, and residents were urged to keep following safety instructions and to avoid gathering or filming.

Houthi media reported Saudi strikes on Yemen’s capital, Sanaa, and three airstrikes in Mokha district in Taiz province on Saturday, as the two sides traded attacks. The interceptions follow a week of escalating strikes: a Houthi attack on the Jazan refinery on Thursday killed a Nepali worker and injured several others, two ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh were intercepted the same day, and an earlier strike on a tower in Riyadh’s financial district injured 11 people.
