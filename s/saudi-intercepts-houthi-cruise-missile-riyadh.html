Saudi Air Defences Intercept Three Houthi Missiles Aimed at Riyadh and the Southern Region

Saudi air defences intercepted three Houthi missiles on Saturday, a cruise missile aimed at Riyadh and two ballistic missiles aimed at the southern region, the Saudi-led coalition said, as civil defence alerts sounded across three southern areas.

The Saudi-led coalition said in a later statement on Saturday that its air defences had intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched towards the southern region. Coalition spokesman Major General Turki al-Maliki announced the interception, saying both missiles were destroyed before they could reach their targets. Civil defence issued emergency alerts in Khamis Mushait, Jazan province and Abha city, urging residents to stay indoors and move to a safe place, and later announced the danger had passed in all three areas.

The southern interception came hours after Saudi defences shot down a cruise missile aimed at the capital, Riyadh, earlier on Saturday. Al-Maliki said the cruise missile was launched towards the Riyadh area and destroyed before reaching the capital. The coalition gave no further details, and there was no immediate word on damage or casualties from any of the three interceptions. It was the second time in three days that Saudi defences have stopped a missile aimed at the capital.

Houthi media reported Saudi strikes on Yemen's capital, Sanaa, on Saturday, as the two sides traded attacks. The coalition has been bombing Houthi-held areas since the civil war reignited over the summer, while Houthi fighters have been targeting Saudi Arabia almost daily, hitting energy facilities, airports and Riyadh's financial district.

The attacks are the latest in a week of escalating strikes. Saudi defences intercepted two ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh on Thursday, along with a cruise missile in the country's western region and a drone near Jeddah's port. Earlier in the week, a Houthi attack on a tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District injured 11 people, and a strike on the Jazan refinery killed one worker. The coalition said its air defences have now intercepted a string of missiles and drones in recent days as the fighting intensifies.

Key points

  • Saudi air defences intercepted two Houthi ballistic missiles aimed at the southern region on Saturday, hours after shooting down a cruise missile aimed at Riyadh.
  • Coalition spokesman Major General Turki al-Maliki announced the interceptions in separate statements; civil defence alerts in Khamis Mushait, Jazan and Abha were lifted shortly after.
  • Houthi media reported Saudi strikes on Sanaa as the two sides traded attacks on Saturday.
  • The interceptions follow a week of escalating attacks, including two ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh on Thursday, an attack that injured 11 people at a Riyadh financial district tower, and a deadly strike on the Jazan refinery.
