Saudi Air Defences Intercept Houthi Cruise Missile Aimed at Riyadh

Saudi air defences have intercepted and destroyed a cruise missile launched by Yemen's Houthis towards Riyadh, the Saudi-led coalition said.

Coalition spokesman Major General Turki al-Maliki said the missile was aimed at the Riyadh area and was shot down before it could reach the capital. The coalition gave no further details, and there was no immediate word on damage or casualties.

The interception is the latest in a series of Houthi missile and drone attacks on Saudi Arabia this week, as fighting between the two sides intensifies. Saudi defences intercepted two ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh on Thursday, along with a cruise missile in the country's western region and a drone near Jeddah's port. The Houthis have also struck Saudi oil facilities this week, including the Jazan refinery, where one worker was killed.

Key points

  • Saudi air defences intercepted and destroyed a Houthi cruise missile aimed at the Riyadh area, the Saudi-led coalition said.
  • Coalition spokesman Major General Turki al-Maliki gave no further details, and there was no immediate word on damage or casualties.
  • It follows a week of escalating attacks, including two ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh on Thursday and a deadly strike on the Jazan oil refinery.
