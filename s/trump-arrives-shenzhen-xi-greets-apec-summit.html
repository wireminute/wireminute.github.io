Trump Meets Putin and Xi in Shenzhen as Historic Three-Way Summit Gets Under Way

Donald Trump held a separate meeting with Vladimir Putin and then began talks with Xi Jinping in Shenzhen on Thursday, as the first-ever three-way meeting of the leaders of the United States, China and Russia got under way.

Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin held a separate meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday, ahead of the first three-way meeting of the leaders of the United States, China and Russia. Trump called Putin "my friend" as their talks began, saying the two men had known each other for a long time and that "a lot of wonderful things can come out of this or at least, start", according to a recording of the meeting released by the Kremlin. It was their first meeting since their summit in Alaska in August 2025.

Trump then arrived at Kylin Villa, a lakeside resort in Shenzhen, at 1:30 p.m. local time, where he posed for photographs with Xi before the two leaders began their own bilateral meeting, their third this year. The three-way meeting follows the talks between Trump and Xi. Earlier in the day, the three leaders had posed for a group photograph with the other leaders at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

Cutting nuclear arsenals is expected to lead the agenda. The last treaty limiting the nuclear weapons of the United States and Russia expired in February, leaving no binding caps in place between the world's two largest nuclear powers. The war in Ukraine, the conflict involving Iran, the military use of artificial intelligence and global energy stability are also expected to come up. Officials on all sides have cautioned that no agreement is likely, calling the session a first conversation at leader level rather than a negotiation.

It is not certain whether the three leaders will adopt a joint statement. Putin's top adviser said Moscow was working on the assumption that they would, while officials in Beijing and Washington have been more cautious. Xi opened the leaders' meeting on Thursday with a call to reduce barriers to trade and investment. On the sidelines, Japan's prime minister, Sanae Takaichi, spoke with Putin in her first face-to-face talks with the Russian president since taking office.

The meetings cap two days of carefully staged encounters in Shenzhen. On Wednesday evening, Xi broke with protocol to greet Trump on the tarmac as he stepped off Air Force One, returning the airport welcome Trump gave Xi in Washington in September. At the welcome banquet, Xi seated Trump beside him at the head table as musicians played "America the Beautiful", and Putin told reporters he expected Thursday's three-way meeting to deliver a big potential benefit.

Key points

  • Trump and Putin held a separate meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday, with Trump calling Putin "my friend" and saying good things could come out of their talks.
  • Trump then began a bilateral meeting with Xi at Kylin Villa, with the first-ever three-way summit of the three leaders to follow.
  • Nuclear arms cuts lead the agenda after the last US-Russia treaty expired in February, with Ukraine, Iran and military AI also expected to come up.
  • A joint statement is not certain, though Moscow says it is working on the assumption that the three leaders will adopt one.
