Iran Says It Brought Down a US Reaper Drone Near the Strait of Hormuz

Iran's army says it brought down an American MQ-9 Reaper drone near the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday, in the latest escalation of the nine-month conflict between the United States and Iran.

In a statement carried by state media, the army said the aircraft was detected flying east of the strait and destroyed by air-defence systems built in Iran. The army said the interception was carried out by units in the country's southeast, and it released a short video it said showed the drone being hit. The United States military has not commented on the claim.

The announcement came a day after the Revolutionary Guards said they had brought down a smaller American MQ-1 drone south of Qeshm Island, and put the number of MQ-1 drones they say they have downed at 18. It also followed American strikes on a missile site on Qeshm Island on Tuesday, and Iranian drone attacks on American bases in Bahrain and Kuwait earlier on Wednesday.

Iran also said its drones had targeted American Patriot air-defence systems and missile launch platforms at Ali al-Salem Air Base in Kuwait early on Wednesday. Kuwait said its air defences were responding to hostile drone attacks, and its foreign ministry condemned what it called repeated criminal Iranian attacks on the country. Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the United Arab Emirates also condemned the attacks on Kuwait and Bahrain, and the Gulf Cooperation Council's secretary-general condemned the latest drone attack on Kuwait.

The army chief, Major-General Amir Hatami, warned that any renewed attack on Iran would draw a more forceful response, as the United States moved a third aircraft carrier group into the region. The US military has not commented on the drone claim, and the claim itself has not been independently verified.

Key points

  • Iran's army says it brought down an American MQ-9 Reaper drone near the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday.
  • The US military has not commented on the claim; Iran released a short video it said showed the interception.
  • The claim follows US strikes on a missile site on Qeshm Island and Iranian drone attacks on US bases in Bahrain and Kuwait.
  • Iran also said its drones targeted US Patriot systems at Ali al-Salem Air Base in Kuwait; Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the UAE condemned the attacks.
