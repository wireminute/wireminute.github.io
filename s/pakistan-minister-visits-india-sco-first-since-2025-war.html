Pakistan Minister Arrives in India in First Visit Since Last Year's Four-Day War

A Pakistani minister has arrived in New Delhi for the first time since Pakistan and India fought a four-day war last year, in the highest-level diplomatic contact between the nuclear-armed neighbours since the conflict.

Climate Change Minister Musadik Masood Malik landed in the Indian capital on Friday to represent Pakistan at the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation's Civilisational Dialogue, a three-day gathering running from November 20 to 22 at the historic Purana Qila fort. Saad Warraich, Pakistan's charge d'affaires in New Delhi, met him at the airport.

No Pakistani federal minister has set foot in India since Bilawal Bhutto Zardari, then the foreign minister, attended an SCO gathering in Goa in May 2023. It is also the first such visit since the two countries fought a brief but intense four-day conflict in May 2025, after an attack on tourists in Indian-administered Kashmir in April, a confrontation that ended with a ceasefire. Only two Pakistani ministers have made the trip in the past ten years, making Malik the third.

The dialogue is the first of its kind for the SCO, bringing together representatives from member states to discuss civilisational dialogue and cultural understanding. The gathering was proposed by Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi at the bloc's meeting in China last year, and India is hosting the opening event at Purana Qila. Representatives from member states are expected to exchange views on shared heritage and cultural ties over the three days.

The visit comes against a backdrop of continued tension. It follows by two days the killing of Lateef Bhat, a commander of the Lashkar-e-Taiba group, by Indian security forces, and relations between the two countries have remained strained since last year's fighting. The trip is being watched closely as the first test of whether the two sides can resume even limited diplomatic contact. There has been no ministerial visit between the two countries since 2023, and the trip is the first sign of movement since the ceasefire.

Key points

  • Malik reached New Delhi on Friday for the SCO Civilisational Dialogue, running November 20 to 22 at Purana Qila.
  • No Pakistani minister has visited India since May 2023, and none since the two countries fought a four-day conflict in May 2025.
  • Only two Pakistani ministers have visited India in the past ten years, making this the highest-level contact since last year's fighting.
  • The inaugural SCO dialogue was proposed by India's prime minister at last year's bloc meeting in China and brings member states together to discuss cultural understanding.
