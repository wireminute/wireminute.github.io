Elderly French Parents Jailed 39 Years After Four-Year-Old Daughter's Death

A French court has jailed an elderly couple over the killing of their four-year-old daughter after prolonged abuse, closing one of France's oldest unsolved cases 39 years after the child's body was found beside a motorway.

A French court has sentenced an elderly couple to prison over the killing of their four-year-old daughter, closing one of the country's longest-running unsolved cases almost four decades after the child's body was found beside a motorway. The Loir-et-Cher assize court in Blois handed Halima El Bakhti, 72, a 20-year sentence on Tuesday, while her husband Ahmed Touloub, 74, received 15 years for his part in the crime.

The court found El Bakhti guilty of torturing her daughter and of cruelty that ended in the child's death, though it found she had not intended to kill her. Touloub was convicted as an accomplice after jurors found he had known about the abuse and done nothing to protect the child. The pair, who entered court as free people, were taken into custody as soon as the verdict was read out.

The victim, Inass Touloub, was found dead on 11 August 1987 beside the A10 motorway near Suevres in central France. She remained unidentified for more than 30 years until DNA testing in 2018 matched her with a sibling whose sample had been taken in an unrelated case, allowing investigators to trace her parents. The trial, which opened on 2 November, lasted 11 days and ended after several hours of deliberation by three judges and six jurors.

The court accepted that El Bakhti's judgement was impaired at the time of the killing, which capped the sentence she could receive at 20 years under the law in force in 1987. Prosecutors had asked for 20 years for the mother and 14 for the father, so Touloub's sentence was one year longer than requested. His lawyers said they would appeal, and the couple have 10 days to lodge a challenge. The case, known in France as that of the little martyr of the A10, had haunted investigators since the child's body was discovered in the summer of 1987.

Key points

  • A French court has jailed Halima El Bakhti, 72, for 20 years and Ahmed Touloub, 74, for 15 years over the abuse and killing of their four-year-old daughter in 1987.
  • Inass Touloub's body was found by the A10 motorway near Suevres in August 1987; she was not identified until DNA testing in 2018.
  • The mother was convicted of torturing her daughter, with cruelty that caused her death; the father was convicted as an accomplice for failing to protect the child.
  • The father's lawyers say they will appeal; the couple have 10 days to challenge the verdict.
