Eleven Killed as Fire Tears Through Shophouse in Phuket's Patong

Eleven people have been killed and 16 injured after a fire tore through a commercial shophouse in the Patong resort area of Phuket, Thailand, in the early hours of Monday, police said. Investigators say an electrical transformer near the building exploded shortly before the blaze, and arson has been ruled out.

The fire started at about 3:17am local time on Monday in a four-storey shophouse on Phang Muang Sai Kor Road that housed a vehicle rental business, a restaurant and rented accommodation, and spread to neighbouring buildings in the town area near Patong beach on Phuket's western coast. Firefighters took around two hours to bring the blaze under control, and 11 people were found dead at the scene.

Police in Patong said an initial investigation found that an electrical transformer near the building had exploded shortly before the fire started, and arson has been ruled out. The exact cause is still under investigation, with a suspected electrical short circuit the leading line of inquiry. The provincial governor said a preliminary assessment pointed to a short circuit at a utility pole in front of the shop during a storm.

The building's ground floor held about 100 motorcycles, along with stored fuel, engine oil, plastic materials and tyres, which officials said would have helped the fire and smoke spread quickly. Victims were found on the upper floors, and most are believed to have died from smoke inhalation rather than burns. The bodies were taken to hospital for autopsy and formal identification.

Those hurt included 15 Thai nationals and one person from Myanmar, police said, and none of the dead or injured was a tourist. Sixteen people were treated in hospital for smoke inhalation and injuries from broken glass. Some Thai outlets reported a higher injury figure, of 36 or more, but police put the figure at 16 and no final total has been confirmed. Five four-storey commercial units were damaged, and police said they were examining CCTV footage and interviewing witnesses.
