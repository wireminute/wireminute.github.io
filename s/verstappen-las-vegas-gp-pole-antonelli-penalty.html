Verstappen Inherits Las Vegas Grand Prix Pole as Antonelli's Penalty Drops Him to Sixth

Max Verstappen will start the Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole position after the fastest qualifier, newly crowned world champion Kimi Antonelli, was handed a five-place grid penalty for an engine change.

Antonelli set the pace in the final phase of qualifying with a lap of 1 minute 33.893 seconds, finishing 0.258 seconds clear of Verstappen's best effort of 1:34.151. But the Mercedes driver will start the race from sixth on the grid, because his team fitted a new internal combustion engine beyond the season's allocation, triggering an automatic five-place drop.

Verstappen, a four-time world champion, has won the Las Vegas race in two of its three runnings but had never started it from pole position. He will share the front row with George Russell, whose third-fastest time of 1:34.224 becomes second on the grid once Antonelli's penalty is applied.

Verstappen had earlier been called before the stewards over incidents with Russell and Franco Colapinto during practice, but no punishment was imposed. He said the slipstream down the back straight had been crucial to his lap and called the outcome unexpected, after starting his final flying lap almost a second behind the car in front.

Lando Norris was fourth fastest in 1:34.244 and Isack Hadjar fifth, while both Ferraris failed to reach the final phase of qualifying, with Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton missing the cut. Several other drivers also carry penalties into the race, including Oscar Piastri with a ten-place drop for an engine change.

The race is scheduled to start at 20:00 local time on Saturday under lights on the Las Vegas Strip circuit, with Verstappen chasing a third win at a venue where he has never previously led the field away.

Key points

  • Kimi Antonelli set the fastest qualifying time in Las Vegas, a 1:33.893, but a five-place grid penalty for an engine change drops him to sixth.
  • Max Verstappen inherits pole position, his first at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, and will share the front row with George Russell.
  • Verstappen escaped punishment after being summoned by the stewards over practice incidents with Russell and Franco Colapinto.
  • Lando Norris and Isack Hadjar complete the top five on the grid, while both Ferraris failed to reach the final phase of qualifying.
