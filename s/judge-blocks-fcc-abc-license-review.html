US Judge Blocks FCC Early Review of ABC Station Licenses

A federal judge in Washington has barred the Federal Communications Commission from carrying out an early review of the licenses of eight Disney-owned ABC television stations, ruling that the move looked like retaliation against the network for programming the Trump administration disliked.

US District Judge Loren AliKhan granted Disney and ABC a preliminary injunction on Wednesday, and also turned down the regulator's request to throw out the lawsuit. The order stops the FCC from holding a hearing on the stations' licenses, a step that could have led to the licenses being revoked and the stations forced off the air.

The FCC ordered the eight stations to apply for license renewal years ahead of schedule in a letter dated 28 April, one day after President Donald Trump criticized remarks by ABC late-night host Jimmy Kimmel. Disney and ABC sued the regulator in August, arguing that the review was punishment for broadcast content. The judge wrote that the network was likely to succeed on its First Amendment claim, saying FCC Chairman Brendan Carr "has taken President Trump's rhetoric as his marching orders" and adding: "This is not a close case."

The FCC has said it launched the review to determine whether Disney's diversity, equity and inclusion initiatives violated the agency's ban on unlawful discrimination. A spokesperson for the regulator said the decision was "wrong on the facts and wrong on the law" and that the agency looks forward to prevailing in full.

The ruling does not stop the FCC from continuing its investigations into Disney's employment practices or the talk show "The View." The judge also declined ABC's request for a broader order barring the regulator from threatening the company over its editorial decisions, writing: "No one doubts that the Commission may enforce the law in the manner it sees fit. But in doing so, defendants may not retaliate against or punish plaintiffs and other broadcast license holders for disfavored speech."

Key points

  • Judge Loren AliKhan granted Disney and ABC a preliminary injunction on Wednesday, halting the FCC's early review of eight ABC station licenses.
  • The judge found ABC was likely to prove the review was retaliation for programming critical of President Trump, writing: "This is not a close case."
  • The FCC ordered the early renewals on 28 April, a day after Trump criticized remarks by ABC host Jimmy Kimmel; the licenses were not due for renewal until 2028 at the earliest.
  • The ruling does not stop FCC inquiries into Disney's employment practices or "The View", and the regulator says it will keep fighting the case.
