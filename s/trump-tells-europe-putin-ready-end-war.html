Zelensky Calls Putin a Killer as Trump Defends Calling Him a Friend

Volodymyr Zelensky has called Vladimir Putin a killer after Donald Trump described the Russian leader as his friend, warning that such warm words only encourage Moscow to keep attacking Ukraine.

In an interview broadcast on Friday, Zelensky said that for Ukraine Putin is an enemy and a killer, and that most people around the world would agree because he keeps killing civilians. He warned that speaking of Putin as a friend takes away the pressure on him, leaving him feeling free to continue the war. He added that Ukraine is ready to make difficult compromises for a ceasefire, but that Putin keeps putting new demands on Kyiv.

Trump defended the relationship at the White House on Friday, saying he has fantastic relationships with Putin and with the Chinese leader Xi Jinping, and that America is safe because of it. Asked whether Putin was a friend to the United States, he said he could not speak for America, but that his close relationship with the Russian leader gives Washington a lot of additional strength. He insisted again that Putin wants the war to end.

The exchange followed a meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday, the first three-way summit of its kind, where Trump greeted Putin warmly and called him his friend. Later that day Trump told European leaders by phone that Putin is tired of the war and ready for a deal, urging them to seize the opening. The Europeans reacted cautiously: Poland's prime minister said Trump sounded fairly optimistic but his words were received very carefully, and officials said they want proof before trusting the Russian leader's intentions.

Hours after that call, Russia launched a massive overnight barrage on Ukraine, firing 84 cruise and ballistic missiles and more than 400 drones at Kyiv and nine other regions, killing eight people. Zelensky called it the biggest attack this year. Missiles strayed into Polish airspace during the assault, and Polish and NATO jets were scrambled, with air defence batteries activated around Rzeszow and Swidnik.

Key points

  • Zelensky told CNN that Putin is an enemy and a killer, and warned that calling him a friend removes the pressure on him to stop.
  • Trump defended the friendship at the White House on Friday, saying his relationships with Putin and Xi keep America safe.
  • Trump had told European leaders that Putin is tired and ready for a deal; they reacted cautiously and want proof before trusting him.
  • Hours later Russia launched a massive barrage on Kyiv, its biggest this year, killing eight people, with missiles straying into Polish airspace.
