Trump Tells Europe Putin Is 'Tired' and Ready for a Ukraine Deal

Donald Trump told European leaders on Thursday that Vladimir Putin is tired of the war in Ukraine and ready to make a deal, urging them to seize the opening even as Russia launched a massive overnight barrage on Kyiv hours later.

Trump joined a meeting of the Coalition of the Willing in Berlin by phone, speaking to Ukraine's president, Britain's prime minister and other European leaders alongside the American envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner. According to European officials briefed on the call, his main message was that Putin "is tired, wants to end this, ready for a deal", and that Europe should reach out and grasp the opportunity.

The reaction in the room was cautious. Poland's prime minister, who took part in the call, said Trump sounded fairly optimistic but that his words were received very cautiously. One senior European official said the Europeans were constructive in response but want proof before trusting the Russian leader's intentions. Trump also urged the leaders to reach a deal so they could stop spending money on Ukraine and spend it on their own countries, the officials said.

Kushner, writing online after the meeting, said it was up to the president to reveal what he had said, adding that Trump wants to make a deal with Europe by his side and that any agreement would be reached in close consultation with Ukraine and Europe. The call came hours after Trump met Putin and Xi Jinping in Shenzhen for the first three-way summit of its kind, where Trump warmly called the Russian leader his friend. The joint declaration the three issued afterwards made no mention of Ukraine, and the talks produced no breakthrough on ending the war.

Hours after the call, Russia launched a massive overnight barrage on Ukraine, firing 84 cruise and ballistic missiles and more than 400 drones at Kyiv and nine other regions, killing eight people. Missiles strayed into Polish airspace during the attack, and Polish and NATO jets were scrambled in response, with air defence batteries activated around Rzeszow and Swidnik. The assault came hours after Putin left China following the summit.
