US Forces Respond After Suspected Drone Spotted Over RAF Lakenheath

American forces at RAF Lakenheath in eastern England responded on Friday evening to suspected unauthorised drone activity over the base, with personnel told to take shelter as sirens sounded — though the military said no one was hurt and nothing was damaged.

The alarm went up at about half past five in the evening, people living near the base in Suffolk said, with sirens sounding and a loudspeaker announcement telling people to stay indoors and take shelter. American security forces moved to deal with the reports, and the base said later that the situation was resolved.

A US Air Force spokesperson said its security forces had responded to suspected unauthorised drone activity over RAF Lakenheath, and that no personnel, aircraft or infrastructure were affected. Britain's defence ministry said the same, adding that it was working with the Americans to establish the facts.

The scare came days after the United States moved aircraft away from two other English bases over a threat linked to Iran, and after charges were brought this week against three men accused of plotting at RAF Fairford. Drones were also seen over Lakenheath and nearby Mildenhall in November 2024.

Key points

  • US Air Force security forces responded to suspected unauthorised drone activity over RAF Lakenheath on Friday evening; no personnel, aircraft or infrastructure were affected.
  • Sirens sounded and personnel were told to shelter in place; Britain's defence ministry says the situation is now resolved and there is no threat to the public.
  • The scare follows the US moving aircraft from other English bases over an Iran-linked threat, and charges this week against three men accused of plotting at RAF Fairford.
