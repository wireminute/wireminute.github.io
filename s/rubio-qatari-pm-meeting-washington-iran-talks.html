Rubio to Meet Qatari PM in Washington as Iran Warns of Bid to Stir Unrest

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio is due to meet Qatar's prime minister in Washington on Monday as mediation continues over the war with Iran, while Tehran warns its enemies are trying to stir unrest at home.

The State Department said the meeting with Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani is scheduled for 2pm in Washington (19:00 GMT), and Qatar's prime minister is also expected to meet other US officials. Qatar has been mediating between Washington and Tehran, and the prime minister met President Donald Trump at the White House earlier this month. Rubio said on Sunday there had been little progress in the talks and rejected Tehran's terms.

In Tehran, the deputy commander of the Revolutionary Guards, Major-General Mostafa Izadi, warned that Iran's enemies were trying to unsettle the country by squeezing its economy and shaking public trust, according to state news agency IRNA. He said the Guards would expand their grassroots Basij network. Iran's president has described the US sanctions and naval blockade as a silent economic war, and Tehran has said its two-day proposal for a pause in hostilities still stands.

Key points

  • Rubio is due to meet Qatar's prime minister in Washington on Monday, with the State Department putting the meeting at 2pm.
  • Qatar is mediating between the US and Iran, and Rubio said on Sunday there had been little progress in the talks.
  • Iran's Revolutionary Guards warned that enemies are trying to stir unrest through economic pressure, and said the Basij network would be expanded.
