Five Killed as Turkish Military Helicopter Crashes During Training Flight

Five Turkish Air Force crew members were killed on Tuesday evening when their helicopter crashed during a training flight in western Turkiye, the defence ministry said.

The AS-532 Cougar helicopter went down after taking off from Eskisehir for a training flight on Tuesday evening, the defence ministry said in a statement. Eskisehir, where the air force's 1st Main Jet Base Command is based, lies between Istanbul and Ankara. Emergency teams reached the crash site near Yorukakcayir, a village roughly 15 kilometres west of the city centre, where television pictures showed rescue vehicles gathered in an open plain near the Tepebasi district.

The ministry first said only that the helicopter had crashed, then later confirmed that all five people on board had been killed, describing the dead as heroic comrades-in-arms. The statement confirming the deaths came hours after the aircraft went down. The ministry did not say what caused the crash, and no further details about the flight or the crew were released.

It is the second fatal Turkish military helicopter crash in less than two weeks. On November 12, an S-70 helicopter went down in the east of the country, near Malatya, killing four soldiers, for reasons that also remain unknown, and no cause has been given for either crash. The two accidents, 12 days apart, have put the spotlight on the safety of the military's helicopter fleet, which flies training and transport missions across the country.

Vice President Cevdet Yilmaz offered condolences to the families of the dead and to the armed forces. The AS-532 Cougar is a French-made twin-engine helicopter used by militaries around the world for transport, search and rescue, and training, and Turkiye has flown the type for years.

Key points

  • An AS-532 Cougar helicopter crashed during a training flight after taking off from Eskisehir on Tuesday evening
  • All five crew members on board were killed, the defence ministry confirmed; the cause has not been given
  • The crash site is near Yorukakcayir village, roughly 15 kilometres west of the city of Eskisehir
  • It is the second fatal Turkish military helicopter crash in 12 days, after an S-70 crash on November 12 killed four soldiers
