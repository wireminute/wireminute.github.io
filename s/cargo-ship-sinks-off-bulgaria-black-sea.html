Wheat Ship Sinks Off Bulgaria After Crew Reports Sea-Drone Strikes

A cargo ship carrying 3,000 tonnes of wheat has sunk in the Black Sea off Bulgaria after its crew reported being hit by two sea drones, in the latest attack on merchant shipping in the region. All 12 crew members were rescued.

The Somali-flagged Betul 8 sent a distress call at about 9.15am local time on Monday, saying the vessel was sinking about 40 nautical miles southeast of Cape Kaliakra, inside Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone. The ship was sailing from the Romanian port of Tulcea to Egypt with a cargo of bulk wheat.

Bulgaria's transport ministry said a rescue operation involving four ships, two from Bulgaria and one each from Romania and Turkiye, brought the crew ashore, and a Bulgarian helicopter flew them to land. The crew, eight Turkish citizens and four Azerbaijani nationals, were in good condition, and the ministry said there had been no fatalities and no reports of environmental damage.

Speaking to journalists after reaching the port city of Varna, crew members said their ship had been struck by two sea drones, and that they had abandoned the vessel in two life rafts before it capsized. Bulgarian officials have not formally confirmed what caused the sinking, and no one has claimed responsibility for the reported strikes.

Maritime security firm Diaplous said the Betul 8 was the 17th vessel attacked off the coasts of Bulgaria and Romania since mid-July. The sinking came two days after three ships were hit by drones off Romania near the Sulina canal, in strikes that caused fires on board but no casualties, and a fourth vessel was hit late on Friday near the Romanian coast.

The attacks have put merchant shipping in the western Black Sea under growing strain, with the Sulina canal a main export route for Ukraine. Romania has scrambled fighter jets in response to the strikes and asked NATO for help with surveillance aircraft and drone defences, while investigators work to establish who is behind the campaign against commercial vessels.

Key points

  • The Somali-flagged Betul 8, carrying 3,000 tonnes of wheat from Romania to Egypt, sank about 40 nautical miles southeast of Cape Kaliakra after sending a distress call on Monday morning.
  • All 12 crew members, eight Turkish citizens and four Azerbaijani nationals, were rescued in an operation involving four ships and a Bulgarian helicopter, and were in good condition.
  • Crew members said the ship was hit by two sea drones, but Bulgarian officials have not formally confirmed the cause and no one has claimed responsibility.
  • Maritime security firm Diaplous said the Betul 8 was the 17th vessel attacked off the coasts of Bulgaria and Romania since mid-July, following three drone strikes off Romania over the weekend.
