Migrant Boat Capsizes Off Senegal's Yenne Coast, Leaving at Least One Dead

A small migrant boat has capsized off the coast of Yenne in western Senegal, killing at least one person, local officials said, in the second deadly shipwreck in Senegalese waters in ten days.

The pirogue, a wooden fishing boat of the kind used for the Atlantic crossing, hit rocks and washed ashore overnight from Tuesday to Wednesday, Yenne Mayor Massamba Mbengue told the AFP news agency. The incident happened at around 2 a.m. local time. Mbengue and local firefighters said one body had been recovered, while two other local sources put the toll at two. A survivor being questioned by the gendarmerie said about 100 people were on board, including five women, although other estimates put the number at between 50 and nearly 100. A search is under way for more bodies.

The wreck comes ten days after 43 people died when a boat went down off the north-western town of Thieppe. The Atlantic crossing from West Africa to Spain's Canary Islands is one of the world's deadliest migration routes, and Senegal has seen a sharp rise in departures this month.

Key points

  • A pirogue carrying migrants hit rocks off Yenne, western Senegal, overnight and capsized
  • At least one person is dead, with two other sources reporting two; a survivor said about 100 people were aboard
  • The wreck follows a shipwreck off Thieppe ten days ago that killed 43 people
