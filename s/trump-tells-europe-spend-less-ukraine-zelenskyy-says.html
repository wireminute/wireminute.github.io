Trump Told Europe Its Ukraine Money Could Stay Home After Peace, Zelenskyy Says

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Saturday that US President Donald Trump told European leaders this week they were spending heavily on the war in Ukraine and could keep that money for their own countries once a peace deal is reached.

Trump called into a meeting of Ukraine's Coalition of the Willing allies in Berlin on Thursday, a day after meeting Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping at an APEC summit in Shenzhen. Zelenskyy, who attended the Berlin meeting, told reporters in Kyiv that Trump raised the cost of backing Ukraine. "He said that Europe is spending a lot of money, and they could spend it on themselves after the end (of the war)," Zelenskyy said. "It didn't sound like a threat."

The remarks came before Zelenskyy's talks in Kyiv with British Defence Secretary Wes Streeting. Trump said on his return to Washington on Friday that Putin had clearly expressed a desire to end the war through diplomacy. Zelenskyy said he was more sceptical than the Americans that Putin was ready for compromises, and noted that Putin had shown no sign of dropping his demand that Ukraine give up all of its eastern Donbas region, which Zelenskyy has rejected.

Zelenskyy welcomed recent announcements by US envoys that Europe would be included as a full partner in the peace process, which resumed after a nine-month break with talks in Abu Dhabi this month. He repeated that he was ready to attend the Group of 20 summit in Miami in December, which Trump is due to host, and would be glad to hold direct talks with Putin there.

"The best response, and the response that I would appeal to all of our allies to undertake, is to do more to support Ukraine," Zelenskyy said. The exchange came a day after Trump defended calling Putin a friend, a description Zelenskyy rejected, and as Moscow continued to resist NATO-style security guarantees for Kyiv.

Key points

  • Zelenskyy said Trump told European leaders in Berlin they spend a lot on Ukraine and could spend the money at home once a peace deal is reached.
  • Trump called into the Coalition of the Willing meeting on Thursday after meeting Putin and Xi in Shenzhen; Zelenskyy said the remark "didn't sound like a threat".
  • Zelenskyy said he is more sceptical than the Americans that Putin is ready for compromises, and Putin still demands all of Donbas.
  • Zelenskyy welcomed Europe being included as a full partner in the peace process and said he is ready to meet Putin at the G20 in Miami in December.
