Cyclone Akvan to Strike Near Gopalpur at Noon as 300,000 Evacuated in Odisha

Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan is due to cross the coasts of north Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha close to Gopalpur around noon on Friday, after Odisha moved about 300,000 people to safety and deployed more than 400 rescue teams.

The weather office said the storm would cross the north Andhra Pradesh, Yanam and south Odisha coasts between Narsapur and Puri, close to Gopalpur in Odisha, by noon on Friday, November 20. It is expected to arrive as a severe cyclonic storm, with sustained winds of 90 to 100 kilometres per hour and gusts reaching 110, weaker than the very severe storm forecast earlier in the week.

Odisha has shifted close to 300,000 people to safer places, the largest number in Ganjam district, where about 138,000 people were moved, followed by about 22,000 in Gajapati and 13,000 in Kandhamal. More than 400 rescue teams are in place across the vulnerable districts, along with about 9,300 cyclone shelters, and schools and colleges have been shut in 11 districts. No deaths had been reported by early Friday.

Heavy to very heavy rain is forecast along the coasts of Odisha and north Andhra Pradesh, with extremely heavy falls in places, and a storm surge of half a metre to one metre is expected to flood low-lying areas of the Ganjam and Puri coasts. Red alerts are in force for Srikakulam and Vizianagaram districts in Andhra Pradesh, and fishermen have been told to stay ashore until November 21.

Railways have cancelled 69 trains, most of them running to or through the temple town of Puri, and rain has already begun over coastal and southern Odisha. The storm lay about 410 kilometres south of Gopalpur on Thursday evening and was moving north-northwest at about 13 kilometres per hour. Officials say the track has shifted north from the Andhra coast, where the first forecasts had placed landfall, and the crossing point is now fixed near Gopalpur.

Key points

  • Cyclone Akvan is due to cross the north Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha coasts between Narsapur and Puri, close to Gopalpur, around noon on Friday, November 20.
  • The storm is expected to arrive with sustained winds of 90 to 100 km/h and gusts up to 110 km/h, weaker than the earlier very severe forecast.
  • About 300,000 people have been evacuated in Odisha, with 423 rescue teams deployed and 69 trains cancelled; no deaths had been reported by early Friday.
  • Heavy to very heavy rain and a storm surge of 0.5 to 1 metre are expected along the Ganjam and Puri coasts, with fishermen told to stay ashore until November 21.
