Cyclone Akvan to Hit Andhra Coast on Friday as Forecasters Fix Landfall Between Nellore and Tuni

Forecasters have fixed Cyclone Akvan's landfall on the Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Tuni around noon on Friday, with the storm expected to arrive as a very severe cyclone packing winds of up to 145 kilometres per hour.

The deep depression over the Bay of Bengal is strengthening on schedule and will become Cyclone Akvan by Monday evening, the weather office said in its latest bulletin. The system was centred about 700 kilometres east-southeast of Chennai on Monday morning, moving north-northwest at 6 to 8 kilometres per hour. It is expected to become a severe cyclonic storm by Tuesday evening and a very severe one by the night of Thursday, November 19.

The weather office now expects the storm to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts between Nellore and Tuni by noon on Friday, November 20, as a very severe cyclonic storm, with sustained winds of 120 to 130 kilometres per hour and gusts reaching 145. That fixes a landfall point that forecasters had left open a day earlier, when only the general direction towards Andhra Pradesh was clear.

A pre-cyclone watch is in effect along the Andhra Pradesh and adjoining south Odisha coasts. Heavy to very heavy rain is expected along coastal Andhra Pradesh on November 19 and 20, with isolated extremely heavy falls, and heavy rain is also forecast in Tamil Nadu, Odisha and West Bengal. Fishermen have been told to stay ashore, and offshore activity along the coast has been paused.

The landfall point is not yet certain. Some private forecasters have suggested the storm could track further south and come ashore along the Tamil Nadu coast, between Chennai and Cuddalore, while the official forecast holds to Andhra Pradesh. Forecasters say a more reliable fix on the landfall point should be possible by Tuesday, about 48 hours before the storm is due ashore. Akvan would be the region's third cyclone in three weeks.

Key points

  • Cyclone Akvan is expected to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts between Nellore and Tuni by noon on Friday, November 20.
  • The storm is forecast to arrive as a very severe cyclone, with sustained winds of 120 to 130 km/h and gusts up to 145 km/h.
  • It becomes a cyclonic storm on Monday evening, a severe storm on Tuesday, and a very severe storm by the night of November 19.
  • A pre-cyclone watch covers the Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha coasts, with heavy to very heavy rain expected on November 19 and 20; the exact landfall point could still shift.
