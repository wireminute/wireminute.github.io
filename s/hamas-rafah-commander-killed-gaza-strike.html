Hamas Rafah Commander Killed in Overnight Israeli Strike in Southern Gaza

Hamas has confirmed that Samir Abu Younis, the commander of its Rafah Brigade, was killed in an Israeli air strike overnight in southern Gaza, a killing the Israeli military also confirmed on Sunday morning.

Abu Younis, 53, was killed in the al-Mawasi area west of Khan Younis, where medics said the strike hit him as he walked down a street. Two other people were wounded in the attack, and his funeral is due to be held in Khan Younis on Sunday afternoon.

Hamas said Abu Younis joined its armed wing in 1991 and was one of the commanders who planned the October 7, 2023 attack on Israel. He had led the Rafah Brigade for only about two months, taking over after his predecessor, Nael Abu Obeid, was killed in September.

The strike came two days after an Israeli attack on the Nasser Hospital complex in Khan Younis, and as both sides accuse each other of breaking the ceasefire that halted major fighting in October 2025. Gaza's health ministry said 16 people were killed and 68 injured in Israeli attacks over the past 48 hours.
