Melbourne Cup Horse So You Are Found in Sydney Skip Bin, Vet Hospital Apologises

The body of Melbourne Cup runner So You Are was found in a skip bin at a waste facility in Sydney, less than two weeks after the five-year-old finished eighth in Australia's most famous race, prompting an apology from the veterinary hospital that treated him and an investigation by racing stewards.

Footage posted on social media on Monday afternoon showed the horse lying in a skip at the Cleanaway Lucas Heights Resource Recovery Centre in southern Sydney. Trainer Bjorn Baker confirmed on Tuesday that the horse in the video was So You Are, saying he was deeply distressed by what he had seen. The video was filmed by a construction worker who came across the carcass among general waste at the site.

So You Are had been euthanised on Monday after a severe bout of colic. He fell ill at a spelling farm after the Cup and was rushed to REC Equine Specialists at Horsley Park in Sydney's west, where surgeons found he could not be saved and put him down under anaesthesia. Baker had announced the death on Monday. The five-year-old, a son of So You Think bred and raced by Stuart Ramsey's Ramsey Pastoral, had won the Bendigo Cup a week before the Melbourne Cup and finished eighth at Flemington on 3 November behind Point Of Law, ridden by Rachel King.

The veterinary hospital apologised on Tuesday, saying the horse had been collected by its contracted service provider and taken to a licensed landfill, where he should have been promptly and respectfully buried. Its managing partner, Dr Jonathan Lumsden, said what happened was unacceptable and disrespectful to So You Are and to everyone who cared for him. The hospital said it had stopped using the contractor and was looking into what happened.

Racing NSW said its preliminary investigation found the horse was being held temporarily while awaiting burial when the footage was recorded, and called the way his body was left distressing and completely unacceptable. Its investigation is continuing. Cleanaway, which runs the Lucas Heights site, said the facility is licensed to accept animal carcasses and that the body had arrived in a small tipper truck that is not allowed onto the landfill tipping face, so it was moved into a skip bin for safe disposal.

Key points

  • The body of Melbourne Cup runner So You Are was found in a skip bin at a waste facility in Sydney, days after he finished eighth in the race on 3 November
  • The five-year-old was euthanised on Monday after a severe bout of colic, having been rushed to an equine hospital for emergency surgery that could not save him
  • The veterinary hospital apologised, saying the horse should have been promptly and respectfully buried, and has stopped using the contractor that collected him
  • Racing NSW called the handling of the body distressing and completely unacceptable and is investigating; the waste company said the site is licensed for animal carcasses
