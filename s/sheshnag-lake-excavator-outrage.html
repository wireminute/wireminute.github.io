Excavator Video Near Sacred Sheshnag Lake Sparks Outrage in Kashmir

A video showing an excavator cutting and levelling ground beside Sheshnag Lake, a sacred high-altitude glacial lake in Kashmir, has triggered outrage from politicians, environmentalists and religious leaders, who are demanding an immediate halt to the work.

The 28-second clip, first shared online by an operator and later deleted, shows the machine digging and dumping soil near the lake, which sits at 3,590 metres on the Pahalgam route to the Amarnath cave shrine. Sheshnag is listed among 14 high-risk glacial lakes in the region and feeds the Lidder River. Critics warn the fragile terrain cannot take heavy machinery.

The Border Roads Organisation, which is carrying out the work, rejects claims of new construction and says it is only improving existing tracks after the pilgrimage season, with safety and environmental safeguards in place. Some leaders have questioned whether the video is authentic and want an investigation. The story is still developing.

Key points

  • The Border Roads Organisation says it is only improving existing tracks after the pilgrimage season, with safety and environmental safeguards in place.
