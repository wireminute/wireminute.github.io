Megaspeed Says Alice Huang Left the Company in August 2025

Singapore-based cloud company Megaspeed said on Sunday that Alice Huang left the firm on 30 August 2025 and holds no position there, as scrutiny grows over its purchases of advanced Nvidia chips in the scandal that forced Nvidia founder Jensen Huang to resign last week.

In a statement on Sunday, the company said it had hired outside lawyers to examine media reports about its business, its supply chain and its global operations, saying the claims lacked verifiable evidence. It said it conducts legitimate and lawful business and declined to comment on her personal affairs.

Corporate records show she took a controlling stake in Megaspeed in March 2023 and served as a director until November that year. Trade records put its purchases of Nvidia chips and equipment above $4 billion since then, and it is under investigation in the United States over whether it helped customers in China obtain advanced chips covered by American export controls. Singapore's home affairs ministry has said there was not enough evidence that she had committed related offences, but she was barred from returning after giving inaccurate information that understated her role at the company.

The statement comes days after Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's chief executive on 12 November, after admitting to an affair. He co-founded the chipmaker in 1993 and led it for 33 years, turning it into the dominant supplier of the processors behind advanced artificial intelligence. Congress has summoned him for closed-door testimony about the relationship. American intelligence officials believe Alice Huang is a Chinese intelligence asset, a claim Beijing has called a groundless attempt to smear China, and the Justice Department has said it has not reached a conclusion on the question.

On 8 November the board opened its own inquiry into whether its former chief had been the target of a foreign intelligence operation. Megaspeed's chief executive said last week that the company had cooperated with Commerce Department inspections and hired an American law firm to verify its export-control compliance. Investigations into the company and six other people are still under way.

Key points

  • Megaspeed says Alice Huang officially left on 30 August 2025 and holds no position at the company.
  • The firm has hired outside lawyers to examine media reports about its business and chip supply chain, which it says lack verifiable evidence.
  • American officials believe Alice Huang is a Chinese intelligence asset; Beijing calls the claim groundless and the Justice Department says it has not reached a conclusion.
  • Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia chief on 12 November after admitting an affair; Congress has summoned him for closed-door testimony.
