US Sends Third Carrier Strike Group to Middle East as Iran Standoff Deepens

A third American aircraft carrier strike group has arrived in the Middle East, a US official said, taking the naval build-up around Iran to its highest level since April as President Donald Trump weighs his next move.

The USS Theodore Roosevelt entered US Central Command's area of responsibility on Sunday, joining the USS George Washington and the USS George H.W. Bush already operating in the region. The Roosevelt left its home port of San Diego in September and had been expected to relieve the George Washington, which arrived in late August. Instead, all three strike groups are now operating together. The Pentagon has not formally announced the move, which a US official confirmed to reporters.

The deployment comes as Trump travels to Asia for talks with the leaders of China and Russia, with Iran high on the agenda. Trump has swung between threatening further large-scale strikes on Iran and insisting the war is, in his words, effectively over on the military front. Tehran, for its part, says it remains in a state of war, while its deputy foreign minister has floated a 48-hour plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.

The Roosevelt's strike group includes the guided-missile cruiser USS Chosin and the destroyers USS Paul Hamilton and USS Decatur, and it deployed alongside the USS Makin Island amphibious group, which carries around 2,000 marines. The wider flotilla in the Arabian Sea now counts 13 destroyers, two cruisers and a littoral combat ship. Central Command says it has redirected 150 merchant vessels and disabled five since June under a naval blockade of Iranian shipping.

Three carriers operating together in the region is a rare concentration of force. The last time it happened was in April this year, and before that not since the 2003 Iraq war. It remains unclear how long all three groups will stay, with officials saying they could remain in place if needed.

Key points

  • The USS Theodore Roosevelt entered Central Command's area on Sunday, becoming the third US carrier strike group in the Middle East.
  • It joins the USS George Washington and the USS George H.W. Bush. The Roosevelt had been expected to relieve one of them, but all three are operating together for now.
  • The build-up comes as Trump travels to Asia for talks with China and Russia, with Iran high on the agenda, while Tehran says it remains in a state of war.
  • Three carriers in the region at once last happened in April, and before that not since the 2003 Iraq war. The wider flotilla includes 13 destroyers, two cruisers and a littoral combat ship.
