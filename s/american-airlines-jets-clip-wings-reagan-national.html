Two American Airlines Jets Clip Wings While Taxiing at Reagan National Airport

Two American Airlines passenger jets clipped wings while taxiing for departure at Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport on Friday evening, shearing off a wingtip, with no injuries reported, the Federal Aviation Administration said.

Flight 638, heading for Dallas-Fort Worth, made contact with the wing of Flight 2152, heading for Chicago, at about 5:10 p.m. local time on Friday as both Boeing 737-800s waited in the departure queue near Runway 19, the Federal Aviation Administration said. The pilot of Flight 638 told controllers the aircraft had gone slightly past the hold-short line and had “swapped paint” with the neighbouring jet. A winglet, the upturned tip of the wing, broke off in the contact and was left lying on the taxiway, and a passenger on Flight 2152 said he felt a large jolt before seeing the broken wingtip on the ground.

No passengers or crew were hurt, the FAA and the airline said. Both aircraft returned to their gates and were taken out of service for maintenance inspections. Controllers told an arriving aircraft to abandon its landing and warned other incoming flights of possible delays while airport workers recovered the sheared-off wingtip from the taxiway.

The FAA said it would investigate the incident. The collision happened at the same airport where, in January 2025, an American Airlines regional jet preparing to land collided with a US Army helicopter, killing all 67 people on board in the country’s worst commercial air disaster in decades.

The incident came during the Thanksgiving holiday travel period, when about six million Americans were expected to fly domestically, according to motoring group AAA, and as storms brought heavy rain, snow and high winds across parts of the United States, disrupting flights. It also followed a nationwide ground stop earlier in November that left thousands of flights cancelled and passengers stranded at airports across the country.

Key points

  • Two American Airlines Boeing 737-800s clipped wings while taxiing for departure at Reagan National at about 5:10 p.m. on Friday; a winglet broke off and was left on the taxiway.
  • Flight 638, heading for Dallas-Fort Worth, struck the wing of Flight 2152, heading for Chicago; its pilot said the aircraft had gone slightly past the hold-short line.
  • No injuries were reported; both jets returned to their gates and were removed from service for inspection, and the FAA is investigating.
  • The incident happened at the same airport where a mid-air collision in January 2025 killed 67 people, during the busy Thanksgiving travel period.
