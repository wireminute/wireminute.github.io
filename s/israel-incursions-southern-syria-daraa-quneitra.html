Israeli Forces Raid Homes in Southern Syria as Border Villages Live in Fear

Israeli forces have carried out fresh incursions across southern Syria, raiding homes in the provinces of Daraa and Quneitra, setting houses on fire and forcing families to flee, as residents describe a widening military presence along the border.

The latest raids took place on Monday in villages near the line with the occupied Golan Heights. In Koya, in the Yarmouk Basin of western Daraa, families were shot at inside their homes, had their houses set on fire and were forced to flee, according to residents. The operation is the latest in a series of incursions that have spread fear through farming communities that have lived beside the border for generations.

Shortly after midnight between Sunday and Monday, a patrol of 11 Israeli military vehicles, including two armoured personnel carriers, entered the southwestern part of Rafid in the southern countryside of Quneitra and searched several houses. Helicopters and drones flew over the area during the raid. Local sources said no abductions were reported and the reasons for the operation were not clear.

Elsewhere in western Daraa, three military vehicles passed through the Sayhan gate into the Sayhan Valley near the village of Maariyah, where gunfire and two flares were fired from a nearby observation post, and helicopters flew over the Yarmouk Basin at dawn. A separate incursion was reported in the Tal Ahmar area of northern Quneitra. Two days earlier, vehicles had moved out of the Al-Jazira barracks in the Yarmouk Basin, drones were seen over Koya, and a temporary checkpoint was set up in Ruwayhina.

Israeli forces are also continuing engineering and fortification works near the separation line with the occupied Golan Heights. Since the fall of the Assad government in December 2024, Israel has occupied strategic points in southern Syria, including Mount Hermon, and has carried out repeated raids and detentions. A Syrian rights group says 35 Syrians detained in the raids remain unaccounted for, and 69 people have been killed in strikes and raids since 2024.

Syria's foreign minister has described the operations as a violation of international law and a provocation, but Damascus has shown little ability to stop them. For villagers in Daraa and Quneitra, the raids, checkpoints and fortifications have become a constant presence, and residents say they fear the military footprint will keep expanding.

Key points

  • Israeli forces carried out fresh incursions in the Daraa and Quneitra provinces of southern Syria on Monday, raiding homes and setting houses on fire in villages near the border.
  • In Rafid, a patrol of 11 military vehicles searched several houses after midnight, with helicopters and drones overhead; no abductions were reported.
  • Separate movements were reported in the Sayhan Valley, Tal Ahmar and Ruwayhina, alongside continuing fortification work near the line with the occupied Golan Heights.
  • The raids are part of a wider Israeli military presence in southern Syria since December 2024, which Syria's foreign minister has called a violation of international law.
