Measles Deaths in the US Reach 10 in Worst Outbreak Year Since 1991

Measles-associated deaths in the United States have reached 10 this year after Pennsylvania and Wisconsin each reported another fatality on Friday, in the country's worst outbreak in more than three decades.

Pennsylvania and Wisconsin each reported one more death on Friday. Wisconsin said a child had died, while Pennsylvania said its latest victim was an unvaccinated resident of Montour County who had tested positive for measles. Pennsylvania has now recorded seven measles-associated deaths this year, Wisconsin two, and New York one — a child whose death on November 2 was the state's first linked to the disease in more than 30 years.

The toll has risen fast. Half of this year's 10 deaths were reported in the past two weeks, and confirmed cases across the country have passed 5,400 — more than double the 2,289 recorded in 2025 and the highest number since 1991. The weekly count has risen by triple digits for 14 weeks in a row, with 230 new infections reported in the latest week alone.

The official federal count stands lower than the states' total. The national health agency lists six measles-related deaths, already twice its 2025 figure, and says its numbers are subject to change as reports come in. The gap has become a public dispute: the country's health secretary asked the agency's director not to include the deaths in the official tally, saying there was no evidence they were linked to measles, while Pennsylvania's governor has defended the state's counting.

Pennsylvania counts a death as measles-associated when a person with confirmed measles dies within 30 days of developing symptoms, unless the death came from an unrelated cause. The rising toll has put the country's long-held measles elimination status in doubt: a panel of international health experts met this week to decide whether the United States can still be considered free of endemic measles, a status it has held since 2000. Losing it would mark the end of a public-health milestone built on decades of vaccination, which can only be regained after a full year without sustained local transmission.

Key points

  • Two more measles-associated deaths were reported on Friday, one each in Pennsylvania and Wisconsin, taking the US total to 10 this year.
  • Pennsylvania has recorded seven deaths, Wisconsin two and New York one; the latest Pennsylvania victim was an unvaccinated resident who had tested positive for measles.
  • Confirmed cases have passed 5,400 in 2026 — more than double last year's total and the highest since 1991.
  • The official federal tally counts six deaths, and a panel of international health experts met this week to decide whether the US keeps its measles elimination status.
