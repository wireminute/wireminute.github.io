Leaked Russian Records Put War Dead at 720,000, Investigation Finds

The death toll among Russian soldiers since the invasion of Ukraine began in 2022 is close to three quarters of a million, according to a joint investigation by two independent Russian news outlets working from leaked government death records.

Mediazona and Meduza reported on Monday that at least 490,000 Russian military deaths had been registered by the end of September. The figures come from internal records of the federal statistics agency, Rosstat, obtained by two hacking groups, the Belarusian Cyber Partisans and the Ukrainian collective Silent Crow, who disclosed the breach on November 1. The database covers more than 11.6 million death records from January 2021 to late September 2026.

The outlets say the registered deaths are only part of the picture. Many battlefield deaths take a year or more to be recorded, and troops reported missing are almost always later confirmed dead. By studying more than 160,000 notices posted by relatives searching for missing soldiers, the researchers estimated the true toll had reached around 720,000 by the autumn of this year, up from about 530,000 at the end of 2025. At that pace, the investigation found, a Russian soldier has died roughly once every four minutes since the war began.

The findings dwarf earlier estimates. A July study by the Washington think tank CSIS put Russian fatalities at between 125,000 and 150,000, while Moscow last published an official death toll in September 2022, when it reported 5,937. Russia stopped publishing detailed mortality figures in the middle of last year. The leaked records also show the war now ranks among the leading causes of death in Russia since the invasion, after heart disease, Covid and strokes, and that poorer regions and ethnic minorities have suffered far heavier losses than wealthier areas.

The two outlets cross-checked the leaked records against their own database of identified war dead, inheritance records and earlier official statistics, and concluded the material was almost certainly genuine. They cautioned that the figure for the most recent year carries the widest margin of error, because many recent deaths have yet to be registered, and that the figure excludes around 5,400 foreign fighters. The figures have not been independently verified, but the confirmed deaths alone support earlier assessments that the real toll was far higher than Moscow admits.

Key points

  • At least 490,000 Russian military deaths were registered by the end of September, according to leaked state statistics examined by two independent Russian outlets.
  • Accounting for missing soldiers and registration delays, the researchers put the true toll at around 720,000 by this autumn.
  • The estimate is far higher than earlier assessments and dwarfs Moscow's last official toll of 5,937, published in September 2022.
  • The war is now among the leading causes of death in Russia, with poorer regions and minorities bearing the heaviest losses.
