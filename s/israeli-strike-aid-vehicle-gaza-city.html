Israel Says Three Killed in Gaza Food-Vehicle Strike Were Militants as Aid Group Denies Link

Three men were killed in Gaza City on Saturday when an Israeli strike hit a vehicle carrying food, and the two sides now dispute whether the men were aid workers or militants and whether the vehicle belonged to a charity.

Three men were killed in Gaza City on Saturday when an Israeli strike hit a vehicle carrying food in the Tel al-Hawa neighbourhood, and the two sides now dispute who the men were and what the vehicle was doing. Gaza's health ministry and civil defence agency said three people were killed and several others were injured when the vehicle was hit as it was about to hand out meals, and witnesses said cooked rice and papers lay scattered around the wreckage. The Israeli military said later on Saturday that the three were members of the military wing of Islamic Jihad, travelling in a vehicle used to carry food and supplies to fellow fighters, and that the vehicle bore no markings identifying it as belonging to a humanitarian organisation.

World Central Kitchen, whose logo appeared on papers found in the wreckage, said the vehicle was not affiliated with it. The charity said the driver had previously worked for the organisation, but the other two men in the vehicle had not, and the vehicle itself had not belonged to the charity. A witness had said the vehicle was just about to stop to distribute food and drink to children when it was hit by a missile, and Gaza City's Al-Shifa Hospital confirmed receiving the bodies of three men.

Hamas, which governs Gaza, requested an urgent meeting of the mediators and guarantors of the Gaza ceasefire after the strike, saying the three were soup kitchen workers delivering food to displaced Palestinians. The ceasefire has been in effect since October 2025, and Gaza's health ministry says more than 1,650 people have been killed in Israeli strikes since then, despite the truce. Civil defence said a fourth person was killed elsewhere in Gaza on Saturday. The strike came amid near-daily accusations from both sides that the other is violating the ceasefire, and the question of who was in the vehicle is now at the centre of the dispute. The story is still developing.

Key points

  • Three men were killed on Saturday when an Israeli strike hit a food-carrying vehicle in Tel al-Hawa, Gaza City; several others were injured.
  • The Israeli military says the three were Islamic Jihad militants and the vehicle carried supplies for fighters, with no humanitarian markings.
  • World Central Kitchen says the vehicle was not affiliated with it; only the driver had previously worked for the charity.
  • Hamas requested an urgent meeting of ceasefire mediators; Gaza's health ministry says more than 1,650 people have been killed since the October 2025 ceasefire.
