Three Killed as Israeli Strike Hits Aid Vehicle in Gaza City

Three Palestinians were killed in Gaza City on Saturday when an Israeli strike hit a vehicle carrying food for the World Central Kitchen charity, just as it was about to hand out meals to children, according to Gaza's health ministry and witnesses.

Three Palestinians died on Saturday when a missile struck a vehicle carrying food and papers for the charity World Central Kitchen in Gaza City, according to Gaza's health ministry and witnesses at the scene. The vehicle was about to stop to serve food and drink to children in the Tel al-Hawa neighbourhood when it was hit, a witness said. Cooked rice from the meals lay scattered around the damaged vehicle afterwards.

The Israeli military said several fighters had been targeted in the area, without giving further details, and did not immediately explain why the strike had hit a vehicle carrying food for distribution. World Central Kitchen had not replied to a request for comment when the report was published. Gaza's health ministry said nine people had been killed and 55 wounded across the territory in the previous 48 hours.
