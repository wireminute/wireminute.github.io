US Confirms Strike on Iran Missile Site on Qeshm Island in Strait of Hormuz

The United States has confirmed that its fighter aircraft struck an Iranian missile launch site on Iran's Qeshm Island on Tuesday, after Tehran first reported the attack and Washington stayed silent for hours.

A US military official, speaking on condition of anonymity, said fighter aircraft used precision munitions to hit a Revolutionary Guards missile launch site on the island in the Strait of Hormuz. The official described the strike as self-defence and said it was carried out in retaliation for Iran targeting American personnel and assets. Arab media outlets carried the same confirmation, citing a US military official.

The confirmation came hours after Ahmad Nafisi, the deputy governor of Hormozgan province, said American forces had struck sites on the island, according to Iran's official news agency. He said homes, businesses and infrastructure were unharmed and the situation was under control, with emergency services on full alert. Residents heard five or six explosions on Tuesday evening, the agency reported. Iranian media also said a strike had targeted the Nariman air defence site, a detail that has not been independently confirmed.

The strikes followed an Iranian drone attack on a radar system used by American forces at the Sheikh Isa air base in Bahrain earlier on Tuesday, the third such claim in five days. Bahrain said its defences intercepted the drones, and American Central Command said there was no damage to American equipment and no injury to American personnel. The Revolutionary Guards also said on Tuesday that they had shot down an American drone over Qeshm, a claim that could not be independently verified.

Qeshm is Iran's largest island and sits in the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow waterway through which about a fifth of the world's oil normally passes. Shipping through the strait has come under repeated attack in recent days, with vessels reported hit by mines, and the United States has imposed a naval blockade on Iranian ports. The United States had previously struck the island on November 2, when American officials described defensive airstrikes against Iranian positions there. The fighting between the United States, Israel and Iran began in late February and has now run for nearly nine months.

Key points

  • A US military official confirmed fighter aircraft struck a Revolutionary Guards missile launch site on Qeshm with precision munitions, calling it self-defence.
  • An Iranian provincial official said several sites were hit and five or six explosions were heard, but homes, businesses and infrastructure were unharmed.
  • The strike came hours after Iran claimed a drone attack on an American radar site at the Sheikh Isa air base in Bahrain, the third such claim in five days.
  • Qeshm sits in the Strait of Hormuz, through which about a fifth of the world's oil normally passes; the US previously struck the island on November 2.
