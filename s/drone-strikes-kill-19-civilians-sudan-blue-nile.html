Nineteen Civilians Killed as Drones Hit Homes in Sudan's Blue Nile State

Drone strikes on residential neighbourhoods in Damazin, the capital of Sudan's Blue Nile state, have killed 19 civilians, including six women, and wounded 20 others, a Sudanese rights group said on Wednesday.

The strikes hit three residential neighbourhoods in Damazin on Tuesday night, the group Emergency Lawyers said in a statement. The dead and 15 of the wounded were members of three families. The group blamed the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces for the attacks.

Five more civilians were wounded earlier on Tuesday in Roseires, where the group said drones targeted the dam and power facilities. Sudan's war between the army and the Rapid Support Forces has raged since April 2023, and Blue Nile state borders Ethiopia.
