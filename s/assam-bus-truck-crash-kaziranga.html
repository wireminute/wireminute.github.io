Death Toll Rises to 26 in Assam Bus-Truck Crash Near Kaziranga

The death toll from a head-on collision between a passenger minibus and a container truck near Kaziranga National Park in north-eastern India has risen to 26, officials said, after several of the injured died in hospital overnight.

The crash happened on Friday afternoon at Rangajan, near Hathikuli Tea Estate in the Kohora range of Golaghat district, on National Highway 715, which runs past the park. The minibus was travelling from Golaghat towards Nagaon when it met the container truck, heading the other way towards Upper Assam, head-on. A doctor at Kohora Model Hospital said 26 people had died and 20 were injured, after the toll climbed through the evening as injured passengers died during treatment.

Many of those killed were college students returning from classes, and women and children were among the dead. Eight of the injured were in intensive care at Jorhat Medical College and Hospital. Officials said 14 of the dead had been identified, and post-mortem examinations will be carried out on Saturday at the civil hospital in Golaghat. The state's chief minister is expected to visit the area on Saturday.

Dashcam footage from the scene appeared to show the minibus veering across the centre of the road into the path of the oncoming truck. Survivors described the vehicle as speeding and overcrowded, and police said it had a history of speeding fines. The district administration has ordered a detailed inquiry into the cause and whether rules on speed and passenger numbers were broken.

The prime minister announced compensation of 200,000 rupees for the family of each person killed and 50,000 rupees for each injured person. The chief minister said he was deeply distressed by the accident and sent a senior minister to the site to oversee relief work. The highway runs past Kaziranga, a protected park famous for its one-horned rhinos and listed as a world heritage site. The crash is the latest deadly collision on India's roads, which record some of the highest numbers of traffic deaths in the world.

Key points
