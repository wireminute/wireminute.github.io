Two Killed in Dymer Drone Strike as Rescuers Pull Second Body From Rubble; 15 Still Missing

Rescue workers have recovered the bodies of two people from the rubble of a five-storey apartment building in Dymer, in the Kyiv region, after a Russian drone strike partly brought the block down overnight, and police said 15 people were still listed as missing, including two children.

Rescue workers have recovered the bodies of two people from the ruins of a five-storey apartment building in Dymer, in the Kyiv region, after a Russian drone strike partly brought the block down overnight. The head of the Kyiv regional military administration, Tymur Tkachenko, confirmed the second recovery, and the search for more people under the rubble was still going on, with dog handlers working through the debris. Families of the missing waited outside the ruins for news.

Police said 15 people were listed as missing, including an 11-year-old girl and a seven-year-old boy. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy had earlier said 14 people were unaccounted for and may be trapped beneath the debris. Nine residents have been evacuated, including one child, and the fire that followed the strike has been contained. Regional officials had earlier put the number possibly trapped at seven or eight, a figure that has not been reconciled with the later counts.

At least four people were injured. A 16-year-old boy was taken to hospital with shrapnel wounds to his arm, and a 22-year-old woman was treated at the scene for cuts to her foot. A third person, a woman aged about 54, suffered shock and was treated by medics at the scene without needing hospital care. The blast also damaged a kindergarten and a shop close to the building.

The strike hit the settlement of Dymer in the Vyshhorod district overnight into Sunday, destroying flats from the first to the fifth floor. Ukraine's air force said Russia launched 186 drones across the country overnight, of which 161 were shot down. Zelenskyy said Russia had launched about 2,700 drones, nearly 1,460 guided aerial bombs and 165 missiles at Ukraine over the past week, across 20 regions, and renewed his call for the United States to authorise the transfer of Patriot air defence interceptors already funded by allies. Russian shelling also killed one person in Zaporizhzhia, where a child was wounded when guided bombs hit residential buildings.

Ukraine's foreign minister, Andrii Sybiha, urged European allies to stop responding to Russian provocations in isolation and instead deprive Moscow of the resources sustaining its war. The search and rescue operation in Dymer was continuing on Sunday afternoon, with emergency crews still picking through the rubble.

Key points

  • Two people confirmed killed after rescuers recovered a second body from the rubble in Dymer; the search for more people under the debris is continuing.
  • Police said 15 people were listed as missing, including an 11-year-old girl and a seven-year-old boy; the president had earlier said 14 were unaccounted for, and nine residents were evacuated.
  • At least four people injured: a 16-year-old boy hospitalised with shrapnel wounds, a 22-year-old woman treated at the scene, and a woman of about 54 treated for shock.
  • The strike was part of an overnight barrage of 186 drones, 161 shot down, Ukraine's air force said; the foreign minister urged European allies to cut off the resources sustaining Russia's war.
