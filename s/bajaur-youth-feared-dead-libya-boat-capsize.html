Families Fear 25 Young Men Dead After Migrant Boat Capsizes Off Libya

Families in Pakistan's Bajaur district say at least 25 young men from the area are feared dead after a migrant boat capsized in the Mediterranean Sea near Libya, though officials have not confirmed the incident and no bodies have been recovered.

The boat is reported to have gone down on November 21 while carrying migrants bound for Europe. Families say they learned of the sinking from the agents who arranged the journeys, and some reports put the number feared dead as high as 35. The Pakistani government, the Foreign Office and international rescue organisations have not confirmed the incident, and no wreck has been reported in international media.

Funeral prayers in absentia have been held for six of the men, and the Federal Investigation Agency has sent a team to Bajaur to investigate suspected human trafficking networks. A government minister said officials were in contact with the Pakistani embassy in Libya and that a search operation was under way, while an opposition party demanded urgent diplomatic intervention to step up rescue efforts.

  • Funeral prayers have been held for six of the men, the FIA is investigating suspected trafficking networks, and a search operation is said to be under way.
