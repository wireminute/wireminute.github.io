MS Dhoni Retires From the IPL, Takes Over as Chennai Super Kings Mentor

MS Dhoni has ended his playing career in the Indian Premier League, with Chennai Super Kings announcing on Tuesday that the 45-year-old will become the team's mentor from the 2027 season.

Chennai Super Kings announced the decision on Tuesday, the day teams had to finalise their retained players for the 2027 season. Dhoni, 45, will move into the dugout as team mentor, and the franchise said he would keep guiding its players into the next phase. Its managing director, K. S. Viswanathan, said the team would miss seeing him in yellow on the field but was glad he would stay on to guide the players.

Dhoni has been with Chennai since the league began in 2008, when he was the franchise's first signing, and he captained the side to five titles, in 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021 and 2023. No other captain has won as many. He also led the team to two Champions League Twenty20 titles, and he is the only captain to have overseen more than 100 wins in the league.

He stepped down as captain before the 2024 season, handing the role to Ruturaj Gaikwad, and played on for two more seasons before missing the whole of 2026 through injury. He retires as Chennai's most-capped player, with 272 appearances and 5,314 runs, and as the seventh-highest run-scorer in the league's history.

Dhoni retired from international cricket in 2020, and the Indian Premier League was the last cricket he still played. Chennai's owner, N. Srinivasan, said there was no Chennai Super Kings without Dhoni, describing him as part of the team's identity after nearly two decades together. The announcement came hours before the retention deadline, with the mini auction for the 2027 season set for December 16 in Goa.

Key points

  • MS Dhoni has retired as an IPL player and will become Chennai Super Kings' team mentor from the 2027 season
  • He captained Chennai to five IPL titles in 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021 and 2023, the joint-most by any captain
  • He stepped down as captain before the 2024 season and missed the whole of 2026 through injury
  • He retires as Chennai's most-capped player with 272 appearances and 5,314 runs, after a career with the franchise stretching back to 2008
