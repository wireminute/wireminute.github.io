Category 5 Cyclone Xavier Batters Northern Cook Islands With 315 km/h Gusts

Severe Tropical Cyclone Xavier has strengthened into a Category 5 storm over the Northern Cook Islands, packing sustained winds of 220 kilometres per hour and gusts up to 315, as emergency officials urge islanders to stay on high alert.

Xavier was about 260 kilometres northeast of Pukapuka early on Wednesday, moving west-southwest at about 7 kilometres per hour, the Cook Islands and Fiji meteorological services said. Gale warnings are in force for Pukapuka and Nassau, with damaging winds, heavy rain, dangerous seas and coastal inundation expected, while Manihiki remains under a strong wind warning.

Emergency Management Cook Islands has urged communities across the Northern Cook Islands to remain on high alert, with shelters activated. No casualties or major damage had been reported by Wednesday morning. On Manihiki, families face the storm with the atoll's electricity system out of service for more than a year, relying on limited solar power.

Key points

  • Xavier strengthened to Category 5 with sustained winds of about 220 km/h and gusts up to 315 km/h.
  • Gale warnings are in force for Pukapuka and Nassau, with heavy rain, dangerous seas and coastal flooding expected.
  • No casualties or major damage had been reported by Wednesday morning, and the storm is not expected to threaten Fiji, Tonga or Samoa.
