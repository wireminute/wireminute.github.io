Two Salvadoran Soldiers Killed in Haiti Blast in First Deaths of Deployment

Two Salvadoran soldiers were killed and two more injured in a blast while handling explosive material during their deployment in Haiti, El Salvador's military said on Friday, in the first officially reported deaths since the large-scale deployment began about a month ago.

The military said the blast happened as the soldiers were working with explosive material, but did not say where in Haiti it took place or what kind of explosives were involved. The two injured soldiers were taken for treatment, and no further details were given about their condition.

The deaths are the first officially reported among Salvadoran troops since the deployment began about a month ago to support Haiti's police against armed gangs. Salvadoran forces serve in the international security mission in Haiti, where armed gangs control large parts of the capital, Port-au-Prince.
