GTA 6 Launches After 13 Years to Near-Perfect Reviews as Players Flood In

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally in players' hands after a 13-year wait, unlocking at midnight in New Zealand and rolling around the world through the day, as critics handed it near-perfect scores and preorders passed $1.5 billion.

The game went live at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, in New Zealand, the first time zone to reach the date, which was 11 in the morning on Wednesday in London and 6 in the morning on the east coast of the United States. Australia followed as its clocks struck midnight, and the release is moving west through each region in turn. It is available only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S, with no PC version announced, and the download runs to about 212 gigabytes after preloading opened on November 12.

The review embargo lifted at the same moment as the New Zealand unlock, and the verdict was close to unanimous. The game's aggregate score stood at 99 out of 100 from more than 140 critic reviews, matching the highest score ever recorded for a game, with reviewers awarding top marks across the board. Early reports put the main story at around 50 hours, and the game returns to Vice City, a fictional version of Miami, in the state of Leonida, with two leads, Lucia and Jason, the series' first playable woman since its early two-dimensional games.

The launch caps a commercial build-up without precedent for a game. Market researchers Sensor Tower estimate about 16 million copies were preordered, worth roughly $1.54 billion, with about three quarters of them on PlayStation. Rockstar delayed the game twice from its original autumn 2025 window before settling on November 19, and the standard edition costs $79.99 in the United States and 69.99 pounds in Britain.

The staggered midnight start revived the scramble to play first. Some Xbox players got in hours early by switching their console's region to New Zealand, a workaround reports say worked this time, while PlayStation unlocks follow the country of the account, leaving those players waiting for their own midnight. Absence data in Britain puts the game in 4.6 percent of time-off requests, with about 83,000 days booked off across November and December, and leave bookings in Australia for November 19 are up 29 percent on last year.

Key points

  • Grand Theft Auto VI unlocked at midnight in New Zealand and is rolling out region by region; it is available only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S, with no PC version announced.
  • Critics were close to unanimous, with the game's aggregate score at 99 out of 100 from more than 140 reviews, matching the highest ever recorded.
