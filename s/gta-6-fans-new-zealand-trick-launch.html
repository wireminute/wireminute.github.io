GTA 6 Fans Book Time Off and Pretend to Be in New Zealand to Play First

With two days to go before Grand Theft Auto VI reaches players, fans are booking time off work, buying new consoles and trying to make their machines think they are in New Zealand, all to play the game as early as possible.

Grand Theft Auto VI arrives on Thursday, November 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, after Rockstar shifted the launch from May 26, 2026, and confirmed November 19 in November last year. The game will unlock at midnight in each region, so New Zealand, one of the first places to reach midnight, gets it while it is still Wednesday morning in much of the world. In Britain that means 11 in the morning, and on the east coast of the United States it means 6 in the morning. There is no official early access, and digital buyers have been able to download the game since November 12, a download of up to 212 gigabytes.

That staggered start has set off a hunt for a head start. Xbox players are talking about changing the region on their console to New Zealand, a switch reports say could open the game up to 19 hours early. PlayStation owners face a harder path, because unlocks follow the country of the account, so some are opening separate New Zealand accounts and buying gift cards in New Zealand dollars. Searches in Britain for the New Zealand workaround have risen by more than 5,000 percent in the past month, according to Google Trends. Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft have not commented on the workaround, and it is unconfirmed whether it will work.

Time off is the other plan. Absence data from the British HR firm BrightHR puts the game in 4.6 percent of time-off requests, with about 83,000 days booked off across November and December. In Australia, workforce figures show leave bookings for November 19 up 29 percent on last year, and up 84 percent among men aged 18 to 44. Some fans are also buying a console to play it on, and one United States Army division offered soldiers a four-day pass to play the game if they signed a re-enlistment contract of at least two years.

The rush follows a 13-year wait since the last game in the series, and the new game returns to Vice City in a fictional state modelled on Florida, with the series' first playable female lead since its early two-dimensional games. Market researchers Sensor Tower estimate the game has taken $1.18 billion from 12 million pre-orders, a figure Rockstar has not confirmed. The standard edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition $99.99, and no PC version has been announced.

Key points

  • Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday, November 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, unlocking at midnight in each region, with New Zealand first.
  • Fans are switching console regions to New Zealand or opening New Zealand accounts to try to play up to 19 hours early, though the trick is unconfirmed and the companies have not commented.
  • Absence data puts the game in 4.6 percent of British time-off requests, with about 83,000 days booked off across November and December.
  • There is no official early access; pre-loading began November 12 and needs up to 212GB, and no PC version has been announced.
