AOC Challenged Over Backing Jeffries as Speaker Amid Questions on His AIPAC Ties

CODEPINK co-founder Medea Benjamin confronted Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez over her support for Representative Hakeem Jeffries as the next House speaker, questioning his ties to the pro-Israel lobby group AIPAC and his record on Israel.

The exchange was filmed and shared online days after House Democrats nominated Jeffries on Wednesday to lead their new majority, putting him on course to be the chamber's first Black speaker when the full House votes in January. Benjamin pressed Ocasio-Cortez on why progressives were not mounting a challenge to a leader whose backing for Israel and links to AIPAC have drawn criticism from the party's left.

Ocasio-Cortez defended her position, saying Jeffries is the only candidate standing and that she is using talks on foreign policy and a campaign to limit super PAC spending to press for change. Her critics point to Jeffries voting in July to keep $3.3 billion in military aid to Israel in place, a vote 103 House Democrats opposed, and to his decision not to sponsor legislation restricting weapons transfers to Israel.

  • Ocasio-Cortez said Jeffries is the only candidate in the race and pointed to talks on foreign policy and a campaign to limit super PAC spending.
