Dashcam Captures Missile Slamming Into Lviv Street as Two Killed in Russian Barrage

Dashcam footage has captured the moment a Russian projectile slammed into a building on a street in Lviv, in western Ukraine, during a missile and drone barrage on Friday that killed two people and injured at least 10.

The footage, recorded by a motorist driving through the city on Friday, November 20, shows the projectile striking a building directly ahead of the car. The impact sent debris flying across the road, and the driver reversed sharply before turning and driving away from the blast.

The strike hit Lviv's Zaliznychnyi district, where fires broke out in residential buildings and garages and parked vehicles were damaged. Ukrainian authorities said the bombardment left a trail of destruction across homes, a dormitory, a warehouse and energy infrastructure, and also damaged a ticket terminal at Sknyliv railway station.

The fallout reached beyond the city limits. In the nearby Zolochiv district, debris from a Russian drone that had been shot down damaged three houses and a vehicle, leaving one further person injured.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia launched more than 400 attack drones and over 110 missiles, including 27 ballistic missiles, in the wider assault across Ukraine that night. The Lviv dashcam clip circulated widely on Sunday, two days after the attack, and quickly became one of the most shared images of the bombardment.

The clip spread quickly after it was published on Sunday, carried by news outlets across Europe. It shows the strike from the driver's point of view, the projectile appearing in the sky moments before it hit the building, and the street filling with smoke and debris.

Lviv lies in western Ukraine, near the border with Poland and far from the front lines in the east. The city has been a refuge for Ukrainians fleeing the fighting since the full-scale invasion began, and strikes there are watched closely because of how near the city sits to the Polish border.

Key points

  • Dashcam footage shows a projectile hitting a building directly ahead of a car in Lviv on Friday; the driver reversed and escaped.
  • Two people killed and at least 10 injured in Lviv's Zaliznychnyi district; one more person injured in the nearby Zolochiv district.
  • Fires in homes and garages, with damage to a dormitory, a warehouse, energy infrastructure and a railway station ticket terminal.
  • Zelenskyy said Russia fired more than 400 drones and over 110 missiles, including 27 ballistic missiles, in the wider attack.
