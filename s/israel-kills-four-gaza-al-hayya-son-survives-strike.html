Hamas Chief's Last Surviving Son Escapes Gaza Strike That Killed Four

Hamas leader Khalil al-Hayya's last surviving son has escaped an Israeli air strike on his family in Gaza, as four other Palestinians were killed in separate attacks across the territory on Friday.

The strike hit at dawn on Friday, targeting a residential apartment where Izzeldeen al-Hayya, 29, was with his family, according to Hamas. He and his family survived, though several relatives were wounded, including the Hamas leader's daughter-in-law. No one was killed in that strike. The family of another of al-Hayya's sons, Azzam, who was killed earlier this year, also survived.

Izzeldeen al-Hayya is the last of the Hamas leader's sons still alive. Four of his brothers have already been killed by Israeli forces. Hamza died in 2008 and Osama in 2014. Humam was killed in September last year. Azzam died this May. Izzeldeen is the twin brother of Humam, who died in a 2025 Israeli strike on Hamas leaders in Qatar, a strike his father narrowly survived. Khalil al-Hayya was named the group's overall leader in July.

Elsewhere in Gaza on Friday, four Palestinians were killed despite a ceasefire agreed more than a year ago. Medical sources said three people were killed and two wounded in a strike on the southern city of Rafah, while one person was killed when a drone hit a vehicle in the north of the territory.

Hamas condemned the attack on the al-Hayya family as an attempt to assassinate Izzeldeen, and said it was linked to threats made against his father by Israeli Defence Minister Israel Katz. A day earlier, al-Hayya had said the group could respond after Israeli attacks left at least 10 people dead in Gaza. The Israeli military had no immediate comment on Friday's strike.

Key points

  • Izzeldeen al-Hayya, 29, survived a dawn strike on a residential apartment in Gaza; relatives were wounded, including his father's daughter-in-law, but no one in the family was killed.
  • He is the Hamas chief's last surviving son: four brothers were killed by Israeli forces between 2008 and this May, including his twin in a 2025 strike in Qatar.
  • Four Palestinians were killed in separate attacks elsewhere in Gaza on Friday, three in Rafah and one in a drone strike in the north, despite a ceasefire agreed more than a year ago.
  • Hamas called the strike an assassination attempt linked to threats by Israel's defence minister; a day earlier, al-Hayya had said the group could respond after Israeli attacks left at least 10 people dead in Gaza.
