Drones Strike Crimea Bridge as Russia Reports 1,336 Drones Intercepted Overnight

Ukrainian drones struck the bridge linking Russia and Crimea overnight, halting traffic across the 19-kilometre crossing as Russia reported intercepting 1,336 drones in one of the largest barrages of the war.

Ukrainian drones struck the bridge linking Russia with Crimea overnight, bringing traffic to a halt across the 19-kilometre crossing. The Russian-installed head of Crimea confirmed that the bridge had been hit and said traffic would be restricted until around midday on Monday, while the Kremlin said the damage was minor and was being assessed.

Russia's defence ministry said its air defences had shot down 1,336 Ukrainian drones overnight, across 17 regions as well as Crimea and the waters of the Black Sea and the Sea of Azov. Moscow's mayor said about 600 drones had headed towards the capital and 191 were brought down on its approaches. Around 2,000 cars were left waiting at the bridge, and four passenger trains heading for Crimea were held up for two to four and a half hours.

How badly the bridge was damaged is not yet clear. A monitoring channel reported that one of the arches over the shipping channel had been hit and shifted, but that could not be independently verified, and Ukraine's military has not commented on the strike. The bridge is the only direct road and rail link between Russia and the peninsula, which Russia seized and annexed in 2014, and it carries supplies to Russian forces in Crimea. It has been hit before, in explosions in October 2022 and July 2023 and again near its support pillars in June 2025, and Ukraine's president has called it a legitimate military target.

The strike comes three days before the leaders of the United States, Russia and China are due to meet in Shenzhen on Thursday, in the first three-way summit of the leaders of the world's three largest nuclear-armed states. The war in Ukraine is expected to be on the agenda, and the overnight barrage shows the fighting intensifying in the run-up to the meeting.

Key points

  • Ukrainian drones struck the 19-kilometre bridge linking Russia and Crimea overnight; the Russian-installed governor confirmed the hit and restricted traffic until midday, while the Kremlin said the damage was minor.
  • Russia said its air defences shot down 1,336 drones overnight, one of the largest barrages of the war; about 600 were aimed at Moscow, with 191 brought down near the capital.
  • Around 2,000 cars were backed up at the crossing and four passenger trains to Crimea were delayed; the full extent of the damage to the bridge has not been independently verified.
  • The bridge is Russia's main supply route to Crimea and has been hit before; the strike comes three days before the leaders of the United States, Russia and China meet in Shenzhen.
