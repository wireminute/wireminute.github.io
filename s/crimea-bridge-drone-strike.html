Ukraine Hits Crimean Bridge Railway Section in Third Strike This Week as Trains Halt

Ukraine has struck the Crimean Bridge for a third time in a week, hitting its railway section overnight and forcing train services across the crossing to stop again, Russian officials said, hours before the leaders of Russia, the United States and China meet in Shenzhen.

Ukraine struck the railway section of the Crimean Bridge in an overnight drone attack, Russia's transport ministry said on Thursday, suspending train services across the crossing for a third time in a week. The ministry said nobody was hurt and specialists were assessing the damage.

Sergei Aksyonov, the Russian-installed head of Crimea, said in a Telegram message that the bridge had been attacked again and traffic was temporarily halted while the damage was assessed. All relevant services were on site, he said. Ukraine's General Staff hinted in a morning statement that the bridge had been hit, in an indirect confirmation of the strike. Local channels reported a series of explosions near Kerch in the early hours, some of them possibly air defences at work.

The railway section was the part of the crossing that had survived the earlier strikes this week. On Tuesday, Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the central road section of the bridge, forcing its main road arch to crumble into the sea and leaving a gap in the crossing, according to video verified by a news agency. Two people were killed in the district around Kerch in that strike. Rail traffic had partly resumed on Tuesday afternoon, while the road section stayed closed.

Ukraine's president said the strikes were the seventh phase of an operation prepared over several weeks, and the Security Service said one of the bridge's metal arches and the road span beneath it were completely destroyed and no vehicles could cross. The first strike came on Monday morning, the first time in more than a year that Ukraine had hit the crossing. Ukraine said Palianytsia drones struck the road section, while Neptune missiles, Ruta drones and Flamingo missiles hit the railway section.

The bridge is the main road and rail link Russia uses to move fuel, ammunition and troops onto the peninsula, and a flagship project inaugurated by President Vladimir Putin in 2018. The Kremlin said Putin had been briefed and vowed the bridge would be rebuilt. Russia's Investigative Committee confirmed structural damage to the bridge and opened a criminal case on terrorism charges, blaming Ukraine's armed forces for the attack. The latest strike came hours before Putin was due to meet the leaders of the United States and China in Shenzhen.

Key points

  • The overnight strike hit the Crimean Bridge's railway section, the part that had survived this week's earlier attacks; train services are suspended again and nobody was hurt, Russia's transport ministry said.
  • Ukraine's General Staff indirectly confirmed the strike, and the Russian-installed governor of Crimea said traffic was halted while damage was assessed.
  • Earlier strikes this week destroyed the bridge's central road section, sending its main road arch into the sea; two people were killed near Kerch.
  • The bridge is Russia's main road and rail supply link to Crimea, and the attacks come hours before Putin meets the US and Chinese leaders in Shenzhen.
