Crimean Bridge Attacked for Third Time in a Week as Traffic Halts Again

Traffic over the Crimean Bridge has been suspended again after a third attack on the crossing in a week, the Russian-installed governor of Crimea said, hours before the leaders of Russia, the United States and China are due to meet.

Sergei Aksyonov, the Russian-installed head of Crimea, said in a Telegram message on Thursday that the bridge had been attacked and that traffic was temporarily halted while damage was assessed. All relevant services were on site, he said. The attack is the third this week on the 19-kilometre bridge linking mainland Russia with the peninsula Moscow seized and annexed in 2014.

Earlier this week, Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the central road section of the bridge, forcing its main road arch to crumble into the sea and leaving a gap in the crossing, according to video verified by a news agency. Ukraine's president said the strike was the seventh phase of an operation prepared over several weeks, and the Security Service said one of the bridge's metal arches and the road span beneath it were completely destroyed and no vehicles could cross.

Two people were killed in the district around Kerch in that strike, the Russian-installed governor said. Rail traffic partly resumed on Tuesday afternoon after a pause, while the road section stayed closed. The Kremlin said President Vladimir Putin had been briefed and vowed the bridge would be rebuilt. Russia's Investigative Committee confirmed structural damage to the bridge and opened a criminal case on terrorism charges, blaming Ukraine's armed forces for the attack.

The bridge is the main road and rail link Russia uses to move fuel, ammunition and troops onto the peninsula, and a flagship project inaugurated by Putin in 2018. The first strike came on Monday morning, the first time in more than a year that Ukraine had hit the crossing. Ukraine said Palianytsia drones struck the road section, while Neptune missiles, Ruta drones and FP-5 Flamingo missiles hit the railway section.

Russia's defence ministry said its air defences shot down 1,441 Ukrainian drones overnight across 22 regions, the largest such attack of the war by its own count. Ukrainian drones also struck energy sites, including a refinery at Ilsky in the Krasnodar region, where falling debris set off a fire and injured six people. Russia answered with its own strikes, hitting Kyiv's North and South bridges across the Dnipro and damaging the subway system, cutting water and heating in parts of the capital. The exchange comes as the leaders of the United States, Russia and China prepare to meet in Shenzhen on Thursday.

Key points

  • Earlier this week, Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the bridge's central road section, sending its main road arch into the sea; two people were killed near Kerch.

  • The bridge is Russia's main road and rail supply link to Crimea, and the attacks come hours before Putin meets the US and Chinese leaders in Shenzhen.
