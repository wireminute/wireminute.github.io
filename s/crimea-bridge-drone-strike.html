Russian Drones Strike Kyiv Hours After Putin Vowed Harsher Retaliation for Bridge Attacks

Russian drones struck Kyiv on Thursday afternoon, hours after Vladimir Putin promised a far harsher response to Ukraine's attacks on the Crimean Bridge, killing at least one person and injuring five in the capital.

Kyiv came under a renewed drone attack on Thursday afternoon, with Ukrainian air defences shooting down many of the incoming drones while several got through and struck at the heart of the capital. Kyiv's mayor, Vitali Klitschko, said one person was killed and five were injured across the city, four of them taken to hospital. A 26-year-old man was killed in the Podilskyi district, where flats in a residential building caught fire.

The attack damaged the windows of St Volodymyr's Cathedral and a foreign embassy building, Klitschko said, and drone debris fell on the grounds of the Foreign Ministry. The daytime assault followed overnight Russian strikes on Ukrainian energy facilities, port infrastructure and vessels.

The strikes on Kyiv came hours after Putin, speaking in Shenzhen after his meeting with the American and Chinese leaders, promised that Russia's answer to the bridge attacks would be far more painful than the strikes themselves. He ruled out negotiations after the attacks and said Moscow would no longer consider a pause in strikes in the Black Sea or on energy infrastructure.

Ukraine had struck the railway section of the Crimean Bridge in an overnight drone attack on Thursday, Russia's transport ministry said, suspending train services across the crossing for the third time in a week. Some rail traffic resumed on Thursday afternoon, the ministry said, with nobody hurt in the bridge strike. The railway section was the part of the crossing that had survived the earlier strikes this week.

On Tuesday, Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the central road section, sending its main road arch into the sea and leaving a gap in the crossing, according to video verified by a news agency. Two people were killed near Kerch in that strike. Ukraine's president said the strikes were the seventh phase of an operation prepared over several weeks, using home-made Palianytsia drones, Neptune missiles and other weapons.

Russian officials said the bridge would be rebuilt. Deputy Prime Minister Marat Khusnullin said individual sections could reopen within one or two months, and the Kremlin said it would be rebuilt better than before. The bridge is the main road and rail link Russia uses to move fuel, ammunition and troops onto the peninsula, and Russia's Investigative Committee has opened a terrorism case over the attacks.

Key points

  • Russian drones struck Kyiv on Thursday afternoon, hours after Putin vowed a harsher response to the bridge attacks; the city's mayor said one person was killed and five injured, and the windows of St Volodymyr's Cathedral and a foreign embassy were damaged.
  • Ukraine hit the Crimean Bridge's railway section in an overnight drone attack on Thursday, the third strike on the crossing in a week; some rail traffic resumed on Thursday afternoon and nobody was hurt.
  • Tuesday's strike destroyed the bridge's central road section, sending its main road arch into the sea; two people were killed near Kerch.
  • Putin ruled out negotiations and any pause in strikes on Black Sea shipping or energy infrastructure; Russian officials said sections of the bridge could reopen within one or two months.
