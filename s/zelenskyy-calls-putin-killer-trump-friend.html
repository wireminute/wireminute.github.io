Zelenskyy Calls Putin a 'Killer' After Trump Describes Russian Leader as His Friend

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has called Vladimir Putin a 'killer' after US President Donald Trump described the Russian leader as his friend, exposing a public split over how to talk about Moscow as Washington presses for an end to the war in Ukraine.

In an interview with CNN broadcast on Friday, Zelenskyy was asked about Trump's description of Putin after the two leaders met in China this week. "For us he's an enemy, and of course, for us, he is a killer," Zelenskyy said. "And I think mostly for the people in the world, he is a killer," he added, saying Putin "is killing each day and night."

Zelenskyy said it was up to the US president how to describe Putin, but warned that calling the Russian leader a friend meant "he doesn't feel enough pressure."

Trump had called Putin his "friend" during their meeting in China, their first since August 2025, with Putin replying in English: "Nice to meet you." Speaking at the White House on Friday, Trump defended the relationship. "I have fantastic relationships with Putin. That's a very good thing. I have fantastic relationships with President Xi. That's a good thing, and our country is safe because of that," he said.

Asked whether Putin was a friend to the United States, Trump said he could not speak for the country, but added that their "very good relationship... gives us a lot of additional strength." Trump also said he believed Putin wanted to end the war through diplomacy and that both Putin and Zelenskyy wanted it to end, while suggesting both men shared responsibility for the fighting continuing.

The exchange came as Russian attacks on Ukraine intensified. Putin's forces launched a full-scale invasion in February 2022, in a war estimated to have killed hundreds of thousands of people. Washington was Kyiv's main backer under former President Joe Biden, but Trump has cut military support for Ukraine since returning to office, and has been pressing both sides towards a negotiated settlement.

Key points

  • Zelenskyy told CNN that for Ukraine Putin is 'an enemy' and 'a killer', saying he is 'killing each day and night'.
  • Trump called Putin his 'friend' at their meeting in China this week, their first since August 2025, and defended the relationship at the White House on Friday.
  • Zelenskyy warned that describing Putin as a friend means the Russian leader 'doesn't feel enough pressure'.
  • The exchange came as Russian attacks on Ukraine intensified, with Trump saying both leaders want the war to end.
