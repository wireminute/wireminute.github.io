At Least 38 Dead as Gunmen Ambush Villagers Bound for Market in Western Niger

Gunmen ambushed a group of villagers on their way to a weekly market in western Niger, killing at least 38 people, local sources said, in the latest mass killing in a border region where armed groups have terrorised civilians for years.

The attack happened early on Thursday, when residents of the village of Tillim set off for the weekly market in Tera, a town in the Tillaberi region close to the borders with Burkina Faso and Mali. They were travelling under the escort of members of a local self-defence group when unidentified gunmen struck the convoy, a former elected official said.

Thirty-seven people were killed at the scene, and another person died of wounds the following day, the official said. Eleven of the dead were members of the self-defence group. Twenty were men and seven were women, residents said, adding that children were also among the victims. Several wounded survivors were taken to a nearby hospital. No group has claimed the attack, and the gunmen have not been identified.

Niger has been ruled by a military government since a coup in 2023, and its forces have struggled to contain armed groups linked to al-Qaeda and Islamic State that operate across the borders with Mali and Burkina Faso. The violence has claimed thousands of lives over the past decade, and attacks on villages, markets and convoys have become a regular feature of life in the west of the country. The army has allowed villagers to form self-defence militias, and the military authorities have said civilian army auxiliaries will be introduced to help fight the armed groups.

Tillaberi, the region where the ambush took place, has become the centre of the violence. Last year it recorded the highest civilian toll anywhere in the central Sahel, according to the conflict monitor ACLED, and it is home to just under half of Niger's 450,000 registered displaced people, according to the United Nations. In February, 25 members of civilian self-defence groups were killed in ambushes in villages across the same region. In the south-east, Niger also faces attacks by Boko Haram and a rival offshoot, Islamic State West Africa Province.

Key points

  • At least 38 people were killed when gunmen ambushed villagers from Tillim on their way to the weekly market in Tera, in Niger's Tillaberi region.
  • Thirty-seven died where the attack happened and one more died of wounds the next day; eleven of the dead were self-defence fighters, and the toll included 20 men and seven women.
