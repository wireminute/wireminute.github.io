Toxic Liquor Kills 11 and Hospitalises More Than 180 in India's Manipur State

At least 11 people have died and more than 180 others are in hospital in India's north-eastern state of Manipur after drinking alcohol that officials suspect was tainted, the state's chief minister said on Friday, as authorities ordered an inquiry and warned residents against drinking liquor from doubtful sources.

Chief Minister Yumnam Khemchand Singh said the victims fell ill after drinking the liquor over the past two days in Chandel district, near the border with Myanmar. "Over 180 patients have been admitted in the two hospitals so far, while the death toll has reached 11 in the last 48 hours," he said in a statement on Friday after visiting the patients.

The patients are being treated at two hospitals in Imphal, the state capital, about 75 kilometres from where the incident was reported. Shija Hospital has admitted 140 people and Babina Hospital 41, according to the chief minister's office. The dead were all men aged between 32 and 50, and the patients came from villages across Chandel district, including the Chakpikarong area.

Police in Chandel said the liquor was bought in Sugnu, a town in the neighbouring district of Kakching, and was not brewed in Chandel itself. The district administration has advised people not to drink locally brewed liquor or alcohol of doubtful origin, particularly from the suspected source, until further notice. The exact cause of the deaths has not yet been medically established and remains under investigation. The advisory was issued through the district authorities of Chandel, Imphal West and Kakching.

Singh has ordered an inquiry to identify those responsible and said they would be arrested and punished. Deaths from illicit or adulterated alcohol are reported regularly in India, where cheap liquor sold outside regulated channels can contain methanol, a highly toxic industrial chemical that can kill in doses as small as 60 millilitres.

Key points

  • At least 11 people have died in the past 48 hours and more than 180 are in hospital after drinking suspected tainted alcohol in Manipur's Chandel district.
  • 140 patients are being treated at Shija Hospital and 41 at Babina Hospital in Imphal; the dead were men aged 32 to 50.
  • Police say the liquor was bought in Sugnu, in neighbouring Kakching district, and was not brewed in Chandel.
  • The chief minister has ordered an inquiry, and district officials have warned people not to drink liquor of doubtful origin while the cause is investigated.
