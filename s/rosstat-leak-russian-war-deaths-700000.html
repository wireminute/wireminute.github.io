Leaked Russian Records Point to 700,000 War Dead in Ukraine, Analysis Finds

At least 490,000 Russian service members had died in the war in Ukraine by the end of September 2026, according to death records obtained in a hack of Russia's state statistics agency, and the true toll is likely above 700,000 once the missing and unregistered dead are counted, independent outlets said in an analysis published on Monday.

The analysis, published on 23 November by the independent outlets Mediazona and Meduza, is based on mortality records taken from Rosstat, Russia's federal statistics service, by the hacker groups Cyber Partisans and Silent Crow. The groups announced the breach on 1 November, saying they had reached inside Rosstat's internal networks and taken terabytes of data, including death records the agency had stopped publishing in mid-2025.

The journalists worked with two sets of files: a database of 29.7 million death registrations from Russia's civil registry system covering 2021 through August 2026, and a further 15 million newer records passed to them on 15 November, covering registrations through 29 September 2026. After removing duplicates and corrected entries, they were left with 11,658,178 unique death records. They checked the data against their own named list of fallen service members, the national probate registry, court rulings and Rosstat's own earlier published figures, and said the records matched 95.1 percent of the named service members whose deaths fell within the period covered.

The files contain about 425,000 records with military causes of death, along with tens of thousands of people declared dead by court decisions and other records of soldiers who died of combat wounds, taking the registered toll to at least 490,000. But the journalists cautioned that the count understates the real figure: a growing share of military deaths is registered a year or more after the event, and many of the missing never appear in the records at all. Allowing for those lags and the missing, they estimated that about 530,000 Russians had been killed by the end of 2025, and that the total had likely risen to 720,000 or more by the autumn of 2026.

The figures are far higher than earlier public estimates based on confirmed names alone, though they remain well below the casualty claims made by Kyiv. Ukraine's military puts total Russian losses at about 1.64 million, a figure that covers killed and wounded together. Moscow does not publish its own military death toll, and the leaked records offer the fullest picture yet of the human cost of the war on the Russian side.

Key points

  • At least 490,000 Russian service members had died by the end of September 2026, according to leaked Rosstat death records.
  • The true toll is likely above 700,000 once the missing and late-registered deaths are counted, the analysis found.
  • The records came from a hack of Russia's statistics agency by the groups Cyber Partisans and Silent Crow.
  • Ukraine's military puts total Russian losses at about 1.64 million, a figure covering killed and wounded together.
