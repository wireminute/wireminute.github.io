Hasina Plans December 16 Return to Bangladesh, Seeking State Airline Flight

Bangladesh's ousted prime minister Sheikh Hasina plans to return home from exile in India on December 16 and has asked the government in Dhaka to arrange her flight on the state airline, her spokesperson has said.

Her spokesperson, Syed Mozammel Ali, said on Tuesday that Hasina plans to return on December 16, Victory Day, the anniversary of the end of the 1971 liberation war. She has asked Dhaka to arrange her return on flag carrier Biman Bangladesh Airlines, and is also seeking the travel paperwork that was cancelled months after she fled to Delhi.

"Since Sheikh Hasina was flown to India on a Bangladesh aircraft on August 5th, 2024, she now calls upon the government to arrange for her air transport from Delhi back to Dhaka," Ali said in a statement. "Having exiled her contrary to the Constitution under such circumstances, it is now the responsibility of the government to make possible her return to Bangladesh on Victory Day on December 16th."

A Bangladeshi government spokesperson did not immediately respond to a request for comment on the return plan.

Hasina has been in India since fleeing in August 2024, when a student-led uprising ended her 15-year rule. In November 2025, a tribunal in Dhaka sentenced her to death in her absence, convicting her of crimes against humanity over the crackdown on protesters during the uprising that forced her from office.

Earlier this month she said in an email interview that she would return in December despite the sentence, saying no threat of death would keep her from her own country. Dhaka has said she would face execution if she returns and has asked India to hand her over; New Delhi says the extradition request is under legal review.

Tensions in Bangladesh remain high. Supporters of her Awami League party clashed with security forces in the town of Gopalganj in recent days, and unrest spread across 14 districts last week, with three people killed and 124 arrested, as the party marked the anniversary of the day she was sentenced to death.
