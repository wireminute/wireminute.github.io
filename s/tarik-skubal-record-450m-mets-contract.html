Skubal Agrees Record $450 Million Mets Deal, the Richest Ever for a Pitcher

Tarik Skubal has agreed a nine-year deal worth $450 million with the New York Mets, the biggest contract ever given to a pitcher, ending one of the most closely watched free-agent races in years.

The agreement, reported on Sunday, will pay the two-time American League Cy Young Award winner an average of $50 million a season and runs through 2035. It carries a $45 million signing bonus, a full no-trade clause and no opt-outs, with $30 million of the money deferred, and it is still subject to a successful physical. The large signing bonus also means a sizeable sum reaches him up front, before any stoppage begins.

Skubal, 30, won the Cy Young Award in 2024 and 2025 with the Detroit Tigers before the club sent him to the Los Angeles Dodgers in August in exchange for three prospects. He finished this season 12-7 with a 2.72 ERA across 26 starts, his fourth straight year with an ERA below 3.00, and helped the Dodgers reach the National League Championship Series, where Milwaukee swept them.

The Mets saw off the Dodgers, who had been widely tipped to keep him, as well as Toronto and San Diego, after Skubal met all four clubs over the past fortnight. The deal lands just before a lockout widely expected in early December, when owners are expected to halt offseason business in a dispute over team spending and a possible salary cap.

For the Mets, it is the second landmark outlay in two years under owner Steve Cohen, after the 15-year, $765 million deal for outfielder Juan Soto, the biggest contract in baseball history. The previous high for a pitcher was the $325 million, 12-year agreement Yoshinobu Yamamoto signed with the Dodgers in December 2023, a mark the Skubal deal beats by $125 million.

Key points

  • Tarik Skubal has agreed a nine-year, $450 million contract with the New York Mets, the biggest deal ever for a pitcher.
  • The contract averages $50 million a year and includes a $45 million signing bonus, a full no-trade clause and no opt-outs; it is subject to a physical.
  • Skubal, 30, won the Cy Young Award in 2024 and 2025 with Detroit and went 12-7 with a 2.72 ERA this season after his August trade to the Dodgers.
  • The Mets beat the Dodgers, Blue Jays and Padres to his signature, days before a lockout widely expected in early December.
