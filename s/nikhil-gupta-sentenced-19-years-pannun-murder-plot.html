Indian Man Gets 19 Years in US Prison for Plot to Kill Sikh Separatist in New York

A United States federal judge on Friday sentenced Indian national Nikhil Gupta to more than 19 years in prison for his part in a failed plan to kill a Sikh separatist leader on American soil, closing a case that has strained relations between Washington and New Delhi.

Judge Victor Marrero of the federal court in Manhattan set the term at 235 months, or 19 years and seven months. Gupta, 55, admitted guilt in February on three counts: arranging a killing for payment, conspiring to arrange such a killing, and conspiring to launder money.

The target of the plot was Gurpatwant Singh Pannun, a lawyer with the group Sikhs for Justice in New York who holds United States and Canadian citizenship and campaigns for an independent Sikh state in northern India. Prosecutors said Gupta worked with Vikash Yadav, described in court papers as an Indian government employee, who recruited him in May 2023. The pair arranged for 15,000 dollars in cash to be handed to a supposed hitman who was in fact an undercover American law enforcement officer.

Prosecutors had asked for a sentence of about 24 years. The term imposed sits at the bottom of the 235-to-293-month range set out in the federal sentencing guidelines. Addressing the court, Gupta said he regretted moving away from the nonviolent example of independence leader Mahatma Gandhi. Pannun, who was in the courtroom, said he was grateful the United States had prosecuted the case and would continue his campaign for Sikh sovereignty.

Yadav faces charges of his own and remains outside American custody. The case is one of several alleged plots against Sikh separatists in the United States and Canada that American and Canadian officials have described as part of a wider campaign against Indian dissidents abroad. Canada has separately accused Indian agents of involvement in the 2023 killing of Sikh activist Hardeep Singh Nijjar in British Columbia, an accusation India denies.

Key points

  • Nikhil Gupta, 55, was sentenced to 235 months, or 19 years and seven months, by a federal judge in Manhattan.
  • In February he admitted guilt on murder-for-hire and related conspiracy counts over the plan to kill Gurpatwant Singh Pannun in New York.
  • Prosecutors said Gupta worked with Indian government employee Vikash Yadav, and 15,000 dollars was paid to a supposed hitman who was an undercover US officer.
  • Pannun, a dual US-Canadian citizen and lawyer with Sikhs for Justice, was in court and said he was grateful for the prosecution; Yadav is charged but not in US custody.
