Second Tanker Hit Near Oman's Khasab as Two Vessels Are Struck Within an Hour

A second tanker was hit by an unidentified projectile near the Omani town of Khasab on Saturday morning, less than an hour after a first vessel was struck on its stern in the same waters, Britain's maritime trade office said, in the latest attacks on shipping leaving the Strait of Hormuz.

The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations agency said the first tanker was hit on its stern about 11 nautical miles north of Khasab at around 5am UTC. At about 5.50am UTC, a second outbound tanker was hit in its engine room about 17 nautical miles east of Khasab. No casualties were reported in either incident, and it was not yet known whether any oil had spilled. It was not immediately clear whether the two reports involved the same vessel.

The agency urged vessels to transit with caution while authorities investigate. Neither vessel was named, and no one was blamed for the strikes.

It was the third such strike reported in the strait in two days. On Friday, the same agency said a tanker had caught fire after being hit by an unknown projectile about eight nautical miles north of Khasab, and Iran's Revolutionary Guards said that vessel had struck a mine. Khasab is a town on the Musandam Peninsula in Oman, at the mouth of the strait, which carried almost a fifth of the world's seaborne crude oil before the war between the United States, Israel and Iran began, and traffic through it has been badly disrupted since Tehran moved to shut the waterway after fighting broke out in late February.

Key points

  • Two tankers were hit by unidentified projectiles near Khasab, Oman, on Saturday morning, about 50 minutes apart, the UK Maritime Trade Operations agency said.
  • The first was struck on its stern 11 nautical miles north of Khasab; the second was hit in its engine room 17 nautical miles east of the town.
  • No casualties were reported in either incident and it was not yet known whether any oil had spilled; it was not immediately clear whether the two reports involved the same vessel.
  • It was the third such strike in the strait in two days, after a tanker caught fire on Friday about eight nautical miles north of Khasab.
