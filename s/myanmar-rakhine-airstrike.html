Myanmar Airstrike Kills 12 at Displacement Camp in Rakhine, Including a Five-Year-Old

A military airstrike on a camp for displaced families in Myanmar's Rakhine state killed 12 people, including a five-year-old child, and wounded 18 others on Friday evening, according to witnesses and rescue workers.

The strike hit the Byar La camp in Ponnagyun township, north-east of the state capital Sittwe, on Friday evening. The camp had been home for about four years to families who fled the fighting in Rakhine, and the attack left its residents reeling.

Chit Su Wai, a 27-year-old shopkeeper who had lived at the camp for four years, said her daughter, aunt and mother were killed in the strike, and that the attack had broken her family. The death toll of 12 was confirmed by Khine Hein Htet, chairman of the Phyusin Yin Khwin Foundation, a rescue and emergency service, who said a five-year-old was among the dead and 18 people were also injured.

Rescue workers reached the camp after the strike, but the military could not be contacted for comment on the attack. The camp's residents had sought safety there from a war that has displaced communities across the state, and many had lived in its shelters for years.

Rakhine is among the regions worst hit by Myanmar's civil war, which began after the military seized power in 2021. The coastal state is now almost entirely controlled by the Arakan Army, an ethnic armed group, and has been cut off by a military blockade while coming under regular airstrikes.

The war has spread across much of the country since the coup, and the monitoring group ACLED estimates that more than 100,000 people have been killed on all sides. For the families at Byar La, the strike ended four years of relative shelter in a single evening.
