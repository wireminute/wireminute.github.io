Marquez Seals Record-Equalling Eighth MotoGP Title With Eighth Place in Portugal

Marc Marquez won the 2026 MotoGP world championship on Sunday, finishing eighth at the Portuguese Grand Prix to seal a record-equalling eighth premier-class title with one round still to run.

Marquez started second on the grid at Portimao and rode a careful race, knowing only a top-nine finish was needed to make the title mathematically certain. He brought his Ducati home in eighth, enough to clinch the crown while Marco Bezzecchi won the race for Aprilia, ahead of team-mate Jorge Martin and Trackhouse rider Raul Fernandez.

The eighth-place finish gave Marquez his eighth premier-class title, drawing him level with Giacomo Agostini, whose eight championships came between 1966 and 1975. It is his tenth world title across all classes and his second in a row, and it moves him past Valentino Rossi, who won seven premier-class crowns.

The title completes one of the great comebacks in the sport. Marquez trailed Bezzecchi by 102 points after the Italian Grand Prix at Mugello, the largest points deficit ever recovered in MotoGP, beating the previous record of 91 set by Francesco Bagnaia in 2022. At one stage he sat eighth in the standings, and he fought back from injuries and two operations that cost him two race weekends, the first champion to miss two Grands Prix on his way to the title since Kenny Roberts in 1979.

Marquez had come close to sealing the championship the day before, crashing out of the Saturday sprint with two laps remaining, and he had a first match point in Qatar that slipped away against the Aprilias at Lusail. On Sunday he refused to take risks, dropping back through the field after leading the opening lap and riding safely to the flag.

At 33 years and 278 days, Marquez is the oldest MotoGP champion since Phil Read in 1974 and the third-oldest ever. The span between his first premier-class title at Valencia in 2013 and this one at Portimao is 13 years and 12 days, the longest in the sport. He leads the standings on 439 points, 43 clear of Martin, with Bezzecchi two points further back. After the race, Agostini himself presented Marquez with a golden helmet, and the Spaniard said: “I don’t know what to say. I was over. I was over, and now I’m champion.”

Key points

  • Marc Marquez finished eighth at the Portuguese Grand Prix, enough to clinch the 2026 MotoGP title with one round to spare.
  • It is his eighth premier-class crown, equalling Giacomo Agostini's record, and his tenth world title across all classes.
  • He recovered from a 102-point deficit, the biggest points comeback in MotoGP history, after injuries and two operations.
  • Marco Bezzecchi won the race ahead of Jorge Martin and Raul Fernandez; Marquez, 33, is the oldest champion since 1974.
