129 Bodies Recovered From Rubble in Central Gaza After 53-Day Search

Rescue teams in central Gaza have recovered the remains of 129 people from destroyed buildings, closing a search that ran for 53 days, Gaza's civil defence said on Saturday.

The remains came from 39 buildings in Nuseirat camp and the nearby towns of az-Zawayda and Deir el-Balah, the agency said. Its central Gaza director, Rami al-Aidi, said 233 people had been reported missing under the rubble there, and search teams worked for 410 hours across 53 straight days.

The International Committee of the Red Cross paid for the recovery operation, which has now finished its second phase, the agency said. The recovery follows the return of residents to Nuseirat this week after an Israeli ground operation there, which the military said had ended on Thursday.

Key points

  • Search teams in central Gaza brought out the remains of 129 people from 39 wrecked buildings.
  • 233 people had been reported missing under the rubble, and crews searched for 410 hours over 53 days.
  • The Red Cross funded the operation, now through its second phase; the recovery follows this week's ground operation in Nuseirat.
