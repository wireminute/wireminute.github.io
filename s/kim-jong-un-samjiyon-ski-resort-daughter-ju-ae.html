Kim Jong Un Tours Ski Resort With Daughter Ju Ae Ahead of APEC Summit

North Korean leader Kim Jong Un inspected a mountain tourism zone near Mount Paektu with his daughter Ju Ae this week, days before leaders gather in China for a summit where the United States president is renewing his push for talks with Pyongyang.

State media reported on Tuesday that Kim spent Sunday and Monday touring construction sites in Samjiyon, in Ryanggang province near the Chinese border, accompanied by Ju Ae, his wife Ri Sol-ju, his sister Kim Yo-jong and senior officials. The tour covered the renovation and expansion of Samjiyon Airport, a ski resort under construction in the Bukpotaesan area, and hotels and other facilities for visitors.

Photos released by state media showed Ju Ae skiing down a slope in a white ski suit while her father watched from a chair at the resort. South Korean outlets focused on the outfit, estimating that the jacket and trousers alone cost about two million won, roughly 1,500 dollars, although the brand was not confirmed. Ju Ae, identified by South Korea's intelligence service as her father's likely successor, has appeared in public more often in recent years, including at missile launches and military events.

The visit to Mount Paektu carries weight beyond tourism. The mountain is closely tied to the ruling family's story and has often been the backdrop for major announcements and shifts in Pyongyang's foreign policy. The timing drew attention too: the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit opens in Shenzhen on Wednesday, where President Donald Trump is expected to meet China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin, and Trump has again said he wants to talk to Kim.

Kim directed officials to expand the airport runway to international standards and to press ahead with a tourist railway, hiking trails and accommodation, presenting the Samjiyon zone as a showcase for the country's tourism ambitions. Work on the sites is proceeding to annual plans, according to the state news agency, with construction priorities for next year now set.

Key points

  • Kim spent Sunday and Monday touring the Samjiyon tourism zone near Mount Paektu with his daughter Ju Ae, inspecting an airport expansion and a ski resort under construction
  • State media photos showed Ju Ae skiing in a white ski suit while her father watched, her latest high-profile public appearance
  • The trip comes days before the APEC summit in Shenzhen, where Trump is due to meet Xi and Putin and has renewed overtures for dialogue with Pyongyang
  • South Korea's intelligence service has identified Ju Ae as Kim's likely successor, and her public appearances have grown more frequent
