China Says Xi, Trump and Putin Will Discuss 'World Peace' at Shenzhen Summit

Beijing has said the first meeting of the three leaders will focus on world peace, giving the first outline of the agenda for Thursday's three-way summit in Shenzhen.

China's foreign ministry spokeswoman, Mao Ning, told reporters in Beijing on Monday that the three leaders would hold thorough talks on protecting world peace and keeping relations between the big powers stable. She called the meeting a moment of historic importance, hours after Beijing confirmed the meeting.

The meeting will be held on Thursday alongside the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit, and will be the first time the leaders of the world's three largest nuclear-armed states have sat down together. It is scheduled for 2:30 p.m. local time at Kylin Villa in Shenzhen. The White House and the Kremlin had already confirmed that Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin would attend, and Beijing called it a meeting of the three countries' heads of state.

Trump wants the talks to focus on cutting nuclear arsenals. The last treaty limiting the nuclear weapons of the United States and Russia expired in February, leaving no binding caps in place between the two largest nuclear powers. Senior diplomats from China and the United States have both raised reducing nuclear weapons as a possible topic, though American officials have cautioned that no agreement is likely and have called it a first conversation at leader level, not a negotiation.

China has long argued that the United States and Russia should cut their own far larger arsenals before Beijing joins talks on its own stockpile. That position sets up a difficult opening exchange, since Washington is pressing for a fresh conversation about cutting arsenals across the board.

The war in Iran is also expected to come up, officials said. Russia's war in Ukraine, by contrast, is not expected to be discussed. Germany's president, Frank-Walter Steinmeier, said Xi had shown irritation when the idea of the three leaders discussing Ukraine came up, telling him plainly that it was a European conflict in which Europeans must also sit at the table.

Trump will also hold a separate working lunch and meeting with Xi on Thursday, their fourth meeting since he returned to power. His Asia trip begins with a stop in the Philippines before he travels on to China, though the three-way meeting is now expected to overshadow the summit itself. No separate one-to-one meeting between Trump and Putin is scheduled, the White House said. Trump, however, told reporters as he left the White House that a meeting with North Korea's Kim Jong-un during the trip was also possible.

Key points

  • Beijing says the three leaders will discuss world peace in Shenzhen on Thursday, in thorough talks on protecting peace and keeping big-power relations stable.
  • It is the first three-way meeting of the leaders of the three largest nuclear-armed states, set for 2:30 p.m. local time at Kylin Villa.
  • Cutting nuclear arsenals is the main topic, with Chinese and American diplomats both raising the idea of talks on reducing weapons; China says the US and Russia must cut theirs first.
  • Iran is also on the agenda, while Russia's war in Ukraine is not expected to be discussed; Xi told Germany's president it is a European conflict in which Europeans must also be at the table.
