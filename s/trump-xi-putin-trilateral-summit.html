Trump, Xi and Putin to Hold First-Ever Three-Way Meeting in Shenzhen

The leaders of the United States, China and Russia will sit down together for the first time next week, the White House has said, with nuclear weapons at the top of the agenda.

The leaders of the United States, China and Russia will meet together for the first time next week, the White House has announced, in a three-way summit that puts nuclear weapons at the centre of the agenda. Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin will sit down on Thursday in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

The White House spokeswoman, Olivia Wales, confirmed the plan on Friday. It will be the first meeting of its kind between the leaders of the world's three biggest nuclear-armed states, and it comes as Washington pushes for a new conversation about cutting nuclear arsenals.

Trump wants the talks to focus on reducing nuclear stockpiles. The last nuclear arms treaty between the United States and Russia expired in February, leaving no binding limits in place between the two largest nuclear powers. American officials were quick to lower expectations, saying no agreement is likely to emerge and describing the meeting as an entry point — a first conversation at leader level rather than a negotiation.

The war in Iran is also expected to come up, officials said. Russia's war in Ukraine, by contrast, is not expected to be discussed — a notable omission, given that Trump last met Putin face to face in Alaska in August in an effort to broker an end to that war.

Trump will also hold a separate working lunch and meeting with Xi on Thursday, their fourth meeting since he returned to power. He hosted the Chinese leader at the White House in September. His Asia trip begins with a stop in the Philippines before he travels on to China for the summit, which brings together 21 economies — though the three-way meeting is now expected to overshadow the gathering itself.

Key points

  • Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen on Thursday, November 19, on the sidelines of the APEC summit — the first three-way meeting of the three leaders.
  • Cutting nuclear arsenals is the main topic; the last nuclear arms treaty between the United States and Russia expired in February.
  • Iran is also on the agenda, while Russia's war in Ukraine is not expected to be discussed.
  • American officials cautioned that no agreement is expected, calling the meeting a first conversation at leader level.
