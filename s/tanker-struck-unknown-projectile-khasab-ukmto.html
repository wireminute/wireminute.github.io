Tanker Struck by Unknown Projectile Near Khasab in Strait of Hormuz, Maritime Agency Says

An outbound tanker was struck by an unknown projectile while crossing the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday, about 18 nautical miles east of Khasab in Oman, the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations agency said.

The agency said the incident happened at 1 p.m. UTC, or 5 p.m. local time, and cited local authorities in its warning, numbered 223-26. The crew were accounted for and no environmental impact was reported. The agency did not name the tanker or say who fired the projectile, and advised vessels to transit with caution and report suspicious activity.

It is the latest in a run of attacks on shipping in the strait since the war between the United States, Israel and Iran began in late February, and the second reported close to Oman in as many days. A separate explosion from an unknown projectile was reported near a tanker about 50 nautical miles west of Dubai late on Wednesday, with the crew safe and no damage to the vessel.

Key points

  • An outbound tanker was struck by an unknown projectile about 18 nautical miles east of Khasab, Oman, on Wednesday at 1 p.m. UTC.
  • The crew were accounted for and no environmental impact was reported; the tanker was not identified.
  • The maritime agency advised vessels to transit with caution while authorities investigate.
