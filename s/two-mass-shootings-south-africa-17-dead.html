Two Mass Shootings in South Africa Leave 17 Dead

Two separate shootings in South Africa have left at least 17 people dead, police announced on Monday, in attacks about 500 kilometres apart whose motives remain unknown.

Nine people were discovered shot dead inside a shack in Jeffsville, an informal settlement in Atteridgeville west of Pretoria, early on Monday morning. Police said six of the dead were men and three were women. The shooting is believed to have happened on Sunday night, with no arrests made so far. Police did not immediately say whether any of the victims were children.

In the second attack, eight men were discovered dead inside a vehicle riddled with bullets in Sweetwaters, outside the city of Pietermaritzburg in KwaZulu-Natal province, after police answered reports of a shooting late on Sunday. All eight had multiple gunshot wounds and were pronounced dead at the scene. KwaZulu-Natal police spokesman Colonel Robert Netshiunda said the victims were still unidentified and that any talk of a motive was premature.

The two scenes lie roughly 536 kilometres apart, and police have not linked the attacks. Detectives are still trying to name the victims in both cases. The Jeffsville case is being handled by Gauteng province’s serious and violent crimes unit. Gauteng’s police commissioner, Tommy Mthombeni, suggested the killings could be connected to an earlier shooting at a nearby settlement in Saulsville last year, in which 12 people were killed and 13 injured.

South Africa records some of the highest murder rates in the world. Police figures released in August showed 5,427 people were murdered across the country between April and June this year, an average of about 60 a day. Such killings are commonly tied to the drugs trade, illegal mining or rivalry over minibus taxi routes. Two mass shootings a fortnight apart last December left 21 people dead.

Key points

  • Two separate shootings in South Africa have killed at least 17 people, police said on Monday; the motives for both attacks remain unknown.
  • Nine people, six men and three women, were discovered shot dead inside a shack in the Jeffsville settlement near Pretoria; no arrests have been made.
  • Eight men were discovered dead in a bullet-riddled vehicle in Sweetwaters, outside Pietermaritzburg; the victims have not yet been identified.
  • The attacks happened about 536 kilometres apart. South Africa averages about 60 murders a day, and two mass shootings a fortnight apart last December left 21 dead.
