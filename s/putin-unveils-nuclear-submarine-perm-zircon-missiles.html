Putin Unveils Nuclear Submarine Armed With Hypersonic Missiles

President Vladimir Putin has unveiled a new nuclear-powered submarine that Moscow says is the first equipped with Zircon hypersonic missiles, attending a flag-raising ceremony at the Sevmash shipyard in Severodvinsk on Friday as the vessel formally joined the Russian navy.

Putin attended the ceremony aboard the Perm, a fourth-generation Yasen-M class submarine, marking its formal transfer to the navy, according to video released by the Kremlin. "Russia is one of the few countries in the world capable of building powerful nuclear submarines of this kind, weapons with global reach and significance," he said.

The Perm is the first submarine to carry Zircon hypersonic cruise missiles as standard armament, Moscow says. Russia describes the Zircon as flying at up to nine times the speed of sound with a range of about 1,000 kilometres, figures that have not been independently verified. The vessel's keel was laid in July 2016 and it was launched in March 2025.

Putin said the navy would receive five more missile-carrying submarines by 2030, including three more of the Yasen-M class, and that the Perm would shortly join the Pacific Fleet. He said the submarines could operate in "any part of the world's oceans" while carrying a range of cruise missiles.

The commissioning comes as Russia continues its war in Ukraine and as tensions with the West run high over the conflict. The Yasen-M class is the backbone of Russia's modern attack submarine force, designed to operate covertly and to strike targets at sea and on land.

The Perm is the fifth Yasen-M boat delivered to the navy in the past six years, and the sixth of the Yasen and Yasen-M classes built at Sevmash. The class carries cruise missiles in vertical launch tubes as well as torpedoes, and is intended to replace older Soviet-era attack submarines.

Key points

  • Putin attended a flag-raising ceremony aboard the Perm at the Sevmash shipyard in Severodvinsk on Friday, marking the submarine's formal entry into service with the Russian navy.
  • Moscow says the Perm is the first submarine equipped with Zircon hypersonic cruise missiles as standard, with a claimed speed of up to Mach 9 and a range of about 1,000 kilometres.
  • Putin said five more missile-carrying submarines would be delivered by 2030, including three more Yasen-M boats, and that the Perm would shortly join the Pacific Fleet.
  • The Perm is a fourth-generation Yasen-M class nuclear-powered submarine; its keel was laid in 2016 and it was launched in March 2025.
