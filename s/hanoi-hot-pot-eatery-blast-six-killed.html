Six Killed as Suspected Gas Blast Flattens Hanoi Hot Pot Eatery

An explosion at a hot pot restaurant in Vietnam's capital, Hanoi, killed six people and injured more than a dozen on Tuesday morning, in a blast officials suspect was caused by leaking cooking gas.

The blast struck at about 8am local time, as the restaurant on Nguyen Van Tuyet Street in the Dong Da district was opening for the day. The force of the explosion partly brought down the single-storey building, which sits behind an apartment block, and left the ground-floor eatery almost completely collapsed. Windows in nearby buildings shattered, metal shutters were ripped off, and roofs and awnings caved in. The restaurant was full of people when the blast hit, according to local reports.

Rescue teams spent hours digging through the rubble, with fears that more people could still be trapped underneath. Dozens of firefighters were sent to the scene, and the city's mayor visited the site. By early afternoon, the city said six people had died and 10 were in intensive care in three hospitals, while other reports put the number taken to hospital at more than 14, including one person with a serious head injury.

People living nearby described a flash of light followed by a deafening noise, with doors, glass and debris thrown across the narrow street. Cars and motorbikes parked outside were damaged or knocked over, and rubble was scattered across the road. Officials later moved residents out of neighbouring buildings judged unsafe after the blast.

Police said early findings pointed to a gas explosion, and the city's governing committee ordered an investigation into what caused it, who was responsible and whether any safety rules had been broken. The cause has not yet been formally confirmed, and the story is still developing.

Key points

  • Six people were killed and more than a dozen injured when a blast hit a hot pot restaurant in Hanoi on Tuesday morning.
  • The explosion, suspected to have been caused by leaking cooking gas, partly collapsed the single-storey building in the Dong Da district.
  • Rescue teams spent hours searching the rubble for people feared trapped, and 10 of the injured were in intensive care.
  • Police are investigating the cause, and residents of nearby buildings judged unsafe have been moved out.
