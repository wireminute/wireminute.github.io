Australia Sanctions Three Israelis and Four Groups Over West Bank Settlement Expansion

Australia has imposed sanctions on three Israeli individuals and four organisations over illegal settlement expansion and settler violence in the occupied West Bank, saying the actions are extinguishing the prospect of a two-state solution.

Australia announced the measures on Wednesday, a day after Britain sanctioned eight Israeli construction companies involved in building settlements in the occupied West Bank. Foreign Minister Penny Wong said the people and groups targeted had taken part in planning, coordinating and setting up new settlements, including raising funds, recruiting volunteers and supporting violent settlers.

Among those sanctioned is Daniella Weiss, the leader of the settler organisation Nachala and a prominent advocate for the expulsion of Palestinians from Gaza. Another is Eliav Libi, who the government said had accelerated the expansion of settlements in the West Bank through his construction company. The measures bar Australians and Australian businesses from dealing with, or providing support to, those named. The sanctioned organisations include groups involved in fundraising and recruitment for new settlements, according to the government's statement.

Wong said she had spoken to Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Saar about the decision. The sanctions follow earlier Australian measures, including sanctions on seven settlers and one organisation in July 2024 and, in June 2025, on two Israeli ministers, Itamar Ben-Gvir and Bezalel Smotrich, for inciting violence against Palestinians. Amnesty International Australia welcomed the latest step but said stronger action was urgently needed.

The move comes as international pressure grows over settlement building in the West Bank, where Israel has pressed ahead with construction including the contentious E1 project east of Jerusalem. Australia has said the expansion of settlements and settler violence are undermining the prospect of a negotiated two-state solution, a position it has repeated as the conflict in the region continues.

Key points

  • Australia sanctioned three Israeli individuals and four organisations over illegal settlement expansion and settler violence in the West Bank.
  • Daniella Weiss of the settler group Nachala and Eliav Libi, linked to settlement construction, are among those named; Australians are barred from dealing with them.
  • The move follows Britain's decision on Tuesday to sanction eight Israeli construction companies building settlements in the West Bank.
