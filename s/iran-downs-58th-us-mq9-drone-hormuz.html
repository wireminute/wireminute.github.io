Iran Claims Fourth US Drone Downed in 24 Hours, a 20th MQ-1 Over Hormuz

Iran's Revolutionary Guards say their air defences brought down a fourth American drone in 24 hours, an MQ-1 over the eastern Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, in the latest in a run of claims from the war that the United States has not confirmed.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said on Sunday that their air defences had shot down an American MQ-1 drone east of the Strait of Hormuz, the fourth American drone they say they have downed in 24 hours. The Guards said the aircraft was an extended-range version of the MQ-1 and the 20th of the type they had brought down. The claim was carried by the state broadcaster IRIB. The Guards did not give the exact position of the interception or release images of wreckage, and the United States military had not addressed the claim by midday on Sunday. The claim could not be independently verified.

The Guards' Aerospace Force had said on Saturday that its air defence units intercepted an MQ-9 Reaper in the early hours of the morning and destroyed it with a newly deployed advanced air defence system. Iranian state broadcaster IRIB carried the claim, which put the total at 58 MQ-9s brought down by Iranian forces. No further details were given about where exactly the interception happened, and the United States had not confirmed the claim.

Later on Saturday, the Guards said a second American drone, an MQ-1 Predator, had been brought down over Qeshm Island in the strait. The claim was made in a statement from the Guards, and no further details were given about the interception. The United States had not confirmed either claim, and neither had been independently verified.

The claims come as the war between Iran and the United States and Israel grinds on, and three days after Iran said it had downed another MQ-9 near the strait. Shipping through the waterway has also come under attack this week, with two tankers struck near the Omani town of Khasab on Saturday morning. Iran's foreign minister said on Friday that the strait could reopen within two hours if the United States met Tehran's demands, including an end to the fighting, the lifting of oil sanctions and an end to the naval blockade of Iranian ports, while a US State Department spokesman said American economic pressure on Iran was working and would compound over time. The war began in late February, and the strait carried almost a fifth of the world's seaborne crude oil before the fighting, with traffic through it badly disrupted since Tehran moved to shut the waterway. The story is still developing.

Key points

  • Iran's Revolutionary Guards say they downed a US MQ-1 drone east of the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, the 20th of the type and the fourth American drone in 24 hours.
  • On Saturday the Guards said they had downed an MQ-9 Reaper in the early hours and an MQ-1 Predator over Qeshm Island later in the day.
  • The Guards say a newly deployed advanced air defence system was used. The claims were carried by Iranian state media; the United States has not confirmed them and they have not been independently verified.
  • The claims come during the continuing war between Iran and the United States and Israel, with shipping through the strait disrupted and two tankers struck near Khasab on Saturday.
