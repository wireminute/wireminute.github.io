Iran Says It Downed a 58th American MQ-9 Drone Above the Strait of Hormuz

Iran's Revolutionary Guards say their air defences brought down an American MQ-9 Reaper drone above the Strait of Hormuz early on Saturday, and claim it is the 58th US drone they have downed during the war.

The Guards' Aerospace Force said in a statement that its air defence units intercepted the aircraft in the early hours of the morning and destroyed it with a newly deployed advanced air defence system. Iranian state broadcaster IRIB carried the claim, which put the total at 58 MQ-9s brought down by Iranian forces. No further details were given about where exactly the interception happened, and the United States had not confirmed the claim.

The claim comes as the war between Iran and the United States and Israel grinds on, and three days after Iran said it had downed another MQ-9 near the strait. Shipping through the waterway has also come under attack this week, with two tankers struck near the Omani town of Khasab on Saturday morning.

Key points

  • Iran's Revolutionary Guards say their air defences downed an American MQ-9 drone above the Strait of Hormuz early on Saturday, the 58th such aircraft they claim to have brought down.
  • The Guards said a newly deployed advanced air defence system was used. The claim was carried by Iranian state media and has not been independently confirmed.
  • The claim comes during the continuing war between Iran and the United States and Israel, three days after Iran said it downed another MQ-9 near the strait.
