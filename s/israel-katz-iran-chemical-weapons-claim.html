Israel Says Iran Is Developing Chemical Weapons and Plotting to Kill Trump

Israel's defence minister said on Tuesday that Iran has begun developing chemical weapons since the war in February, and claimed Tehran has ordered the killing of the US president and members of his family.

Israel Katz told reporters in Tel Aviv that Iran was working to build a chemical weapons capability to offset Israel's military advantage after the damage it suffered in the February war. He said both Israel and the United States were aware of the programme and that Israel would not allow Tehran to acquire the capability, but he did not say how far the work had advanced or where it was taking place.

Katz also claimed Tehran had ordered the assassination of the American president, his family and senior figures in his government. He said Israel and the United States were preparing for a possible joint attack, that plans for a joint operation with US Central Command were ready, and that the decision to launch it rested with Trump. He added that Israel's preference was to act together with the Americans, and raised the possibility of a pre-emptive strike if Israel judged Iran was about to attack.

The warning came after days of reports about the programme. US media said Mossad chief Roman Gofman had shared intelligence on the programme with senior officials in Washington about two weeks ago, and Katz's remarks landed amid debate in both countries over whether the allies should go to war with Iran for a third time. Katz also said Iran had not restarted its nuclear programme since the June 2025 strikes on its main sites, though there were signs it was trying to. A senior Israeli defence official had said earlier this month that Tehran had no chemical weapons capacity that could yet threaten Israel.

Iran rejected the claims. Its foreign ministry spokesman called the reports fraudulent and accused the United States of building a case for an extended war. At home, opposition figure Gadi Eisenkot said he saw no immediate threat that required action before a new government was formed, and pointed instead to the 440 kilograms of enriched uranium Iran still holds, which has yet to be destroyed or removed.

Key points

  • Israel's defence minister said Iran began developing chemical weapons after the February war and vowed Israel would not let Tehran acquire the capability.
  • Katz claimed Iran has ordered the assassination of the US president, his family and senior officials in his administration.
  • He said joint attack plans with US Central Command are ready, with the decision resting with Trump, and raised a pre-emptive strike.
  • Iran denied the claims as fraudulent; Israeli officials acknowledge Iran cannot yet strike Israel with chemical weapons.
