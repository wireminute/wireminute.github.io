Bellingham Plays Down Zidane Comparisons After Wondergoal Sparks Real Madrid Comeback

Jude Bellingham came off the bench to score a stunning volley that turned Real Madrid's Champions League tie against PSV Eindhoven, then waved away comparisons with Zinedine Zidane, the club great whose shirt number he wears.

Real Madrid trailed 1-0 at half-time at the Santiago Bernabeu on Tuesday, and Bellingham was sent on at the break to change the game. Federico Valverde levelled the score in the 50th minute, and ten minutes later Bellingham struck, controlling a pass from Kylian Mbappe in the air and volleying from outside the area with the outside of his right foot into the far corner. Mbappe added two more goals and Vinicius Junior completed a 5-1 win.

The strike was the moment of the night. PSV manager Peter Bosz admitted he had tried to work out how the goal could have been stopped and could not find an answer. It was Bellingham's 11th Champions League goal for Real Madrid, more than all other English players for the club combined have managed since the competition took its current form in 1992.

Bellingham himself was at a loss to explain the finish. "I have no idea what I did," he said, adding that he had been surprised to take the shot at all and that the ball had popped up nicely, so he simply decided to hit it. He said the goal mattered because it put Madrid in front and gave the team control of the match.

The comparisons with Zidane followed at once, not least because Bellingham wears the No. 5 shirt the Frenchman made famous at Madrid. He would have none of it. "Zidane is much better than me. He's much more elegant and he's my idol," he said. Manager Jose Mourinho called him the best midfielder in the world, while Bellingham credited Mourinho with helping him find his best form. The win was Madrid's 10th in a row, and Bellingham has now scored or assisted in six of his last seven matches for club and country.

Key points

  • Jude Bellingham came off the bench at half-time and scored a stunning volley as Real Madrid came from 1-0 down to beat PSV Eindhoven 5-1 in the Champions League.
  • The 23-year-old controlled Kylian Mbappe's pass in the air and struck from outside the area with the outside of his right foot, then admitted he had no idea how he scored it.
