Iranian Attack Sparks Fire at Civilian Industrial Site in Bahrain, Ministry Says

Bahrain said late on Saturday that an Iranian attack had struck a civilian industrial facility and started a fire, the latest strike on the Gulf state as the war across the region spreads.

Bahrain's interior ministry said the attack was part of what it called Iranian aggression, and that civil defence teams were tackling the blaze. Early reports pointed to damage at the site, it said, with no injuries reported. Officials gave no name for the site and no location.

Residents told reporters they saw smoke rising over Sitra Island, home to several industrial plants. Bahrain's military said its air defence systems had shot down several incoming Iranian attacks, and the ministry had earlier told residents to take shelter against a possible threat. Bahrain later issued an all-clear notice.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said the operation used missiles and drones against American military positions at two bases in Bahrain. Iran's state news agency, meanwhile, said a US interceptor missile had come down on Sitra Island and set off the blaze, a conflicting account of how the blaze began.

Key points

  • Bahrain's interior ministry said an Iranian attack struck a civilian industrial facility and started a fire, with civil defence teams tackling the blaze and no injuries reported.
  • The ministry did not name the facility or its location; residents reported smoke rising over Sitra Island, home to several industrial plants.
  • Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they targeted American military positions at two bases in Bahrain, while Iran's state news agency blamed an American interceptor missile for the fire on Sitra Island.
