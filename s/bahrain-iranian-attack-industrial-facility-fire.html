Two Wounded in Iranian Attacks on Bahrain, One in Critical Condition, Ministry Says

Bahrain said on Sunday that two people were wounded in Iranian attacks on the kingdom the day before, the first casualties reported from the strikes, with one of them in critical condition in hospital.

Bahrain's interior ministry said on Sunday that two people were wounded in Iranian attacks on the kingdom on Saturday, the first casualties reported from the strikes on the country. One of the wounded is a Bahraini national with a disability, whose condition is stable, and the other is a Pakistani resident, who is in critical condition. Both are receiving treatment in hospital. The ministry had said on Saturday that an Iranian attack had set a civilian industrial facility on fire and caused damage, but it reported no injuries at that point, so the Sunday statement revises the earlier account. Residents told reporters they had seen smoke rising over Sitra Island, east of Manama, which hosts several industrial plants including a refinery run by the state oil company.

The foreign ministry condemned the attacks and said the Revolutionary Guards had publicly claimed responsibility for the strike on the civilian industrial facility. Bahrain has asked the United Nations Security Council to act, demanded that Iran be held accountable and pay full compensation for the attacks, and Saudi Arabia also denounced the strikes. The defence force said on Saturday that its air defences had intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks, and that falling debris had caused material damage but no casualties at the time.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said their forces had fired missiles and drones at American positions in Bahrain, claiming to have hit two Patriot radar systems at the al-Riffa and Awali bases. Bahrain's foreign ministry rejected that claim, saying the site hit was a civilian industrial facility. An earlier Iranian report had named two different bases, and neither side's account of what was hit has been independently verified. The facility that caught fire has still not been named and its location has not been given.

The attacks came as the war between Iran and the United States and Israel continued, with shipping in the Strait of Hormuz also under attack. Bahrain, home to the US Navy's Fifth Fleet, has repeatedly come under attack in recent days, and Iranian strikes on the kingdom resumed on November 13 after a lull since early September. The story is still developing.

Key points

  • Two people were wounded in Saturday's Iranian attacks on Bahrain, the interior ministry said on Sunday: a Bahraini national with a disability, in stable condition, and a Pakistani resident, in critical condition.
  • The ministry had said on Saturday that the attack on a civilian industrial facility caused a fire and damage but no injuries; the Sunday statement is the first report of casualties.
  • Bahrain's foreign ministry condemned the attacks, demanded Iran be held accountable and pay compensation, and asked the UN Security Council to act; Saudi Arabia also denounced the strikes.
  • Iran's Revolutionary Guards claim they hit two US Patriot radars at al-Riffa and Awali; Bahrain says the site hit was civilian. Neither account has been independently verified.
