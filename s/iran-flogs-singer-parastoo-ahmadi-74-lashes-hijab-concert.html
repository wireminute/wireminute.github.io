Iran Flogs Singer Parastoo Ahmadi 74 Times Over Hijab-Free Online Concert

Iranian authorities have carried out a sentence of 74 lashes on singer Parastoo Ahmadi and four members of her production team over an online concert in which she performed without the mandatory hijab, her lawyer said on Monday.

Ahmadi, 29, and the four others reported to the sentence enforcement unit of the prosecutor's office in the central city of Qom, where the sentences were carried out, lawyer Mostafa Nili told the Iranian news outlet Emtedad. Each of the five received 74 lashes. The others were named as Soheil Faqih-Nasiri, Amin Taheri, Amirali Pirnia and Mohsen Lashkari.

The case dates to a concert Ahmadi filmed on 11 December 2024 at the Deir-e Gachin caravanserai, a historic trading post outside Qom, and posted to her YouTube channel. She sang without a headscarf, in a sleeveless dress, alongside male musicians, to an empty hall. The video, which became known as the Imaginary Concert, was viewed millions of times. In June, a court in Qom convicted Ahmadi and eight members of her band and production team of offending public decency by producing and publishing vulgar and immoral content online. The sentence was upheld on appeal before it was carried out.

Alongside the lashes, the five were barred from artistic and musical work and from travelling abroad for two years. It was not immediately clear what happened to the other four people sentenced in June. Iranian law requires women to wear the hijab in public, and women have been effectively banned from singing solo in public since the 1979 revolution.

Key points

  • Parastoo Ahmadi, 29, and four members of her production team each received 74 lashes in Qom on Monday, her lawyer Mostafa Nili said.
  • The punishment followed her December 2024 online concert, filmed without an audience at a historic caravanserai, in which she sang without a hijab alongside male musicians.
