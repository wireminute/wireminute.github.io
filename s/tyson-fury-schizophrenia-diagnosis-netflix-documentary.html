Tyson Fury Reveals Schizophrenia Diagnosis in Documentary Ahead of Joshua Fight

Former heavyweight champion Tyson Fury has revealed he was diagnosed with schizophrenia after winning the world title in 2015, speaking publicly about the diagnosis for the first time in a new documentary.

Fury, 38, makes the disclosure in Tyson Fury, a documentary directed by Lucy Wilcox that premieres on Netflix on Friday. He was diagnosed with schizophrenia soon after beating Wladimir Klitschko in November 2015 to become unified heavyweight champion, and later also received a diagnosis of bipolar disorder. "Words can’t explain the darkness I was in. I came from the living version of the pits of hellfire. And I 100% thought at times I’d end up in a padded room with the door locked, and that scared me so much," he says in the film.

He describes waking in the night with crippling anxiety, hearing voices in his mind, including threats against himself and his family, and believing messages were being sent to him through the radio. In June 2016 he was taken to hospital with a suspected heart attack that doctors determined was an anxiety attack, and psychiatric assessments followed. "I was waking up in the night like this, the anxiety was crippling me. I just could not overcome it. Completely gone, and that’s when I went and saw the psychiatrist," he says.

The period derailed his career. He withdrew from a scheduled Klitschko rematch after being declared medically unfit, relinquished his world titles, and spent nearly three years out of the ring as his weight rose and he struggled with alcohol. His wife Paris says in the film: "He’s a mild schizophrenic and even then the penny didn’t drop." His father John says his mental health problems have always been there. Fury returned to boxing in June 2018 and rebuilt his career.

The disclosure comes two weeks before the biggest fight of his recent career, against Anthony Joshua in Cardiff on December 11, a long-awaited all-British heavyweight meeting. Fury has won two fights in 2026 since returning to action, and holds a record of 36 wins, two defeats and one draw, with 25 knockouts.
