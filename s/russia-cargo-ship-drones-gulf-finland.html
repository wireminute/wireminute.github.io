Finland Says Ukraine Likely Behind Drone Strike on Russian Cargo Ship as Crew Evacuates

Finland said on Saturday that Ukraine was likely behind the drone strike that set a Russian cargo ship on fire in the Gulf of Finland, as the crew evacuated the burning vessel and a Russian helicopter was suspected of violating Finnish airspace.

Finland's Defence Forces said on Saturday afternoon that the drone strike on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland was likely carried out by Ukraine, hours after the vessel caught fire and drifted into Finnish waters. Armed forces spokesperson Aki-Mauri Huhtinen told a press conference it was likely Ukraine was behind the attack, though it was not clear why this particular ship had been targeted. Ukraine has not claimed responsibility for the strike.

The crew was evacuating the burning vessel into life rafts and a rescue boat after the ship anchored in Finnish waters south of Loviisa, the Finnish Border Guard said. Earlier the Border Guard had said the crew had not been forced to abandon ship, but coast guard commander Mikko Simola later said the vessel had anchored and the evacuation was under way. There were no immediate reports of casualties.

Finland's Defence Ministry also reported a suspected violation of Finnish airspace by a Russian helicopter, which flew more than four kilometres into Finnish territory. Defence Minister Antti Hakkanen said the suspected violation was possibly linked to rescue or tracking operations for the damaged ship. The Border Guard is investigating the incident.

The Sparta II, bound from St Petersburg to Kaliningrad, was hit by two drones in international waters west of Gogland Island, according to Russian officials, and Finland has imposed temporary air and sea restrictions in the eastern gulf. The vessel is under US sanctions and belongs to a company linked to Russia's Defence Ministry. The Border Guard said there was no confirmed information about its cargo, but military materiel could not be ruled out. Prime Minister Petteri Orpo said the war was spilling over into Finland's neighbourhood.

Key points

  • Finland's Defence Forces said the drone strike on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II was likely carried out by Ukraine, though the reason the ship was targeted is unclear.
  • The crew is evacuating the burning vessel into life rafts after it anchored in Finnish waters; no casualties have been reported.
  • A Russian helicopter is suspected of violating Finnish airspace by more than four kilometres, possibly linked to rescue or tracking of the damaged ship.
  • The Sparta II, under US sanctions and linked to Russia's Defence Ministry, was sailing from St Petersburg to Kaliningrad; its cargo is unknown and military materiel cannot be ruled out.
