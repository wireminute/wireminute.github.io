Man Sentenced to More Than 27 Years Over Iran-Backed Plot to Kill Trump

A Pakistani man has been sentenced to more than 27 years in an American prison for plotting to kill Donald Trump and other leading politicians in 2024, in a plan prosecutors said was ordered by Iran.

Asif Merchant, 48, was sentenced on Tuesday by Judge Eric R. Komitee in a federal court in Brooklyn to 330 months in prison, after a jury convicted him in March on terrorism and murder-for-hire charges. Prosecutors said he travelled to the United States to recruit people to carry out killings on American soil. The sentence of 330 months is equal to 27 and a half years.

The targets of the plot included Trump, then the leading Republican candidate for president, along with Joe Biden, then the president, and Nikki Haley, who was challenging Trump for the party's nomination, prosecutors said. They said the killings were planned as revenge for the American strike that killed Iranian commander Qassem Soleimani in 2020, during Trump's first term in office.

Merchant flew to the United States in April 2024, after meeting his handler in Tehran about 15 times over the previous year and a half. He set out the plan to an acquaintance, who reported him to the FBI and became an informant. He then met undercover agents posing as hitmen, described the killing of a politician at a rally, and paid them $5,000 as a first instalment. He was arrested in July 2024 in Richmond, Texas, as he was about to board a flight out of the country, and the plot was stopped before any attack took place.

Prosecutors said Merchant had been recruited by Iran's Revolutionary Guard in about 2022, after he began travelling to Iran on religious pilgrimages, and was trained in counterintelligence before being sent to the United States. At trial, Merchant said he had taken part in the plot unwillingly, saying he wanted to protect his family in Tehran. The trial opened in late February, days before the United States and Israel began their military offensive against Iran. Iran's government says it never planned any attack on Trump or other American officials.

Key points

  • Asif Merchant, 48, was sentenced in Brooklyn to 330 months, more than 27 years, for a 2024 plot to kill Donald Trump and other American politicians.
  • Prosecutors said the plot was ordered by Iran as revenge for the 2020 killing of commander Qassem Soleimani, and the targets also included Joe Biden and Nikki Haley.
  • A jury convicted him in March after a trial that began in late February; he testified he joined the plot unwillingly to protect his family in Tehran.
  • Merchant paid $5,000 to undercover agents posing as hitmen and was arrested in July 2024 in Texas before any attack took place.
