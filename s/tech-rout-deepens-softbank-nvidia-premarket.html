Nasdaq Falls 4% as AI Sell-Off Hits Wall Street and Nvidia Drops Again

Wall Street's technology sell-off deepened on Wednesday as the Nasdaq fell about 4 percent and Nvidia lost nearly 9 percent, with stubborn inflation and new tariff proposals adding to the pressure on investors.

The Nasdaq Composite fell about 4 percent to around 22,790 by midday in New York, while the S&P 500 dropped close to 3 percent toward 7,000 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average lost more than 1,000 points. The technology-heavy Nasdaq-100 fell 4.3 percent and at one point stood 20 percent below its record closing high from early October, a level that would mark a bear market if it held into the close. A widely watched gauge of expected market volatility rose above 30, its highest in weeks.

Nvidia, the chipmaker at the centre of the artificial intelligence trade, fell 8.7 percent, extending a 5.9 percent drop on Tuesday and giving back more of Monday's 31.5 percent surge after Jensen Huang returned as chief executive. Advanced Micro Devices fell 9.6 percent, memory maker Micron dropped 10 percent and Broadcom lost 6.3 percent, while Microsoft fell 3.7 percent. An index of semiconductor shares fell to its lowest in more than seven months. Anthropic, the maker of the Claude chatbot, fell about 8 percent to below its listing price only days after its stock market debut.

Investors are questioning whether the huge sums being spent on data centres and other AI infrastructure will ever pay off, and worries are growing about how much of that spending is funded by borrowing. Doubts about governance at Nvidia have added to the unease, with Huang due to appear before congressional committees next week, while a stronger-than-expected showing from Dell failed to lift its shares.

Fresh US inflation figures added to the gloom. Consumer prices rose 0.3 percent in October from the month before and 3.8 percent from a year earlier, while core prices, which strip out food and energy, were up 3.3 percent on the year. The figures strengthened bets that the Federal Reserve will raise interest rates when it meets in December. Proposed new tariffs on imports from 14 trading partners also weighed on sentiment.

The selling began in Asia, where SoftBank fell about 15 percent in Tokyo, its second near-15 percent fall in four sessions and among its worst in more than two decades. Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 3.6 percent and South Korea's Kospi dropped about 6 percent, with chipmakers SK Hynix, Samsung, Advantest and Kioxia all down sharply. US markets are closed on Thursday for the Thanksgiving holiday.

Key points

  • The Nasdaq fell about 4 percent and the S&P 500 close to 3 percent as the sell-off in artificial intelligence shares spread across Wall Street.
  • Nvidia dropped nearly 9 percent, with AMD, Micron and Broadcom also down sharply and semiconductor shares at a seven-month low.
  • US inflation stayed hot, with consumer prices up 3.8 percent on the year, lifting expectations of a December interest rate rise.
  • The rout began in Asia, where SoftBank sank 15 percent, the Nikkei fell 3.6 percent and South Korea's Kospi dropped 6 percent.
