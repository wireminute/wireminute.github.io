ADNOC Ship Attacked in Strait of Hormuz as One Crew Member Goes Missing

A vessel belonging to the shipping arm of the United Arab Emirates' state oil company was hit while crossing the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday evening, after one crew member went missing and 11 were hurt, the company said.

ADNOC Logistics and Services said the attack happened as its vessel was passing through the strait on the evening of 17 November. Search and rescue work is under way with the relevant authorities, and every other crew member has been taken off the vessel. Eleven were hurt and are getting medical care where needed. The company did not name the vessel, say what struck it, or blame anyone for the attack.

The attack is the latest strike on commercial shipping in the strait, where several vessels have been hit in recent days as fighting between the United States, Israel and Iran has spread across the Gulf. The Tara Gas, another gas tanker, was hit in the same waterway on Tuesday; one of its crew is unaccounted for and two were taken to hospital. The company said it would share more information as it becomes available.

Key points

  • A vessel operated by ADNOC Logistics and Services came under attack while crossing the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday evening.
  • One crew member is missing and search and rescue work is under way; 11 others were injured and the rest of the crew have been taken off the vessel.
  • The company did not name the vessel or say who was behind the attack; the Tara Gas, another gas tanker, was also hit in the strait on Tuesday.
