Robert Pattinson Named the New James Bond, Seventh Actor to Play 007

Robert Pattinson has been confirmed as the next James Bond, ending five years of speculation over who would succeed Daniel Craig as the world's most famous secret agent.

Amazon MGM Studios announced the casting on Monday in a social media post, sharing a short video that showed Pattinson in a tuxedo holding a gun and ended with the words 'James Bond will return.' The 40-year-old London-born actor becomes the seventh performer to play the agent in the official film series, following Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Craig.

In a statement, Pattinson said being entrusted with 007 was the honour of a lifetime. The next film, the 26th in the series, is due in cinemas in 2028, seven years after Craig's final outing, No Time to Die, in 2021. The gap is the longest between films in the franchise's history.

The film will be directed by Denis Villeneuve, the filmmaker behind Dune and Blade Runner 2049, from a screenplay by Steven Knight, the creator of Peaky Blinders. Villeneuve said Pattinson brought a spirit that was completely new to the role, and praised the talent around the production. No title or exact release date has been announced.

The casting ends one of the longest-running guessing games in cinema. Actors including Josh O'Connor, Callum Turner and Jack Lowden were reported to have reached the final screen tests. Pattinson, known for Twilight and The Batman, said 14 years ago that he wanted the role one day, but not for another 20 years. The wait turned out to be much shorter.

It will be the first Bond film made under Amazon MGM's full creative control, after longtime producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson stepped back. The announcement came on the 20th anniversary of the British release of Casino Royale, the film that introduced Craig as Bond. Pattinson is also currently working on The Batman Part II and stars in Villeneuve's Dune: Part Three.

Key points

  • Robert Pattinson, 40, has been confirmed as the seventh actor to play James Bond in the official film series.
