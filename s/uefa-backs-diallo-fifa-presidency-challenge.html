UEFA Picks Diallo to Take On Infantino in FIFA Presidential Election

European football's governing body has unanimously backed French football chief Philippe Diallo to challenge Gianni Infantino for the presidency of FIFA, giving the incumbent his first serious rival in next March's election.

UEFA said on Monday that the national associations represented on its executive committee and the FIFA Council had invited Diallo to stand as a candidate in the election in March 2027 and had unanimously pledged their full support. The meeting was chaired by UEFA president Aleksander Ceferin. Diallo, 63, has accepted the invitation, and his candidacy is due to be confirmed by the French Football Federation's executive committee on Tuesday.

The election will be held at the FIFA Congress in Rabat, Morocco, on 18 March 2027. Candidates must declare themselves by Wednesday, 18 November, with the backing of at least five of FIFA's 211 member associations. UEFA, which has 55 member associations, had struggled for months to find a credible candidate willing to stand against Infantino, who is seeking a fourth term.

Diallo has led the French federation since January 2023, after nearly two decades leading the body representing France's professional clubs. In September, the French federation withdrew its backing for Infantino's re-election, with Diallo saying the trust placed in the FIFA president had been broken.

Opposition to Infantino has grown since July, when he put forward a plan to bring private investors into a new FIFA commercial company holding rights linked to competitions including the World Cup, a proposal that was later abandoned. UEFA said its members wanted a change in the way world football's governing body operates, calling for greater unity, trust and accountability. Germany coach Jurgen Klopp said on Sunday that FIFA had become worse under Infantino than under his predecessor Sepp Blatter. Before Diallo, only former Swiss player Ramon Vega and Japan's Kozo Tashima had declared they would stand, while African football's governing body reaffirmed its backing for Infantino in October.

Key points

  • UEFA has unanimously backed Philippe Diallo to challenge Gianni Infantino for the FIFA presidency in March 2027.
  • Diallo, 63, has led the French Football Federation since 2023 and accepted the invitation; the federation is due to confirm his candidacy on Tuesday.
  • Candidates must declare by Wednesday 18 November with the backing of five member associations; the vote takes place in Rabat on 18 March 2027.
  • The challenge follows growing opposition to Infantino over a failed plan to sell stakes in FIFA's commercial business to private investors.
