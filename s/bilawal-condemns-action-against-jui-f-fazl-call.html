Bilawal Condemns 'Political Victimisation' of JUI-F in Call With Fazlur Rehman

Pakistan Peoples Party chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari has condemned what he called political victimisation of the Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam-Fazl, telling its chief, Maulana Fazlur Rehman, in a telephone call that the government must tolerate opposition criticism and stop turning political differences into revenge.

Fazlur Rehman called Bilawal on Sunday, and the two leaders discussed the country's political situation and parliamentary affairs in detail, according to a statement from the PPP's media cell shared on X. Bilawal condemned what he described as government action against the JUI-F, along with the targeting of the party, and said political differences should not be turned into acts of revenge.

Bilawal said the government should accept criticism from the opposition with tolerance, arguing that a responsible opposition plays an important part in a democracy and in the parliamentary system. He said the government should avoid any step that could weaken democracy and parliament.

The call came as pressure on the JUI-F has grown. A terrorism case has been registered against Mufti Abrar Ahmed, who serves as secretary to Fazlur Rehman, and the property and assets of Fazlur Rehman, his son and his brothers are reportedly being examined by law-enforcement agencies over claims that wealth was acquired through unfair means.

The friction follows Fazlur Rehman's criticism of the Centre's decision to impose governor's rule and an emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, after which federal ministers attacked the JUI-F chief and the authorities began issuing notices to businesses and people linked to him over alleged rule violations. Bilawal has in recent days urged the government to stop fighting its opponents and called for a political reset built on unity, warning that if parties kept fighting one another, the people's suffering could not be eased. His party continues to support the ruling coalition without joining the federal government.

Key points

  • Fazlur Rehman called Bilawal on Sunday to discuss the political situation and parliamentary affairs.
  • Bilawal condemned alleged government action against the JUI-F and said political differences must not become revenge.
  • He said the government must tolerate opposition criticism and avoid steps that weaken democracy and parliament.
  • The call follows a terrorism case against Fazl's secretary and reported scrutiny of Fazl-linked businesses and family assets.
