South Africa Records 581 Women Killed in Three Months as Crime Figures Lay Bare Femicide Crisis

South Africa's police have reported that 581 women were killed across the country in just three months, new crime figures released on Friday show, in the latest grim measure of a femicide crisis the president has called a national disaster.

The figures cover July to September 2026 and were released in Pretoria by Acting Police Minister Firoz Cachalia. Of the 5,549 murder victims whose age and sex could be established, 4,819 were men, 581 were women and 149 were children. Gauteng, the province that includes Johannesburg, recorded the highest toll among women, with 131 killed. Overall murders fell slightly: 5,590 people were killed in the quarter, 204 fewer than the same months last year, about 60 a day. But Cachalia warned the numbers were still far too high, and that greater efforts were needed to prevent violence against women and children.

The women's toll includes 13 women whose bodies were found dumped in public places in and around Johannesburg, a discovery that set off nationwide outrage. On Thursday, Gauteng's police chief said eight people had now been arrested in connection with those deaths. Three of the arrests relate to Elizabeth Moselakgomo, a 38-year-old runner who went missing on a daily run in Kempton Park and whose body was found on 12 September. Two more people were detained over the death of Jabulile Ntimba, 33, found wrapped in a sheet beside a road in Boksburg on 17 September, the ninth body found in the area. Others were arrested over the deaths of Ntombifuthi Nxumalo, found at a cemetery in Tembisa on 26 September, and Busisiwe Mahlangu, whose body was found in Kempton Park on 15 July, the first of the 13.

The figures landed a day after another shocking killing: five women and a toddler were shot dead in Botshabelo township, Free State, on Wednesday night, when three masked men opened fire on a family home. The owner's wife was also shot and taken to hospital. No arrests have been made. Police also recorded 9,908 rapes in the three months, including 4,362 victims who were children under 18 and 5,116 adult women. Firearms were used in 2,552 murders, the most common weapon.

It is not clear whether the number of women killed rose or fell, because police had stopped publishing the breakdown after what they described as significant anomalies in the statistics. President Cyril Ramaphosa declared violence against women a national disaster last year and in September promised that no stone would be left unturned in finding those behind the Johannesburg killings. Even as overall murders edge down, more than six women a day were killed on average.

Key points

  • 581 women were killed in South Africa between July and September 2026, including 131 in Gauteng province.
  • Total murders fell to 5,590 in the quarter, 204 fewer than a year earlier, an average of about 60 killings a day.
  • Eight people have been arrested over the 13 women whose bodies were found dumped around Johannesburg.
  • Police also recorded 9,908 rapes in the quarter, including 4,362 victims under the age of 18.
