Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Postponed to Saturday in First Weather Delay in 100 Years

The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade will not march on Thanksgiving Day after all. Organisers postponed the New York City procession to Saturday because of a powerful storm, the first time in the parade's century-long history that weather has forced a delay.

Macy's announced the decision on Wednesday, saying the parade would now begin at 8:30 a.m. ET on Saturday, November 28, two days later than planned. The retailer said the safety of participants and spectators was the priority. The parade, first held in 1924, was suspended from 1942 to 1944 during the Second World War, but it had never before been postponed because of weather. The three-hour procession covers a 2.5-mile route from 77th Street and Central Park West to Herald Square, with giant character balloons, floats and marching bands.

Forecasters have warned New York City of damaging winds and coastal flooding for Thursday into Friday, with gusts expected to reach 60 mph and one to three inches of rain falling. The parade's giant character balloons, which are grounded under city rules when sustained winds pass 23 mph or gusts pass 34 mph, were already being inflated on Wednesday when the delay was announced, and crews had to deflate them again. They will be inflated once more on Saturday morning.

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani described the decision as tough but necessary given the forecast. NBC, which broadcasts the parade, said it would show a repeat of last year's edition on Thursday instead. In Philadelphia, organisers cancelled the Thanksgiving Day parade, billed as the oldest in the nation, after first announcing they would ground its balloons. Saturday is expected to bring sunny skies and light winds, a sharp contrast to the storm.

Key points

  • The 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed from Thursday, November 26, to Saturday, November 28, starting at 8:30 a.m. ET.
  • It is the first weather postponement in the parade's history; the event was suspended during the Second World War but never rescheduled for weather.
  • Forecasters warn of gusts up to 60 mph and coastal flooding in New York City on Thursday into Friday, with balloons grounded when winds pass city safety limits.
  • NBC will air a repeat of the 2025 parade on Thursday, and Philadelphia has cancelled its own Thanksgiving Day parade.
