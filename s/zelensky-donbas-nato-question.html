Trump Says Someone Else Should Negotiate for Kyiv as Zelenskyy Heads to Oslo Talks

President Donald Trump has said someone other than Volodymyr Zelenskyy should negotiate on Ukraine's behalf, as the Ukrainian president prepares for talks in Oslo on Tuesday with the US envoy Steve Witkoff and European leaders.

Mr Trump made the remark on Monday, outside the White House. "Zelenskyy is a man that should have had settled this war a long time ago," he said. "Maybe it's time for Zelenskyy to get somebody else to negotiate." Mr Zelenskyy, speaking to US senators at a meeting on Monday, said decisions taken by the Trump administration "are hard to explain between friends".

The Oslo talks on Tuesday will bring Mr Zelenskyy together with Mr Witkoff, Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Store and Finnish President Alexander Stubb. Jared Kushner is expected to join remotely because of illness. The discussions are also expected to include the E4 group of Britain, France, Germany and Poland, along with the European Union and NATO. Mr Store said Mr Zelenskyy had asked for both American envoys to take part.

Mr Kushner said on Saturday that the focus of American diplomacy had shifted to bringing Moscow and Europe into direct engagement, and to preparing the agenda for the next round of talks between the United States, Russia and Ukraine. He said there were new ideas that "may lead to breakthroughs". Mr Zelenskyy warned that the American plan "can be dangerous", saying wider talks about Europe's security could let President Vladimir Putin seek changes to NATO's military presence, including the withdrawal of American troops.

Mr Zelenskyy disclosed on Saturday that American negotiators had raised, for the first time, the idea of Ukraine leaving its eastern Donbas region in exchange for NATO membership, after talks in Abu Dhabi on 10 and 11 November. He said Kyiv will not give up its own land, but if Washington and other NATO allies confirmed such an offer, he would ask the Ukrainian people, and any deal would need a full security package, a strong funded army and reconstruction money.

A Ukrainian drone strike killed a family of three at their home in Russia's Belgorod region on Saturday, Russian officials said. Ukraine's air force said Russia sent 96 drones from Friday evening, and 81 were shot down or jammed. Russian strikes also killed at least two people in Ukraine, including a 17-year-old, and wounded 30 in Zaporizhzhia. Mr Zelenskyy ruled out any commitment to neutrality and said Russia had rejected a ceasefire on energy targets. There is no formal proposal yet, and the United States has not publicly confirmed Mr Zelenskyy's account.

Key points

  • Mr Trump said on Monday that someone else should negotiate for Kyiv, and Mr Zelenskyy told US senators the administration's decisions are "hard to explain between friends".
  • Mr Zelenskyy meets Mr Witkoff in Oslo on Tuesday with Mr Store, Mr Stubb, the E4 countries, the EU and NATO; Mr Kushner joins remotely because of illness.
  • Mr Kushner says the US is now brokering direct talks between Russia and Europe, with new ideas that may lead to breakthroughs; Mr Zelenskyy warned the plan "can be dangerous".
  • American negotiators raised a Donbas-for-NATO swap for the first time, Mr Zelenskyy said; Kyiv will not give up land, and would put any such offer to a referendum.
