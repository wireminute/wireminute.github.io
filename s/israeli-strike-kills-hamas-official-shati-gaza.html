Israeli Strike Kills Senior Hamas Security Official and His Family in Gaza City

An Israeli airstrike on an apartment in the Shati refugee camp in western Gaza City has killed a senior Hamas security official, his wife and their two daughters, officials and medics said.

The strike hit the family's apartment in the Beach refugee camp, also known as Shati, at dawn on Wednesday, Gaza's interior ministry said. The official was named as Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, 47, the director of the internal security service in northern Gaza. His wife, 41, and their daughters, aged 15 and 13, were also killed, and 12 other people were wounded, rescue officials said.

The Israeli military said it had targeted a Hamas militant, without giving further details. It was the second Israeli attack in 24 hours to kill a senior Hamas security official: Naim Abu Nahal, the head of the group's internal security apparatus across Gaza, was killed in a strike on Tuesday.

Key points

  • An Israeli airstrike at dawn on Wednesday hit an apartment in the Shati refugee camp in western Gaza City.
  • Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, who headed the Hamas internal security service in northern Gaza, was killed along with his wife and two daughters, aged 15 and 13; 12 others were wounded.
  • It was the second senior Hamas security official killed in 24 hours, after Naim Abu Nahal died in a strike on Tuesday.
