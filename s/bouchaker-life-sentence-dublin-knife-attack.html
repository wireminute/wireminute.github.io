Life Sentence for Man Who Stabbed Dublin Schoolchildren in Attack That Sparked Riots

A man who stabbed three young children outside their Dublin school in 2023 has been sentenced to life in prison, in a case the judge described as exceptional and deeply disturbing.

Riad Bouchaker, 52, was sentenced on Monday at Dublin's Central Criminal Court after a jury found him guilty in July of attempting to murder three children, two girls aged five and six and a five-year-old boy. The attack took place outside Gaelscoil Cholaiste Mhuire on Parnell Square East on 23 November 2023, shortly after half past one in the afternoon, as a group of 13 children aged five and six waited to be collected for after-school care. Bouchaker produced a 36cm kitchen knife and turned on the children. Childcare worker Leanne Flynn was helping a boy with his jacket when the attack began.

One five-year-old girl was stabbed in the heart and left with a lifelong disability, while Flynn suffered serious harm as she tried to protect the children. A French teenager who was passing by wrenched the knife from Bouchaker's hand. The judge imposed a life sentence for the attempted murder of the girl stabbed in the heart, with concurrent terms of 15 years for each of the other two attempted murders, 20 years for the serious harm caused to Flynn, seven years for assaults on two other children, and five years each for the assault on the teenager and for producing the knife. Bouchaker, an Algerian national and Irish citizen who had lived in Ireland for 25 years, had denied all eight charges against him.

The attack set off rioting across Dublin that evening, with buses and a tram set alight and shops looted, prompting the largest deployment of riot police in the history of the Irish state. The senior investigating officer, Garda Superintendent Colm Murphy, paid tribute to those who stepped in on the day, including Flynn and the teenager who took the knife.

The sentencing brings the criminal proceedings over the attack to a close, but the girl who was stabbed in the heart and her carer face consequences that will last the rest of their lives.

Key points

  • Riad Bouchaker, 52, was given a life sentence on Monday at Dublin's Central Criminal Court for the attempted murder of three children at Parnell Square in November 2023.
  • A five-year-old girl stabbed in the heart was left with a lifelong disability; childcare worker Leanne Flynn was seriously hurt shielding the children, and a passing teenager wrenched the knife away.
  • The judge also imposed concurrent terms of 15 years for each of the other two attempted murders and 20 years for the serious harm caused to Flynn; Bouchaker had denied all eight charges.
  • The attack sparked rioting across Dublin that evening, with buses and a tram set alight and shops looted, and drew the largest riot-police deployment in Irish state history.
