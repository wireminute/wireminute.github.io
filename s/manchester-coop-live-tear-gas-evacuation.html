Thousands Evacuated From Manchester Arena After Suspected Tear Gas Incident at Concert

More than 12,000 people have been evacuated from the Co-op Live arena in Manchester after a substance believed to be tear gas was let off in the crowd during a Don Toliver concert, with police declaring a major incident.

Greater Manchester Police said a substance believed to be tear gas was let off in the crowd towards the end of the US rapper Don Toliver's concert at the Co-op Live arena on Sunday night, and declared a major incident. Officers said more than 12,000 people were safely evacuated, and asked people to stay away while emergency services helped concertgoers reach their transport home.

The North West Ambulance Service said more than 50 people had been assessed by clinicians, with some reporting eye and breathing symptoms. One person was taken to Manchester Royal Infirmary for further assessment, and decontamination was carried out as a precaution. Police said no serious injuries had been reported.

No arrests have been announced, and police have not said who released the substance. Toliver is next scheduled to play in London on Monday. The story is still developing.
