Russian Drones Kill at Least 10 Across Ukraine as Kyiv Infrastructure Hit

Russian drone attacks killed at least 10 civilians across Ukraine on Saturday, officials said, and a daytime strike hit a critical infrastructure facility in Kyiv, where the mayor warned some districts could lose electricity and water.

Russian drone attacks killed at least 10 civilians across Ukraine on Saturday, officials said, and a daytime strike hit a critical infrastructure facility in Kyiv. A woman was killed and at least four people were wounded in the capital, local authorities said. Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said specialists were urgently assessing the damage and warned that some districts could face disruptions to electricity and water supplies.

Ukraine's air force said Russia launched 162 drones from Friday evening, and that 141 were shot down or suppressed by Saturday morning. In Bila Tserkva, south of Kyiv, three people were injured and an energy facility was damaged, while one person was injured when drones hit two apartment buildings in Kharkiv. Moscow said its forces struck a combined heat and power plant in Bila Tserkva and a substation in Kyiv. The toll included seven people killed at an enterprise in Nizhyn, in the Chernihiv region, that was hit on Friday, where rescue work ended on Saturday.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the overnight strikes had focused on energy infrastructure in the Zhytomyr region and that air defences had held, while the power situation remained difficult in Dnipropetrovsk and repairs continued in Odesa. Russia's Defence Ministry said its air defences shot down 1,246 Ukrainian drones overnight, including more than 460 aimed at the Moscow area, a claim far above the numbers Ukraine reports launching.

The attacks followed the overnight barrage from Thursday into Friday, when Russian forces struck railway and energy infrastructure in nine Ukrainian regions with 155 drones, killing one person and injuring six and leaving consumers in six regions without electricity. They came hours after Zelenskyy warned that Russia is preparing a major strike on Ukraine's energy infrastructure, saying Moscow is completing preparations for an assault on energy facilities, logistics and critical services as winter approaches.

Key points

  • Russian drone attacks killed at least 10 civilians across Ukraine on Saturday, including a woman in Kyiv.
  • A daytime drone strike hit a critical infrastructure facility in Kyiv; the mayor warned some districts could lose electricity and water.
  • Ukraine's air force says 162 drones were launched from Friday evening, with 141 shot down; three people were hurt in Bila Tserkva and seven were killed in Nizhyn.
  • Zelenskyy says strikes focused on Zhytomyr energy infrastructure and air defences held; Russia claims it downed 1,246 Ukrainian drones overnight.
