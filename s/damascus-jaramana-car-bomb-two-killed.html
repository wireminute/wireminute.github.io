Two Killed, Including a Girl, in Car Bomb Blast in Damascus Suburb of Jaramana

Two people, including a young girl, were killed and at least one wounded when a car bomb exploded in Jaramana, a suburb southeast of Damascus, on Saturday evening.

An explosive device planted inside a car detonated in Jaramana, a suburb southeast of Damascus, on Saturday evening, killing two people, including a young girl, and wounding at least one other, according to Syrian state television and the Interior Ministry.

State television said the blast went off in the al-Naseem neighbourhood, near a battery factory, and that two people were wounded. The Interior Ministry put the number of wounded at one and said the dead included a child. Security forces cordoned off the area and opened an investigation. No group has claimed responsibility, and officials have not named a motive.

Jaramana is home to large Druze and Christian communities and has seen sporadic violence since the fall of Bashar al-Assad's government in December 2024. In August, an explosive device inside a passenger bus in the same suburb wounded 14 people.
