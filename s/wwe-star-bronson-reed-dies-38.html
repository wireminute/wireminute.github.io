WWE Star Bronson Reed Dies at 38

World Wrestling Entertainment has announced the death of Jermaine Haley, the Australian wrestler known to fans as Bronson Reed, at the age of 38, saying he died on Friday due to medical complications.

World Wrestling Entertainment has announced the death of Jermaine Haley, the Australian wrestler known to fans as Bronson Reed, at the age of 38. The company said he died on Friday due to medical complications, in a statement that gave no further details about the cause or where he died.

"WWE is devastated to announce that Jermaine Haley, also known as Bronson Reed, passed away earlier today at the age of 38 due to medical complications," the company said in a statement posted on social media. It offered its condolences to his family, including his wife Paige, who is pregnant, and their young daughter.

Reed, born in Adelaide on 25 August 1988, signed with WWE in 2019 and became a one-time NXT North American Champion. He wrestled in two stints with the company, from 2019 to 2021 and again from 2022, and was a member of the Vision faction in his final run.

His death comes three weeks after he was seriously injured at the Crown Jewel event in Riyadh on 7 November, when he was thrown from the stage through tables during a run-in. Reports at the time said he had suffered broken ribs and a lacerated kidney, and he stayed in Saudi Arabia for treatment before returning home. WWE has not said whether the injury is connected to his death, and the company has disclosed no cause beyond medical complications.

Reed's final match was a six-man tag match in Brooklyn on 2 November, five days before the Crown Jewel appearance. Tributes poured in from across wrestling after the announcement, with WWE posting a memorial graphic reading "WWE remembers Jermaine Haley, Bronson Reed, 1988-2026".
