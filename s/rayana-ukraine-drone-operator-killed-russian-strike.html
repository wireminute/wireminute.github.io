Ukraine Bids Farewell to Rayana, the Mother and Drone Operator Killed on the Front Line

Ukraine has bid farewell to Rayana, a mother and former Muay Thai coach who became a drone operator with a volunteer battalion and was killed in a Russian air strike in the Donetsk region.

Rayana joined Ukraine's armed forces in 2024, two years into Russia's full-scale invasion, and trained as a drone pilot before joining the Sheikh Mansur Battalion, a volunteer unit of Chechen fighters serving alongside Ukrainian forces. She became the unit's only woman member, flying reconnaissance and strike drones over the Donetsk sector, where she tracked Russian armoured vehicles and helped direct attacks on them.

Before the war she taught Muay Thai to children and adults and was raising her young son. She said she took up arms to protect his future and to do what she could for Ukraine's victory, a decision her comrades say defined her service. She also took charge of the battalion's food supplies and, as a practising Muslim, looked after a mosque in the frontline town of Kostiantynivka, in the Donetsk region, while attending a mosque in Kyiv when she could.

The battalion announced on November 14 that Rayana and a fellow fighter, Khamza, had been killed fighting Russian forces. Ukrainian reports say she died when a Russian guided bomb struck a military position. The exact date of her death has not been made public. A farewell ceremony was held in Odesa, the city where her family lives and where she will be buried.

Tributes from fellow soldiers, Muslim community leaders and Ukraine's sports committee described her as a devoted mother, a disciplined athlete and a determined fighter. Her death has become one of the most widely shared stories of the war's human cost this week, a reminder that Ukraine's defence relies heavily on volunteers, including women who joined up after the invasion, and that the fighting in the Donetsk region continues to claim lives daily.
