William and Kate Meet Modi in New Delhi as Three-Day India Visit Ends

The Prince and Princess of Wales have met Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi, closing a three-day visit to India built around the Earthshot Prize environmental awards.

The meeting took place at the prime minister's residence on Wednesday, with William holding a bilateral meeting with Modi. Kensington Palace described it as a fitting conclusion to a very special few days in India. The meeting was a late addition to the royal schedule, arranged after Modi confirmed he would be back from the ASEAN summit in the Philippines in time to see the couple.

The couple arrived in Mumbai on Monday for the sixth Earthshot Prize awards, the environmental prize founded by William in 2020. Five winners were each given one million pounds to scale up solutions to climate change, air pollution and biodiversity loss. Tuesday night's ceremony in Mumbai was hosted by filmmaker Karan Johar, with Bollywood stars Alia Bhatt and Sonam Kapoor, actor Rami Malek and cricket great Sachin Tendulkar among the presenters.

The rest of the visit mixed diplomacy with culture. William and Kate played street cricket with Tendulkar, visited the offices of Dharma Productions, one of Bollywood's best-known film companies, and took part in engagements on sustainable fashion, crafts and maternal care. For the meeting in Delhi, Kate wore a suit by Indian designer Gaurav Gupta, continuing a run of Indian designers across the trip.

The visit was the couple's first joint overseas trip in four years and their first together in India since 2016, when they also met Modi. For Kate, it was her most prominent overseas engagement since she completed cancer treatment in 2024. Reports said the talks with Modi were expected to cover clean energy, trade and technology, as Britain and India deepen a partnership that has grown steadily despite the weight of their shared colonial history.

Key points

  • William and Kate met Modi at his residence in New Delhi on Wednesday, closing their three-day visit to India.
  • The meeting was a late addition to the schedule, arranged after Modi confirmed his return from the ASEAN summit in the Philippines.
  • The trip centred on the Earthshot Prize in Mumbai, where five winners each received one million pounds.
  • It was the couple's first joint visit to India since 2016 and Kate's biggest overseas engagement since her cancer treatment in 2024.
