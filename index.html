About WireMinute

WireMinute is an independent online newspaper that brings you breaking news and live updates from around the world, refreshed every minute. Our newsroom follows the biggest developing stories around the clock — world affairs, politics, business, technology, sport and health — and publishes them as short, clear reports you can read in under a minute.

Every story on WireMinute is written in our own words by our own desk. We check the confirmed facts, cut away the noise, and present each report with a headline, a short summary and the key points that matter, together with a real photograph of the subject. Stories open right here on WireMinute, so you can follow a developing story from the first report to the latest update without being sent away to another website.

Start with the latest headlines on the home page, or go straight to the section you care about: Latest, World, Politics, Business, Technology, Sports and Health. The page refreshes itself every minute, and the Live Updates rail shows the newest developments as they land.
