Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information WireMinute collects when you read our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Information we collect

WireMinute does not ask readers to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, address or payment details to read the news. The information collected when you visit is limited to:

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics to understand how many people read WireMinute and which stories they open. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, how long you spend on them, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), and your device and browser type.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider automatically records standard technical logs when your browser requests a page — your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs are used to operate and secure the website.
  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, so that we can reply.

Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your browser. The cookies used on WireMinute come from the Google services described on this page — analytics and advertising. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; the website will continue to work.

Advertising

WireMinute uses Google AdSense to display advertising, which helps keep our reporting free to read. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to WireMinute and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at www.google.com/settings/ads. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. Opting out does not remove ads; it means the ads you see may be less tailored to you.

How we use information

We use the information described above to run and improve WireMinute, to measure readership, to keep the website secure, to display advertising, and to respond to emails we receive. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Sharing

Analytics and advertising data is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policies, and server logs are held by our hosting provider. We do not otherwise share reader information with third parties, except where required by law.

Children's privacy

WireMinute is a general news service and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date above. Continuing to use WireMinute after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to the newsroom at avfgamer581@gmail.com, or through our Contact page.
